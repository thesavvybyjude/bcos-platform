# AI_Content_Future.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for the future of AI content.

## Core Philosophy
**AI content is coming like a tidal wave.**

- The more commoditized the content, the faster it'll be replaced
- The last to be replaced: Live streams, long-form
- 2020-2028: The free distribution arbitrage window
- After 2028: Pay-to-play likely

## Principles

### 1. The AI Content Wave
```
Easy to Replace → Hard to Replace
  │
  ├─ Twitter (AI text)
  ├─ Short-form video
  ├─ Long-form video (5-year window)
  └─ Live streams (last to be replaced)
```

### 2. The Dichotomy
- Everyone wants to use AI to go faster
- But you can't get caught using AI (soft cancellation)
- The new cancellation = Being caught using AI
- Everyone wants the speed, no one wants the label

### 3. The Free Distribution Window (2020-2028)
- 8-year window of free amplification to infinity
- If you didn't eat during this window, you're never getting it back
- You have about 3 years left to go ham
- You could build a $50M business in 3 years

### 4. After 2028: Pay-to-Play
- $10 per post to cut above AI content
- AI-generated content will flood the feeds
- Human-only verification platforms will emerge
- The era of free distribution is ending

## Decision Framework

```
What Should I Do About AI Content?
  │
  ├─ Am I playing the media game?
  │   ├─ YES → AI will replace you
  │   └─ NO → Focus on human content
  │
  ├─ Am I using AI for speed?
  │   ├─ YES → Don't get caught
  │   └─ NO → Fine
  │
  ├─ Am I building skills?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Start
  │
  └─ Am I going hard now?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → You're missing the window
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Go hard now (3-year window)
- Build human-only skills
- Focus on one-of-one content
- Expect pay-to-play by 2028

**Never:**
- Wait to start
- Think the window will stay open
- Rely on AI for originality
- Underestimate the tidal wave
