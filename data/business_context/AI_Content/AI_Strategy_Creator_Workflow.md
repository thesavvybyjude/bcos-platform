# AI Strategy & Creator Workflow

## Purpose
To provide a framework for how creators can leverage AI as a collaborator, the timeline and implications of AI in content creation, and the opportunities in the AI creative economy.

## Core Philosophy

> "The barriers to creation are truly going to disappear and the only thing that's going to remain is taste, creativity, understanding of culture and distribution."

> "If people really listen to this and take it to heart, they're going to get rich with AI."

## Principles

### 1. AI is a Collaborator, Not a Replacement
- Don't ask AI to write a script for you (it's garbage)
- Use AI as a creative brainstorming partner
- Piece-by-piece refinement with AI yields results

### 2. Quality of AI Output Depends on the Human
- The same AI tool produces wildly different results
- Creatives get the best results because they understand storytelling

### 3. The Infinite Content Machine is Coming
- 1000x content output on social platforms is guaranteed
- 2028: AI-generated content will be indistinguishable from human

### 4. Traditional Production is Cooked
- $1.5M Hyundai commercial can be made for $150 in AI credits
- AI filmmaking demand is exploding from Fortune 500

### 5. Speed is the Advantage
- RPN's unique talent: speed (can produce in 30 minutes what takes others hours)
- AI accelerates speed even further

### 6. Ideas Become the Whole Game
- Execution is becoming commoditized
- AI handles production; humans handle ideation
- "Ideas person" is now the most in-demand role

### 7. The AI Arbitrage Window
- 2028: content arbitrage winding down
- 2028-2038: building audience becomes nearly impossible
- Act now or get smoked

### 8. Distribution, POV, Storytelling Are the Moats
- AI can replicate production, not taste
- Culture, creativity, storytelling, distribution are irreplaceable

## AI Collaboration Framework

```
Creator Script/Message
    │
    ▼
Upload to AI (Claude, GPT, etc.)
    │
    ▼
Set Context:
    "You're my creative brainstorming partner.
    Let's talk about this line.
    Do you find it compelling?
    What are other ways we can word it?"

    (Back and forth conversation)
    │
    ▼
Refined Output
    (Human-led, AI-optimized)
```

## Rules

- **Always lead with human creativity** — AI supports, doesn't replace
- **Always provide context to AI** — Garbage in, garbage out
- **Never ask AI to write a script from scratch**
- **Never resist AI** — Adapt or get cooked
- **Always experiment with AI tools** — New tools appear every 3 months
- **Never rely on AI for your brand** — Your POV is the moat

## Heuristics

- If it's newscasty, AI will automate it
- If it requires POV, you're safe (for now)
- AI tools improve every 3 months
- By 2028, content arbitrage is over
- Creatives who adapt will make more than ever

## Failure Modes

1. **Resisting AI entirely** — Will become obsolete
2. **Letting AI write from scratch** — No unique perspective
3. **Not evolving fast enough** — Competitors will adapt faster
4. **Not understanding the arbitrage window** — Miss the window, miss the opportunity
5. **Believing AI kills creativity** — AI kills commoditized production, not creativity

    ├── Connect AI to Notion database

    └── Store SOPs in Notion

PHASE 3: AUTOMATION (AI-Driven)
    │
    ├── Set up scheduled tasks
    ├── AI executes tasks on schedule

PHASE 4: MAINTENANCE (AI-Driven)
    │
    └── Quarterly SOP review
    └── AI flags outdated processes
    └── Human updates as needed
```

## AI-Assisted System Building (Added by UI)

### The Shift

- Five years ago: building a custom system cost millions
- Today: use Replit, Lovable, or similar tools to build systems in hours
- The barrier to building custom software has collapsed

### How It Works

```
Step 1: FIND A SYSTEM YOU LIKE
│
├── Find a website or system you admire
├── Note the workflows, pages, links, and graphics
└── This is your reference

Step 2: GIVE IT TO REPLIT
│
├── Paste the URL and say: "Copy all workflows, pages, links, and graphics. Write the code to build this."
├── Replit generates the code
└── Review and iterate

Step 3: DEPLOY
│
├── Deploy the code on your own platform
├── Custom system, fraction of the cost
└── Iterate quickly based on feedback
```

### Agentic Interfaces

- The era of WIMP (Windows, Icons, Menus, Pointers) is over
- The new interface is agentic: "Hi phone, find me a speaker for my conference"
- The system goes into the web, finds the information, and returns it
- **Implication:** Businesses must build systems that work in an agentic world, not just websites

### Stop Features Silently (Heuristic)

- Test if features are actually valued by removing them silently
- If no one complains, it wasn't valuable
- The best features are the ones people miss when they're gone
- Test value by subtraction, not addition

```
TIER 1: ADMINISTRATIVE (Do First)
├── Invoicing, payment processing

## The Future: Systems as AI Programming

### The Vision
"The systems you document today will become the programming of the machines tomorrow."

### AI Agent Execution
- Feed your SOPs into an AI agent
- Tell the agent: "Execute this task" or "Do the first few steps and come back to me"
- Human-in-the-loop: review what the AI has done, provide feedback, and let it continue

### The Shift
- Previous: RPA required every variable to be explicitly programmed
- Now: AI has general intelligence — give it data and direction, and it can fill in the gaps

### Leverage AI for Systemization
- Record someone doing a task
- Transcribe and feed into ChatGPT
- Get a draft process created
- Save and store in your system
- **Result:** 10x faster than manual documentation

### The Warning
- New businesses are embracing these technologies from day dot
- If you're stuck with the legacy way, you won't be able to compete
- Systems documented today become the programming of AI agents tomorrow
