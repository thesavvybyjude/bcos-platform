# Supply_Chain_Quality.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for supplement sourcing quality.

## Core Philosophy
**Quality starts at the source. If you don't know where it comes from, you don't know what it is.**

- Most supplement companies don't visit their factories
- Jeff Byer is one of the first to ask
- Sourcing is not a commodity
- Every step of the supply chain matters

## Principles

### 1. The Sourcing Journey
```
Fish caught → Transported → Turned into oil → Brought to US → Encapsulated
  │
  └─ Quality happens at every step, not just the last one
```

### 2. Know the Source
- Know what port your omegas come in from (Norway)
- Know the farms your whey protein comes from
- Know the fish that were caught
- Know how it's transported

### 3. The Dirtiest Secret
- Sourcing doesn't matter to most brands
- In apparel, you'd visit factories
- In supplements, you don't
- This is a competitive advantage

### 4. Quality Costs Money
- Higher quality sourcing costs more
- But it leads to better outcomes
- Customers invest in their health
- They deserve the best

## Process

### Sourcing Quality Assurance:
```
1. Visit the source (factories, farms)
2. Understand the entire supply chain
3. Ask: "Is it clean working conditions?"
4. Ask: "What's the quality standard?"
5. Verify the highest quality
6. Certify (NSF Certified for Sport)
7. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
Is This Source High Quality?
  │
  ├─ Have I visited the source?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Need to visit
  │
  ├─ Do I know the entire supply chain?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Need to understand
  │
  ├─ Is it the highest quality available?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Find better source
  │
  └─ Is it certified (NSF)?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → Need certification
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Visit the source
- Understand the supply chain
- Verify quality
- Certify everything

**Never:**
- Assume sourcing is a commodity
- Skip visiting factories
- Ignore supply chain
- Trust without verification
