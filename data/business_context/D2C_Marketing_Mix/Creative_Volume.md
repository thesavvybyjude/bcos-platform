# Creative_Volume.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for creative volume and production.

## Core Philosophy
**More and different is what you need.**

- Meta needs radically different creative
- Launch hundreds of ads a week
- 50 ads a day to feed the beast
- Creative is the new targeting

## Principles

**1. Creative Volume Requirements**
- $100K+ daily spend = Hundreds of ads per week
- A banger ad taps out at $100K
- Need a banger every day
- 50 ads a day minimum

**2. Radically Different Creative**
- Meta's Andromeda AI knows if it's the same
- Blue background vs pink background = Same
- Front angle vs tree background = Same
- Need COMPLETELY different creative

**3. The Thumb Stop / Hook / Body Framework**
- Thumb stop (first image) is most important
- Hook is second most important
- Middle and end matter less
- But you need to test all of it

**4. You Need All Types**
- High production studio shoots
- Agency partners
- UGC creators
- Internal content
- All of it in the funnel

## Process

**Creative Production Workflow:**
```
1. Identify what's working (Northbeam data)
2. Creative strategists write scripts
3. Shoot across all formats (studio, agency, UGC)
4. Editors cut everything
5. Launch hundreds of ads
6. Meta figures out what works
7. Double down on winners
8. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Creative Should I Make?
  │
  ├─ High production? → Yes (some)
  ├─ UGC? → Yes (a lot)
  ├─ Internal? → Yes (yes!)
  ├─ Different formats? → Yes (need variety)
  └─ Radically different? → Yes (not just tweaks)
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Launch hundreds of ads per week
- Use radically different creative
- Test all formats
- Let Meta find the winners

**Never:**
- Just tweak existing creative
- Have a single creative source
- Stop producing
- Think you have "enough" creative
