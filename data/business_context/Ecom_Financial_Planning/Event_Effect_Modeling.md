# Event_Effect_Modeling.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for modeling the effect of marketing events.

## Core Philosophy
**Peaks create leverage against market prices.**

- Market price of ad inventory is dynamic
- Your conversion rate is what you control
- Peaks move your conversion rate while holding price constant
- The question: "Why does someone need to buy this today?"

## Principles

### 1. The Ad Auction Economics
```
Ad Inventory Price = Market determined
  │
  ├─ Goes up with increased demand
  ├─ Goes down independent of you
  ├─ Higher on weekends, lower on weekdays
  └─ You are a market taker
```

### 2. Your Arbitrage Opportunity
- Conversion rate is what you control
- Market price of ad inventory stays constant
- Create a reason for higher conversion
- That's what peaks do

### 3. The Peak Effect
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday: Everyone's conversion goes up
- You don't gain unique advantage
- Create peaks in April, June, September
- When no one else has a peak, you arbitrage the market

### 4. The Question
```
Why does someone need to buy this right now?
  │
  ├─ Not: Why do they need to buy it?
  ├─ Not: Is this a good product?
  └─ Why TODAY?
```

## Process

### Event Effect Modeling:
```
1. Absorb historical marketing calendar
2. Map every action you've ever taken
3. Ask: "What happened to revenue when we did that?"
4. Ask: "What happened to efficiency?"
5. Ask: "What happened to existing customer revenue?"
6. Use that to model future events
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Peak Should I Create?
  │
  ├─ What cultural moment fits my brand?
  │   ├─ International Women's Day
  │   ├─ Veterans Day
  │   └─ Create your own
  │
  ├─ What's the story?
  │   └─ Why does someone need to buy TODAY?
  │
  ├─ What's the SKU?
  │   └─ Limited edition, high AOV, high margin
  │
  └─ What's the halo effect?
      └─ New customer acquisition → Future peaks
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Create peaks when others don't
- Answer "Why today?"
- Manufacture moments
- Progressive peaking (build on success)

**Never:**
- Let the market set your conversion rate
- Rely only on Black Friday
- Forget to answer "Why today?"
- Ignore the halo effect
