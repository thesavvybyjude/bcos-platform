# FP_and_A.md

## Purpose
To provide a complete framework for financial planning and forecasting in e-commerce.

## Core Philosophy
**Great forecasting is an exercise in execution way more than it is in modeling.**

- Set the target, then every day make it true
- Finance and marketing must be integrated
- The marketing department should lead the numbers
- Forecasting without the marketing calendar is fiction

## Principles

### 1. Finance Meets Marketing Meets Product
- Most businesses silo these functions
- Finance builds forecasts independent of marketing calendar
- Marketing plans without financial modeling
- The combination of ops, finance, and marketing = Business

### 2. The Marketing Calendar Is the Forecast
- Every email, SMS, ad, influencer post drives revenue
- You can't forecast without knowing what actions you'll take
- Last January's product launch created revenue—if it's not happening this year, don't extrapolate

### 3. Units of Growth
- Individual actions that drive revenue reality
- Launch an ad → Send an email → Post on social → Get an influencer
- Map these to financial expectations
- No action = No revenue

### 4. The Two Core Forecasts
```
New Customer Revenue Model
  │
  ├─ Spend efficiency curve (more spend = less efficiency)
  ├─ Seasonal adjustments
  ├─ AOV considerations
  └─ New customer revenue expectation

Existing Customer Revenue Model
  │
  ├─ Cohort-specific LTV analysis
  ├─ Every cohort's revenue over time
  └─ Forecast future existing customer revenue
```

## Process

### Financial Forecasting Process:
```
1. Build marketing calendar (all actions planned)
2. Model new customer revenue (spend vs efficiency curve)
3. Model existing customer revenue (cohort LTV)
4. Align expectations
5. Track daily performance
6. Course-correct early (day 5, not day 22)
7. Adjust calendar as needed
```

## Decision Framework

```
Who Should Own the Forecast?
  │
  ├─ Marketing knows what actions will be taken
  │   ├─ Marketing should lead the forecast
  │   └─ Finance should validate and model
  │
  ├─ Finance alone = Historical extrapolation
  │   └─ Without marketing calendar, it's fiction
  │
  └─ Marketing alone = Optimism without rigor
      └─ Without financial modeling, it's hope
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build forecast from marketing calendar
- Track daily performance
- Course-correct early
- Align marketing and finance

**Never:**
- Forecast without the marketing calendar
- Extrapolate history without context
- Silos finance from marketing
- Wait until day 22 to course-correct
