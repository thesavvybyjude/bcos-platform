# Generalist_to_Spike.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for team evolution (revisited with ecom focus).

## Core Philosophy
**First hire generalists, then hire spikes.**

- 0-10M: Generalists do everything
- 10-50M: Spikes (specialists) in key areas
- The best founders hire for pace
- Generalists evolve into spikes

## Principles

### 1. The Generalist Phase
- One person does everything
- Social media, paid, content
- Willing to do anything
- Pace > Perfect

### 2. The Spike Phase
- Best creative director
- Best paid media team
- Best content team
- One major skill set, best in the world

### 3. The Profit Engineer Is the Ultimate Generalist
- Can do it all
- Powered by AI
- Accountable for outcome
- The future of the role

### 4. "Good" vs "Bad" Role Documents
- Tell people what it looks like to be good
- Tell people what it looks like to be bad
- Aspirational identity + boundaries
- A16Z/Ben Horowitz approach

## Process

### Team Evolution:
```
1. Start with generalists
2. Document good vs bad for each role
3. As you grow, add spikes
4. Generalists evolve to spikes
5. Profit engineers replace siloed teams
```

## Decision Framework

```
Generalist vs. Spike?
  │
  ├─ <10M → Generalists
  │
  ├─ 10-50M → Spikes in key areas
  │
  ├─ >50M → Profit engineers
  │
  └─ Always → Document good vs bad
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Start with generalists
- Document good vs bad
- Evolve to spikes
- Consider profit engineers

**Never:**
- Hire spikes too early
- Skip documentation
- Keep generalists too long
- Ignore AI productivity
