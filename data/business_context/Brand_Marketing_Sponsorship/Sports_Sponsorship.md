# Sports_Sponsorship.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for sports sponsorship strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**Sports sponsorships are brand positioning engines.**

- Sports create emotional connection
- Premium events signal quality
- Content from events is invaluable
- Authenticity matters

## Principles

**1. Events Should Be Revenue-Generating**
- Pop-up stores at events
- Tennis tournaments: $500K in 9 days
- Even if break-even, brand value is worth it
- Actually selling at events = Authentic

**2. Authentic > Paid Sponsorship**
- People seeing Oakberry at events
- Not just a logo - actual customers eating product
- Lines of people holding cups
- Real demand is visible

**3. Tennis Is a Global Premium Platform**
- Indian Wells, Miami Open, US Open, Roland Garros
- Premium audience
- Global reach
- Fits healthy brand positioning

## Process

**Sports Event Strategy:**
```
1. Identify premium events (F1, Tennis Grand Slams)
2. Set up actual locations at events
3. Generate real sales ($500K in 9 days)
4. Capture content (crowds, lines, product)
5. Deploy content across channels
6. Build brand association
7. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
Which Sports Events?
  |
  |- Is the event premium?
  |   |- YES → Good fit
  |   |- NO → Lower value
  |
  |- Is it global?
  |   |- YES → Good fit
  |   |- NO → Regional only
  |
  |- Can I sell at the event?
  |   |- YES → Strong ROI
  |   |- NO → Less valuable
  |
  |- Can I capture content?
      |- YES → Proceed
      |- NO → Less value
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Be where your customers are
- Generate real sales at events
- Capture high-quality content
- Build premium brand association

**Never:**
- Just put logo without activation
- Miss content capture opportunities
- Underestimate event power
- Be inauthentic
