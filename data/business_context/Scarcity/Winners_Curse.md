# Winners Curse

## Purpose
To provide a framework for avoiding the winner's curse in business.

## Core Philosophy
**Don't overpay for success. The winner's curse is real.**

- The person who wins the bidding war often overpays
- The thrill of winning can cost you the battle
- Know your BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement)
- Often the best move is to walk away

## Principles

### 1. The Winner's Curse
- When you win a bidding war, you've likely overpaid
- The other side knows something you don't
- The thrill of winning clouds judgment
- Your pride is costly

### 2. Know Your BATNA
- Before entering any negotiation, know your fallback
- If you don't have a good alternative, you have no leverage
- The best time to negotiate is when you can walk away
- Real power is not needing the deal

### 3. Walking Away Is Winning
- The best deal is sometimes the one you don't make
- Overpaying destroys value
- Patience beats desperation
- There's always another opportunity

## Process

### Avoiding the Winner's Curse:
```
1. Determine your BATNA before negotiating
2. Set a walk-away price
3. Don't get emotionally invested in winning
4. If the price exceeds your walk-away, leave
5. There will be other opportunities
6. Better to walk away than to overpay
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Make This Deal?
  │
  ├─ What's my BATNA (Best Alternative)?
  │   ├─ Strong → I have leverage
  │   └─ Weak → I'm at a disadvantage
  │
  ├─ What's my walk-away price?
  │   ├─ Set it in advance
  │   └─ Don't exceed it
  │
  ├─ Am I emotionally invested in winning?
  │   ├─ YES → Dangerous
  │   └─ NO → Good
  │
  └─ Is this a good deal, or just a win?
      ├─ Good deal → Take it
      └─ Just a win → Walk away
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Know your BATNA
- Set a walk-away price
- Be willing to walk away
- Don't overpay for a win

### Never:
- Get emotionally attached to winning
- Exceed your walk-away price
- Negotiate without a BATNA
- Think you can't walk away
