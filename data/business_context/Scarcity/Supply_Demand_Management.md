# Supply_Demand_Management.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for managing supply and demand in exclusive businesses.

## Core Philosophy
**If you build it right, they will come.**

- Quality attracts demand
- Scarcity creates urgency
- Experience drives repeat business
- Premium positioning commands premium prices

## Principles

**1. Quality Over Quantity**
- Every seat must be premium
- Every experience must be memorable
- Every interaction must be intentional
- Excellence is non-negotiable

**2. Price Per Person Matters More Than Volume**
```
Revenue = Volume × Price
  |
  |- High Volume × Low Price = High operational cost
  |- Low Volume × High Price = High margin per customer
```

**3. When You're Scarce, You Can Be More Expensive**
- Premium pricing is a signal
- Price reflects value
- People pay for exclusivity
- Higher price = Higher perceived value

## Decision Framework

```
Supply/Demand Decision Tree:
  |
  |- Are we consistently full?
  |   |- YES → We're too cheap or too available
  |   |- NO → We need to improve quality
  |
  |- Are we turning people away?
  |   |- YES → Good sign
  |   |- NO → Need more exclusivity
  |
  |- Can we raise prices?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Improve product first
  |
  |- Is demand exceeding supply?
      |- YES → Maintain or tighten
      |- NO → Improve marketing/quality
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Monitor supply/demand balance
- Turn people away regularly
- Keep quality higher than demand
- Raise prices when demand exceeds supply

**Never:**
- Over-expand
- Lower prices
- Compromise quality
- Make it easy to get in
