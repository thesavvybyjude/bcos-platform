# Restaurant_Operations.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for restaurant operations.

## Core Philosophy
**Food is the foundation; service is the differentiator.**

- Kitchen is the hardest part
- Service makes or breaks
- Margins are thin
- Details matter

## Principles

**1. Lead with Food**
- Food quality is non-negotiable
- Consistency is everything
- Menu development takes time
- Chefs are critical hires

**2. Kitchen Is the Hardest Part**
- Food service is difficult
- Getting kinks out takes months
- Respect the line cooks
- Food operations are real work

**3. Alcohol Drives Profits**
- Food margins are thin
- Alcohol margins are higher
- Beverage program matters
- Events drive revenue

## Margin Profile

| Revenue Stream | Margin |
|----------------|--------|
| Food | 10-20% |
| Alcohol | 70-80% |
| Events | 50-60% |
| Private Dining | 40-50% |

## Rules

**Always:**
- Prioritize food quality
- Respect kitchen staff
- Monitor margins
- Build beverage program

**Never:**
- Cut food quality corners
- Ignore service issues
- Underpay kitchen staff
- Neglect beverage program
