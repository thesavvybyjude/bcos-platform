# Supper_Club_Model.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for the supper club model.

## Core Philosophy
**The supper club is the ultimate hybrid model.**

- Best of restaurant and nightclub
- Longevity + margins
- Food + vibe
- Restaurant first, nightlife second

## Principles

**1. Lead with Restaurant, Add Nightlife**
- Be a restaurant first
- Food quality is non-negotiable
- Let the nightlife emerge naturally
- Don't back-end into a club

**2. Create Curated Experiences**
- Sunday jazz
- Live music
- Themed nights
- Event programming

**3. Balance the Energy**
- Not every night is a party
- Curated variety
- Regular schedule
- Heart and soul

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Create a Supper Club?
  |
  |- Can I run a restaurant?
  |   |- YES → Continue
  |   |- NO → Start with restaurant
  |
  |- Can I add entertainment?
  |   |- YES → Consider
  |   |- NO → Focus on restaurant
  |
  |- Do I understand the hybrid model?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Study first
  |
  |- Can I maintain both?
      |- YES → Proceed
      |- NO → Choose one model
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Lead with food
- Curate experiences
- Balance energy
- Maintain quality

**Never:**
- Become a club disguised as restaurant
- Sacrifice food quality
- Let every night be a party
- Lose the restaurant identity
