# Venue_Launch.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for launching hospitality venues successfully.

## Core Philosophy
**Opening day is when the real work begins.**

- The launch is just the start
- First 90 days are critical
- First year determines trajectory
- Never rest on your laurels

## Principles

**1. The First 90 Days Are Observation Period**
- Watch everything
- Listen to feedback
- Note what works and doesn't
- Identify improvements

**2. Don't Judge Good or Bad for a Full Year**
- Too early to tell
- Adjustments needed
- Market conditions change
- Patience is essential

**3. Focus on What's Wrong, Not What's Right**
- Don't accept smoke
- Seek criticism
- Identify problems
- Fix them early

## Process

**Venue Launch Timeline:**
```
Pre-Launch (6-12 months):
  - Design and build
  - Team hiring
  - Supplier relationships
  - Pre-opening marketing (word of mouth)

Launch Day:
  - Execute opening
  - Observe everything
  - Document issues

Days 1-90:
  - Intensive observation
  - Adjust seating, lighting, flow
  - Fix food and service issues
  - Listen to feedback

Months 3-12:
  - Continue adjustments
  - Monitor trends
  - Build reputation
  - Don't judge success yet

Month 12+:
  - Review performance
  - Assess long-term viability
  - Plan improvements
  - Consider expansion
```

## Areas to Observe in First 90 Days:
1. Seating - What tables do people want?
2. Lighting - Does it flatter everyone?
3. Food - What plates come back empty?
4. Service - What gets complaints?
5. Flow - Do people move naturally?
6. Vibe - Is the energy right?
7. Music - Does it match the room?

## Rules

**Always:**
- Watch more than you act
- Seek negative feedback
- Make continuous improvements
- Wait one year to judge success

**Never:**
- Rest on early success
- Ignore problems
- Get too high or too low
- Stop improving
