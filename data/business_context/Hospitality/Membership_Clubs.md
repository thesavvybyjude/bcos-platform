# Membership_Clubs.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for membership club operations.

## Core Philosophy
**A membership club is an annuity, not a venue.**

- Members pay for access
- Recurring revenue model
- Experience is the product
- Community is the value

## Principles

**1. Members Are the Product**
- The community makes the club
- Curate the right people
- Diverse industries
- Quality over quantity

**2. Economics Are Different**
- Revenue = Membership dues + Events
- F&B can run at a loss
- Venue is a home, not a restaurant
- Long-term model

**3. It Takes Time to Become Profitable**
- 1-2 years to breakeven
- Patience required
- Don't chase quick profits
- Build community first

## Economics

**Membership Club Revenue Model:**
```
Total Revenue
  |
  |- Membership Dues: 60-80%
  |- Events: 15-25%
  |- F&B: 5-10% (can be negative)
```

**Membership Economics:**
- Initiation fee: $10,000-50,000
- Annual dues: $5,000-20,000
- 200-500 members optimal
- Focus on retention, not acquisition

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Open a Membership Club?
  |
  |- Do I have the right network?
  |   |- YES → Continue
  |   |- NO → Build network first
  |
  |- Can I sustain 1-2 years of losses?
  |   |- YES → Consider
  |   |- NO → Start with restaurant
  |
  |- Can I curate the right members?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Don't do it
  |
  |- Is this about community or profit?
      |- Community → Proceed
      |- Profit → Reconsider
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Curate members carefully
- Protect the experience
- Focus on community
- Be patient

**Never:**
- Let in wrong people for money
- Chase quick profits
- Compromise exclusivity
- Rush to profitability
