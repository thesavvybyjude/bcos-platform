# EOS_Rhythm.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for EOS/Traction operating rhythm.

## Core Philosophy
**EOS is the heartbeat of your company.**

- Weekly Level 10 meetings
- 90-day goals
- Company-wide alignment
- Tight feedback loops

## Principles

### 1. The Level 10 Meeting
```
1. Wins (5 min)
2. Dashboard (5 min)
3. Opportunities/Challenges (15 min)
4. Top 5 Opportunities (30 min)
5. To-Do List (15 min)
6. Review Last Week's To-Dos (10 min)
7. Cascade (10 min)
```

### 2. 90-Day Goals
- Company-level goals
- Department-level goals
- Individual-level goals
- Incentivize performance

### 3. Tight Feedback Loops
- Daily: End of day reports
- Weekly: Level 10 meetings
- Monthly: Personal board meeting
- Quarterly: Big picture review

## Process

### EOS Implementation:
```
1. Train leadership on EOS
2. Start weekly Level 10 meetings
3. Set 90-day company goals
4. Cascade to departments
5. Cascade to individuals
6. Track weekly
7. Review quarterly
```

## Decision Framework

```
What's the Right Meeting Cadence?
  │
  ├─ Leadership → Weekly Level 10
  ├─ Department → Weekly Level 10 (cascaded)
  ├─ Sales → Daily end-of-day reports
  ├─ Content → Weekly content meetings
  └─ Company → Monthly all-hands
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Never miss a meeting
- Start with wins
- Track dashboard metrics
- End with clear to-dos

**Never:**
- Skip Level 10 meetings
- Forget the dashboard
- End without clear ownership
- Let feedback loops stretch
