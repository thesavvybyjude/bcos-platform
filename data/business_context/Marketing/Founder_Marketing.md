# Founder_Marketing.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for founders to market through personal brand and authenticity.

## Core Philosophy
**You are your company's best marketing channel.**

- You know your product best
- You are authentic
- People buy from people
- Founder marketing builds trust

## Principles

**1. Share Your Journey**
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Real numbers
- Authentic failures
- Genuine wins

**2. Create Content About Your Business**
- Product decisions
- Team building
- Customer insights
- Strategy thinking

**3. Be Consistently Present**
- Daily social posts
- Weekly newsletter
- Regular engagement
- Build habit

**4. You Are Your Own Best Media Channel**
- Inbound from quality followers > Ad spend
- Deal flow increases dramatically
- Investors find you
- Partners find you

**5. Shout From the Rooftops**
- If you don't tell people, no one knows
- The tree falling in the forest metaphor
- Don't be humble about success
- Build in public always

**6. Build Your Own Audience**
- Don't rely on someone else's platform
- Own your followers
- Your audience is your asset
- Control your distribution

## Core Philosophy Addendum
**Founder content is the cheapest acquisition channel.**

## Decision Tree

```
How to Approach Founder Marketing:
  |
  |- Am I comfortable being public?
  |   |- YES → Go all in
  |   |- NO → Start with small steps
  |
  |- Do I have unique insights?
  |   |- YES → Share them
  |   |- NO → Develop expertise first
  |
  |- What's my unique POV?
  |   |- Technical expertise
  |   |- Industry experience
  |   |- Personal story
  |
  |- What format suits me?
      |- Writing
      |- Video
      |- Audio
      |- Combination
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Be authentic
- Share learnings
- Engage your audience
- Create value first
- Build personal brand
- Shout your wins
- Attract inbound
- Own your audience

**Never:**
- Be fake
- Over-promote
- Ignore feedback
- Stop sharing
- Be quiet about success
- Rely only on company channels
- Underestimate founder content
- Forget to build in public
