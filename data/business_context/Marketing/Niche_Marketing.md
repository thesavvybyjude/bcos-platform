# Niche Marketing

## Purpose
To provide a framework for niche marketing.

## Core Philosophy
**The riches are in the niches.**

- Broad markets are competitive; niches are loyal
- When you target everyone, you reach no one
- A passionate niche will buy everything you make
- Go narrow to go deep

## Principles

### 1. The Power of Niches
- Niches are less competitive
- Niches are more loyal
- Niches pay more
- Niches are easier to reach

### 2. The Passion Economy
- People pay for what they are passionate about
- The more passionate the audience, the higher the price
- Find a group that is underserved and serve them
- Build a brand that feels like a community

### 3. Escaping the Average
- If you are average, you are invisible
- The goal is to be remarkable to someone, not acceptable to everyone
- Average is not a strategy
- Niche is the opposite of average

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Go Niche?
  |
  +-- Is the niche passionate?
  |   +-- YES -> Go for it
  |   +-- NO -> Keep looking
  |
  +-- Is the niche underserved?
  |   +-- YES -> Opportunity
  |   +-- NO -> Competition
  |
  +-- Can I reach them?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need to find the channel
  |
  +-- Is the niche profitable?
      +-- YES -> Go deep
      +-- NO -> Consider different niche
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Go niche
- Be specific
- Serve the passionate
- Build community

### Never:
- Try to be everything to everyone
- Be average
- Ignore the power of niche
- Think broad is better
