# Product_Led_Growth.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for organic growth through product features.

## Core Philosophy
**Your product is your best marketing channel.**

- Every user is a marketer
- Features drive adoption
- Network effects are powerful
- Product quality = Growth

## Principles

**1. "Powered By" Strategy**
- Public attribution
- Drives referrals
- Creates awareness
- Low cost, high return

**2. Network Effects**
- Recommendation networks
- Cross-promotion features
- Ecosystem growth
- Compounding benefits

**3. Referral Engine**
- Built-in sharing
- Incentivized growth
- Viral loops
- Organic flywheel

## Example

**Beehiiv "Powered By" Flywheel:**
```
User sends newsletter
  |
  |- 100,000 recipients see "Powered by Beehiiv"
  |
  |- Recipients notice quality
  |
  |- 0.1-0.5% become users
  |
  |- Loop repeats
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Make product shareable
- Include attribution
- Incentivize sharing
- Build network effects

**Never:**
- Hide your technology
- Remove attribution (early)
- Ignore viral features
- Make sharing hard
