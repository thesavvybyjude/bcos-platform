# TikTok & Creator Marketing Playbook

## Purpose
To provide a comprehensive framework for building a creator community on TikTok, managing affiliates, and leveraging the platform for brand growth.

## Core Philosophy

> "TikTok has democratized the way people can make money by spending as little or as much as you want as long as you make good content."

> "If you don't know how a platform works or if you don't know how creators work in general, you're not going to be able to understand how to direct them."

## Principles

### 1. Consistency is the Secret
- Post every single day
- You learn hooks, what works, what doesn't through volume
- "We built everything in-house ourselves"

### 2. Creator Education is Everything
- Make every creator sit through a 3-hour orientation
- They need to understand your product deeply to sell it
- If they don't understand the brand, they can't add to it

### 3. Culture is the Differentiator
- A creator is only so driven by money
- Create a gaming system, a community, a culture
- When creators are friends and iterating together, the system works on its own

### 4. The Tier System
- Create hierarchies (Tier 1, 2, 3)
- People earn their way up through GMV
- Make top performers feel special (billboards, trips, prizes)

### 5. Treat Creators Like Partners
- Put them on billboards in Times Square
- Send them to Coachella
- Take them on trips
- They'll be loyal forever

### 6. The Flywheel
- Credibility leads to Creators leads to Sales
- Leverage every win to create the next win
- Joe Rogan mentions leads to PR leads to Creator content leads to More sales

### 7. Top of Funnel is the Game
- TikTok is both top and bottom of funnel
- Pushing top of funnel elevates every other channel
- 13% awareness across the US because of TikTok

### 8. Data Tells You Where to Go
- Use consumer insights to identify over-indexed groups
- Partnerships should feed into TikTok
- "All roads lead to repurposing it and throwing it to TikTok"

## Decision Frameworks

### Creator Onboarding & Management Framework

```
STEP 1: RECRUITMENT
├── Source from other TAPs (TikTok Affiliate Partners)
├── Use referrals from existing creators
└── Cold outreach rarely works (too many requests)

STEP 2: ONBOARDING (3-Hour Orientation)
├── Video orientation + Notion document
├── Deep product understanding
├── Brand culture and values
└── We know if they went through it

STEP 3: TESTING and ITERATION
├── Excel sheet of hooks
├── AB test everything
├── Creators add their own twist
└── Community shares what's working

STEP 4: TIER SYSTEM
├── Tier 3: Entry level (GMV goals)
├── Tier 2: Mid level (higher commissions)
└── Tier 1: Top performers (special treatment)

STEP 5: CULTURE and LOYALTY
├── Discord community (creators talk to each other)
├── CEO calls (quarterly)
├── Special treatment (billboards, trips, prizes)
└── They become brand evangelists

RESULT: "The system works on its own after a certain point"
```

### TikTok Content Strategy Framework

```
HOOKS DATABASE:
├── Excel sheet of proven hooks
├── Media appearances (Shark Tank, Rogan)
├── Customer reviews and testimonials
└── Underdog stories (work best)

TESTING METHODOLOGY:
├── Same hook, different creator
├── Different hook, same creator
├── Measure: Views, engagement, conversions
└── Winner gets amplified

CONTENT THAT ALWAYS WORKS:
├── Shark Tank clips (framed as "biggest mistake")
├── Joe Rogan clips (framed as endorsement)
├── Underdog stories (founder background)
└── "Kevin O'Leary called our deal stinky poo poo"

THE FRAME:
└── Take the clip + spin it into a compelling story
└── Don't just post the clip; narrate it
```

## Rules

- **Always make creators sit through orientation** — If they don't understand the product, they cannot sell it
- **Always have a tier system** — People want to feel special and earn their way up
- **Always treat top creators like royalty** — Times Square billboards, trips, prizes
- **Always have a hooks database** — Don't reinvent the wheel every time
- **Always frame clips as stories** — Don't just post; narrate and contextualize
- **Always post every single day** — Consistency is the secret
- **Never cold outreach creators** — They're getting hundreds of requests; go through TAPs or referrals

## Heuristics

- If a creator doesn't go through orientation, they won't perform
- The top 1% of creators drive 90% of sales
- Joe Rogan mentions didn't move the needle until we learned how to capitalize on them
- PR is a lever; you have to pull it intentionally, not wait for it

## Triggers

### When a Creator Joins:

```
New creator on board

→ Send 3-hour orientation video
→ Send Notion document with brand bible
→ Add to Discord
→ Track if they completed it
→ Start them in Tier 3 with GMV goals

If they don't complete orientation: They're not serious
If they don't perform: They get kicked out
```

### When a PR Moment Happens:

```
Joe Rogan mentions Neurogum again

→ Don't just celebrate
→ Get clips of the mention
→ Spin it into a compelling story
→ Send to creators as a hook
→ Creators make content around it

PR is only valuable if you capitalize on it.
```

### When Scaling Creators:

```
Creator community growing

→ Tier system becomes essential
→ Top performers need to feel special
→ Not everyone gets the same treatment
→ "We only take 10 of them to Florida"
→ "Only 3 got to be on billboards in Miami"

Exclusivity drives performance.
```
