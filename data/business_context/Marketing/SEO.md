# SEO.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for organic search growth through content.

## Core Philosophy
**SEO is the compounding interest of content marketing.**

- Create content that answers questions
- Build authority over time
- Compounding returns
- Long-term asset

## Principles

**1. Create Content That Answers Questions**
- What does your audience Google?
- Write comprehensive answers
- Update regularly
- Build topical authority

**2. Consistency Over Time**
- Daily content creation
- 2+ articles per day
- 4-year minimum investment
- Compounding results

**3. Use AI/GEO for Modern Search**
- Optimize for ChatGPT
- Optimize for Claude
- Answer AI questions
- Build for AI consumption

## Process

**SEO Content Engine:**
```
1. Research search demand
2. Create high-quality content
3. Publish consistently (2x/day)
4. Link internally
5. Monitor performance
6. Update and improve
7. Optimize for AI
```

## Decision Tree

```
Should I Invest in SEO?
  |
  |- Do I have content budget?
  |   |- YES → Consider SEO
  |   |- NO → Focus short-term channels
  |
  |- Am I patient (12-24 months)?
  |   |- YES → SEO is for you
  |   |- NO → Use paid acquisition
  |
  |- Is my market competitive?
  |   |- LOW → SEO works great
  |   |- HIGH → Need significant investment
  |
  |- Do I have unique expertise?
      |- YES → Leverage it
      |- NO → Build authority first
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Answer real questions
- Build authority
- Keep content fresh
- Monitor performance

**Never:**
- Expect overnight results
- Underinvest
- Stop creating
- Ignore AI optimization
