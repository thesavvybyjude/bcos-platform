# Brand Positioning

## Purpose
To provide a framework for brand positioning.

## Core Philosophy
**Positioning is how you stand out in a crowded market.**

- If you are not positioned, you are just another choice
- Position before you market
- The narrower your positioning, the wider your impact
- Be the only one, not just one of many

## Principles

### 1. The Art of Positioning
- Positioning is defining your unique space in the market
- If you do not position yourself, someone else will
- The right positioning makes marketing effortless
- The wrong positioning makes marketing impossible

### 2. Narrow Is Wider
- If you try to please everyone, you please no one
- The narrower your audience, the more deeply you connect
- A passionate niche beats a broad market
- Depth beats breadth

### 3. The 3 C's of Positioning
```
Category: What are you in?
  +-- Define the category

Competition: What are they doing?
  +-- Be different

Customer: Who are you for?
  +-- Be specific
```

## Decision Framework

```
Is My Positioning Clear?
  |
  +-- Can someone explain what you do in one sentence?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need clarity
  |
  +-- Are you the only one who does what you do?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need differentiation
  |
  +-- Is your audience specific?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need to narrow
  |
  +-- Is your positioning defensible?
      +-- YES -> Good
      +-- NO -> Need more differentiation
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Position before you market
- Be narrow to be wide
- Be the only one
- Protect your position

### Never:
- Try to be everything to everyone
- Let others define your position
- Be vague
- Forget that positioning equals pre-selling
