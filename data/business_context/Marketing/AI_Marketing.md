# AI_Marketing.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for AI-powered marketing.

## Core Philosophy
**AI is a commodity. Creativity is the differentiator.**

- AI makes IQ a commodity
- Creativity is the new advantage
- Early adoption creates outsized results
- Transparency is key with AI content

## Principles

### 1. AI Is a Commodity
- Everyone will have access
- Knowledge is no longer scarce
- Creativity is the differentiator
- How you feed the AI engine matters

### 2. Transparency Is Critical
- Always disclose AI usage
- Creates trust with audience
- Generates positive engagement
- Sets you apart from deceptive brands

### 3. Speed of Adoption Matters
- Early adopters get outsized results
- The ad that cost $25K got 200M views
- Timing matters as much as quality
- Move fast

### 4. Use AI for Content
- Ideas, outlines, drafts
- Research and editing
- Personalization at scale
- Schedule creation

### 5. Use AI for Lead Generation
- Find leads (Manis)
- Write messages
- Personalize at scale
- Send emails

### 6. Use AI for Retention
- Analyze customer signals
- Predict churn
- Write retention emails
- Schedule webinars

### 7. Creative Strategy: Story > Iteration
- Iteration optimizes for local maxima
- Break the model with novel stories
- Create for your audience's audience
- The algorithm rewards novelty

## Process

### AI Marketing Workflow:
```
1. Identify AI opportunity (new tool/platform)
2. Act quickly (3-4 weeks from idea to launch)
3. Create high-quality AI content
4. Be transparent about AI usage
5. Leverage the conversation
6. Capitalize on timing
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Use AI for Marketing?
  │
  ├─ Is it high quality?
  │   ├─ YES → Use it
  │   └─ NO → Wait
  │
  ├─ Can I be transparent?
  │   ├─ YES → Use it
  │   └─ NO → Don't use it
  │
  ├─ Is the timing right?
  │   ├─ YES → Move fast
  │   └─ NO → Wait
  │
  └─ Will it create conversation?
      ├─ YES → Great
      └─ NO → Less value
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Be transparent about AI usage
- Move fast on new tools
- Prioritize creativity
- Leverage AI as a commodity
- Use AI for content
- Use AI for lead generation
- Use AI for retention
- Let AI do the work
- Break the model with novel stories
- Create for your audience's audience

**Never:**
- Deceive about AI usage
- Move too slowly
- Underestimate timing
- Ignore creative input
- Write from scratch
- Manually research leads
- Ignore customer signals
- Write retention emails by hand
- Optimize for local maxima only
- Forget the power of story
