# Brand Sponsorships & Negotiation

The definitive framework for evaluating, pricing, and negotiating brand partnership deals.

## Purpose

This module provides the complete playbook for brand sponsorship negotiations — from initial outreach through deal closure. It covers pricing strategy, value articulation, objection handling, and contract structure.

## Principles

1. **Anchor High, Adjust Down** — Always open negotiations with a price point 20-40% above your target. This creates room for concession and ensures you never leave money on the table. The first number spoken sets the frame.

2. **Value Before Price** — Never discuss pricing before establishing the full scope of value you deliver. Lead with audience demographics, engagement rates, case studies, and unique positioning before any numbers enter the conversation.

3. **Walk-Away Power** — The willingness to walk away from a deal is your greatest leverage. If a brand knows you need the deal, you've already lost the negotiation. Always have alternatives.

4. **Package, Don't Itemize** — Present sponsorship offerings as integrated packages rather than à la carte items. Bundling increases perceived value and makes direct price comparison harder.

5. **Exclusivity Has a Premium** — Category exclusivity (e.g., sole tech sponsor) commands a 30-50% premium. Always quantify the value of exclusivity in your proposals.

6. **Relationship Over Transaction** — Long-term partnerships generate 3-5x more revenue than one-off deals. Structure proposals to incentivize multi-quarter or annual commitments with escalating benefits.

7. **Data Tells the Story** — Every claim must be backed by verifiable metrics. CPM, engagement rates, conversion data, and audience overlap percentages are the language brands speak.

## Rules

**Always:** Back every pricing claim with data — CPM benchmarks, engagement rates, audience demographics.

**Always:** Send a one-page proposal summary before a detailed deck. Brevity earns attention.

**Never:** Accept the first offer without a counter. Even if it's generous, countering signals professionalism.

**Never:** Discuss pricing over email without a prior verbal conversation. Context and tone are lost in text.

**Avoid:** Discounting below your floor price. It devalues your platform for future negotiations.

**Prefer:** Annual contracts over quarterly — they provide revenue predictability and reduce admin overhead.

**Prefer:** Performance-based bonuses alongside base fees. They align incentives and can increase total deal value.

## Decision Frameworks

### Pricing Decision Tree

If the brand is a Fortune 500 with dedicated influencer marketing budget:
- Then: Price at the top of your range (premium tier). Propose a multi-channel package.
- Otherwise: If it's a startup or emerging brand → offer a performance-based hybrid with a lower base.

### Exclusivity Evaluation

If the brand requests category exclusivity:
- Then: Apply a 30-50% premium on top of base pricing.
- Otherwise: If they decline exclusivity → reduce price by 10% and offer non-exclusive placement.

### Negotiation Stalemate Resolution

If negotiations have stalled after two rounds of counters:
- Then: Introduce a new variable (timeline, deliverables, bonuses) to create movement.
- Otherwise: Propose a smaller pilot deal to build trust, with an option to scale.
