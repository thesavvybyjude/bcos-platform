# Lead_Magnet_Systems.md

## Purpose
To provide a complete framework for lead magnets and email capture.

## Core Philosophy
**Your email list is the only audience you truly own.**

- Social platforms can disappear
- Email is your leverage
- Lead magnets capture attention
- Sequences convert to customers

## Principles

### 1. Contextually Relevant Lead Magnets
- Every piece of content needs a lead magnet
- Must be relevant to the content
- Three lead magnets per month (one per pillar)
- Title matters most (80 cents in the dollar)

### 2. The Welcome Sequence
```
Day 1: Welcome + Case Studies → Sell/Book Call
Day 2: Why You Created This → Sell/Book Call
Day 3: Deeper Value → Context
Day 4: More Value → More Context
Day 5: FOMO → Final Push
```

### 3. The FOMO Sequence
- 4 emails over 4 days
- Case studies
- Why it exists
- Social proof
- Scarcity

## Process

### Lead Magnet Workflow:
```
1. Content idea → What's the lead magnet?
2. Create lead magnet (3-7 page PDF)
3. Make title irresistible
4. Put in video description or content
5. Capture email
6. Welcome sequence (5 days)
7. FOMO sequence (4 days)
8. Convert 15% of leads to customers
9. Weekly newsletter nurture
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Lead Magnet Should I Create?
  │
  ├─ What are my 3 content pillars?
  │   ├─ Pillar 1 → Lead magnet 1
  │   ├─ Pillar 2 → Lead magnet 2
  │   └─ Pillar 3 → Lead magnet 3
  │
  ├─ What's the title?
  │   └─ Make it irresistible
  │
  ├─ What's the promise?
  │   └─ Make it specific
  │
  └─ What's the next step?
      └─ Sales call, product, etc.
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Have a lead magnet per pillar
- Make titles irresistible
- Create welcome + FOMO sequences
- 15%+ conversion target

**Never:**
- Use generic lead magnets
- Forget the title matters most
- Skip welcome sequences
- Let leads go cold
