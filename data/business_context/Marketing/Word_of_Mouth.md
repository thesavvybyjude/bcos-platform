# Word_of_Mouth.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for word-of-mouth marketing.

## Core Philosophy
**The best marketing is word of mouth.**

- Paid marketing is inefficient
- Word of mouth is trust-based
- Physical presence matters
- Relationships drive referrals

## Principles

**1. Create a Reason to Talk**
- Unique experience
- Exceptional service
- Exclusivity
- Memorable moments

**2. Physical Presence Matters**
- Hand-to-hand combat
- Personal invitations
- Physical flyers
- In-person relationships

**3. Word of Mouth Is the Most Efficient Marketing**
- Cost per acquisition: Free
- Trust: High
- Conversion: High
- Scalability: Organic

**4. Influencer Strategy**
- Find creators who make good content
- Gift product to build roster
- Partner ads work better
- Flat fee, not percentage
- 30-50 creators in UGC roster

**5. YouTube Sponsorships**
- YouTube sponsorships are growing
- Better signal than other platforms
- Two handles = Better signal
- AI algorithm prefers partner ads

## Process

**Word of Mouth Flywheel:**
```
Create Experience → People Talk → More People Come → More People Talk → More People Come
```

**Influencer & Partner Workflow:**
```
1. Gift product (Refunnel)
2. Identify who makes good content
3. Bring into UGC roster (30-50 people)
4. Pay flat fee for content
5. Get whitelisting access
6. Run ads from their handle
7. Scale winners
```

## Decision Tree

```
Word of Mouth vs. Paid Marketing:
  |
  |- Can I create word of mouth?
  |   |- YES → Prioritize it
  |   |- NO → Need paid
  |
  |- Is this high-quality experience?
  |   |- YES → Word of mouth works
  |   |- NO → Paid won't save it
  |
  |- Can I create scarcity?
  |   |- YES → Word of mouth accelerates
  |   |- NO → Must work harder
  |
  |- Do I want long-term value?
  |   |- YES → Build word of mouth
  |   |- NO → Paid marketing is fine
  |
  |- Are partner ads working?
  |   |- YES → Scale
  |   |- NO → Build roster
  |
  |- Do I have a YouTube sponsorship strategy?
      |- YES → Good
      |- NO → Start building
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Create exceptional experiences
- Encourage people to talk
- Make it share-worthy
- Invest in relationships
- Build a creator roster
- Use partner ads
- Run YouTube sponsorships

**Never:**
- Rely solely on paid marketing
- Underinvest in experience
- Ignore word of mouth
- Make it boring
- Complicate partner ads with percentage math
- Skip gifting to find creators
