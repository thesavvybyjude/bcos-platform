# YouTube_Analytics.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for YouTube analytics.

## Core Philosophy
**Data is confirmatory, not leading.**

- The leading indicator is the principles
- If you can't tell it's good, data won't help
- Analytics are a lagging indicator
- For business owners, dollars per view matters most

## Principles

### 1. Two Ways to Approach YouTube
```
Media Game:
  ├─ Maximize views
  ├─ One of 10 ranking matters
  └─ Brand deals based on views

Business Game:
  ├─ Maximize dollars
  ├─ On-target views matter
  └─ Dollars per view is the metric
```

### 2. The One of 10 Ranking
- 1 out of 10 = Best performing of last 10
- 10 out of 10 = Worst performing
- 1, 2, 3 = Outperforming relative to your own videos
- Best apples-to-apples metric

### 3. CTR Is Noisy
- High CTR can lead to bad video performance
- Low CTR can lead to good video performance
- Not a clean metric
- Use as a signal, not a decision

### 4. If It's Underperforming
- Only thing you can change in real time: Title + Thumbnail
- Repackage it
- If it's a 7-10, ask ideas guy for new title
- Or let it go and learn for next time

## Process

### Analytics Review:
```
1. Check one of 10 ranking
2. 1-3: Study what worked (replicate)
3. 4-6: Middle of pack (fine)
4. 7-10: Consider repackaging
5. If repackaging doesn't work, learn and move on
6. For business: Track dollars per view
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Should I Do With This Video?
  │
  ├─ 1-3 of 10 → Great, replicate
  ├─ 4-6 of 10 → Fine, move on
  └─ 7-10 of 10 → Repackage or learn
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Focus on the principles
- Use data as confirmation
- Repackage underperformers
- Track dollars per view

**Never:**
- Obsess over data
- Make decisions solely on data
- Ignore the principles
- Forget that CTR is noisy
