# YouTube_Packaging.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for YouTube packaging (titles and thumbnails).

## Core Philosophy
**YouTube is a packaging game more than a content game.**

- If they don't click, the video is useless
- Thumbnail first, then title, then intro
- The intro confirms the click
- The packaging is the rocket booster (discarded after launch)

## Principles

### 1. The Packaging Sequence
```
1. Thumbnail catches the eye (stun)
2. Title triggers desire loop
3. Thumbnail complements title
4. Intro confirms the click
5. 30-60 seconds: First dopamine hit
6. Packaging is now discarded (viewer forgot it)
```

### 2. The Desire Loop
- Title triggers FOMO or desire
- "This is the most valuable marketing conversation"
- FOMO → Need to click
- Title creates a desire loop

### 3. The Rocket Analogy
- Packaging = Rocket booster
- Job: Get the rocket up
- Once up, booster is discarded
- Video must stand on its own

### 4. Clickbait ≠ Bait and Switch
- Clickbait: Bait the click, then give the bait
- Bait and switch: Bait the click, then switch
- People hate being switched
- You want clickbait, not bait and switch

## Process

### Packaging Creation:
```
1. Design thumbnail (stun the eye)
2. Write title (trigger desire loop)
3. Thumbnail should complement title
4. Write intro (confirm the click)
5. Put dopamine hit in first 30-60 seconds
6. Let the video stand on its own
```

## Decision Framework

```
Why Is My CTR Low?
  │
  ├─ Is the thumbnail interesting?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Fix it
  │
  ├─ Does the title create a desire loop?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Rewrite
  │
  ├─ Does the thumbnail complement the title?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Align them
  │
  └─ Does the intro confirm the click?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → Fix the intro
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Do packaging first (not last)
- Trigger a desire loop in the title
- Complement title with thumbnail
- Confirm the click in the intro

**Never:**
- Do packaging at the end
- Bait and switch
- Have disconnected thumbnail/title/intro
- Forget that packaging is the rocket booster
