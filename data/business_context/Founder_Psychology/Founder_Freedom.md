# Founder_Freedom.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for founder freedom and lifestyle design.

## Core Philosophy
**You are a free agent. You can go anywhere, do anything.**

- Entrepreneurship is about optionality
- The four Ws: Work when, where, with who, on what
- Play > Performance
- Live your life now, not later

## Principles

### 1. The Four Ws
```
1. Work WHEN you want
2. Work WHERE you want
3. Work WITH WHO you want
4. Work ON WHAT you want
```

### 2. Play Over Performance
- The "dread" vs. "play" mindset
- If you dread it, change it
- Focus on service, not outcomes
- Process > Outcome

### 3. Seasons, Not Steady State
- Work 60 hours in some seasons
- Vanish for 3 weeks in others
- Variety is the spice of life
- You choose what you do

## Process

### Founder Freedom Framework:
```
1. Build systems that run without you
2. Document everything
3. Build a team you trust
4. Step away regularly
5. Travel, experience, learn
6. Bring new insights back
7. Let spaciousness breed creativity
```

## Decision Framework

```
How to Design Your Life?
  │
  ├─ What's my ideal work week?
  │   └─ Build systems to enable it
  │
  ├─ Where do I want to live?
  │   └─ Go there (you're a free agent)
  │
  ├─ Who do I want to work with?
  │   └─ Only work with A players
  │
  └─ What do I want to work on?
      └─ Only work on what you love
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Design your life
- Choose your circumstances
- Focus on play, not performance
- Be a free agent

**Never:**
- Accept a victim mindset
- Wait to enjoy life
- Work on things you dread
- Let circumstances control you
