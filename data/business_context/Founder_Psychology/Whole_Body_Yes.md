# Whole_Body_Yes.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for making aligned decisions.

## Core Philosophy
**If it's not a hell yes in your mind, heart, and gut, it's a no.**

- Your head rationalizes
- Your heart feels
- Your gut knows
- Alignment = Whole body yes

## Principles

### 1. The Three Bodies
```
Mind → Rationalizes, calculates
Heart → Feels, emotional
Gut → Knows, intuition
```

### 2. Whole Body Yes
- Hell yes in your mind
- Hell yes in your heart
- Hell yes in your gut
- If any one is off → It's a no

### 3. Saying No to the Wrong Thing = Saying Yes to the Right Thing
- 55% equity in a 9-figure business
- But it didn't feel right
- Gave it back
- Built something better

## Process

### Whole Body Yes Framework:
```
1. When making a decision, ask:
   - What does my mind say?
   - What does my heart say?
   - What does my gut say?

2. If any one is a "no" or "maybe"
   → It's a no

3. If all three are "hell yes"
   → Go for it

4. Trust your gut
   → It's smarter than you think
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Say Yes?
  │
  ├─ Is it a hell yes in my mind?
  │   ├─ YES → Check next
  │   └─ NO → It's a no
  │
  ├─ Is it a hell yes in my heart?
  │   ├─ YES → Check next
  │   └─ NO → It's a no
  │
  ├─ Is it a hell yes in my gut?
  │   ├─ YES → Proceed
  │   └─ NO → It's a no
  │
  └─ All three yes? → Whole body yes
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Check all three bodies
- Trust your gut
- Say no to wrong things
- Say yes to aligned things

**Never:**
- Ignore your gut
- Rationalize a no into a yes
- Force something that feels wrong
- Let your head override your heart
