# Bottleneck_Mindset.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for overcoming founder bottleneck.

## Core Philosophy
**The biggest bottleneck in most businesses is the founder.**

- 98% of founders live in founder prison
- Constant Slack checking, email checking
- Team can't function without you
- Business collapses when you leave
- Systems are the escape route

## Principles

### 1. The Founder Prison
- 72% of founders deal with mental health issues
- Feeling like you can't step away
- Constant checking of Slack and email
- Business is dependent on you

### 2. The Bottleneck Is You
- Everyone comes to you for answers
- You're the single point of failure
- No systems = No freedom
- You are the cap on your success

### 3. The Escape Route
- Document everything
- Build systems
- Hire A players
- Trust your team
- Step away

## Process

### Bottleneck Escape Plan:
```
1. Identify where you're the bottleneck
2. Document that process
3. Assign ownership to someone else
4. Train them
5. Let go of control
6. Trust they'll do it
7. Free yourself
```

## Decision Framework

```
Am I the Bottleneck?
  │
  ├─ Do people come to me for every decision?
  │   ├─ YES → You're the bottleneck
  │   └─ NO → You're not (yet)
  │
  ├─ Can I step away for a week?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → You're the bottleneck
  │
  ├─ Does the team need my energy?
  │   ├─ YES → You're the bottleneck
  │   └─ NO → You have a system
  │
  └─ Would the business survive without me?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → You're the bottleneck
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build systems to remove yourself
- Trust your team
- Step away regularly
- Test the business without you

**Never:**
- Be the only decision maker
- Keep processes in your head
- Not take a vacation
- Think you're irreplaceable
