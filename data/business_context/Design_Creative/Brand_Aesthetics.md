# Brand_Aesthetics.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for brand aesthetics in hospitality.

## Core Philosophy
**Design is the story you tell.**

- Every venue tells a story
- Aesthetics communicate values
- Brand should be consistent but unique
- Design creates feeling

## Principles

**1. Inspiration Is Everywhere**
- Art blogs
- Fashion
- Film
- Everything

**2. Fashion + Film = Best Inspiration**
- These mediums capture aesthetics well
- They translate to hospitality
- They create emotional connections
- They define eras

**3. Each Venue Has Its Own Story**
- Nice Guy: Elevated restaurant
- Delilah: Supper club
- Bird Streets: Members club
- Different stories, same quality

## Sources of Inspiration

| Source | Why It Works |
|--------|--------------|
| Fashion | Colors, textures, patterns |
| Film | Mood, lighting, drama |
| Art | Composition, emotion |
| Architecture | Space, flow |

## Decision Framework

```
What Aesthetic Fits This Venue?
  |
  |- What's the concept?
  |   |- Restaurant → Warm, approachable
  |   |- Nightclub → Dark, energetic
  |   |- Supper club → Elegant, lively
  |   |- Members club → Home, comfortable
  |
  |- Who's the audience?
  |   |- Young → Trendy, bold
  |   |- Established → Elegant, refined
  |   |- Mixed → Balanced
  |
  |- What story does it tell?
      |- Romantic → Candlelit
      |- Social → Open, inviting
      |- Exclusive → Private, intimate
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Tell a story through design
- Be inspired by fashion and film
- Create unique experiences
- Maintain quality standards

**Never:**
- Copy others
- Be generic
- Ignore the narrative
- Sacrifice quality
