# Lighting_Design.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for lighting design in hospitality.

## Core Philosophy
**Lighting makes or breaks a room.**

- Good lighting creates magic
- Bad lighting ruins the experience
- People feel better with good lighting
- Lighting is the most important design element

## Principles

**1. No Overhead Lighting**
- Overhead lighting is harsh
- It creates shadows and bad angles
- Never use overhead lights
- Everyone looks better in ambient light

**2. Face Wash Lighting**
- Light should come from below
- Upward-facing illumination
- Flatters every face
- Creates intimacy

**3. Natural Flame/Candle Ambiance**
- Candlelight is universally flattering
- Multiple small lights are better than one big light
- Face wash = Candle at table level
- Warm tones over cool tones

## Process

**Lighting Design:**
```
1. No overhead lights
2. Candlelight at every table
3. Warm amber tones
4. Light should wash faces from below
5. Test with different people
6. Adjust until everyone looks good
7. Create intimate vignettes
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Avoid overhead lighting
- Use warm tones
- Test lighting on different people
- Create intimate spaces

**Never:**
- Use cool/white light
- Have harsh overheads
- Ignore how people look
- Overlight spaces
