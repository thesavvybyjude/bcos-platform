# Experience_Design.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for designing premium experiences.

## Core Philosophy
**Every detail is an opportunity to create magic.**

- People notice when you care
- Surprise and delight
- Attention to detail is the difference
- Experiences create loyalty

## Principles

**1. Create Little Surprises**
- Custom sayings on plates
- Unique touches
- Details that make people smile
- Surprise and delight

**2. If Even One Person Notices, It's Worth It**
- You're doing it for the 1%
- The detail matters
- It creates differentiation
- It builds brand

**3. Every Element Is Curated**
- Plates, forks, knives
- Napkins, bathroom details
- Matchbooks, coasters
- Everything is intentional

## Examples

**Delilah Plate Details:**
- Custom sayings on plate bottoms
- "You dirty thief"
- "Caught you looking"
- Small touches that surprise guests

**Candy Station:**
- Guests get candy on the way out
- Fun, memorable touch
- Leaves a lasting impression
- Creates goodwill

## Rules

**Always:**
- Curate every detail
- Surprise and delight
- Create memorable moments
- Make it personal

**Never:**
- Be generic
- Ignore the details
- Under-invest in experience
- Forget the small touches
