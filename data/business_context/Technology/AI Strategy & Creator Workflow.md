# AI Strategy & Creator Workflow

The framework for integrating AI tools into business operations and creator workflows to maximise output while preserving quality and authenticity.

## Purpose

This module covers the strategic adoption of AI tools across the business — from content creation and research to automation and agent workflows. It provides principles for selecting, deploying, and governing AI tools within a creator-led business.

## Principles

1. **AI Amplifies, Not Replaces** — AI should multiply the output of your best people, not substitute for thinking. Use AI for speed and scale; use humans for judgement and nuance.

2. **Start With the Workflow, Not the Tool** — Map your existing workflow first, identify bottlenecks and repetitive steps, then select the AI tool that addresses those specific friction points. Don't adopt tools looking for problems.

3. **Quality Control is Non-Negotiable** — Every AI-generated output must pass through a human quality gate before publication or action. The 80/20 rule applies: AI gets you 80% there; human refinement delivers the final 20%.

4. **Own Your Prompts** — Effective prompts are intellectual property. Document, version, and iterate on your prompt library. A well-crafted prompt is a reusable business asset.

5. **Data Privacy First** — Never send confidential business data, client information, or proprietary strategies to public AI models without understanding the data retention and training policies.

6. **Build Agent Workflows Incrementally** — Start with single-step automations (e.g., summarise this transcript), graduate to chains (summarise → extract action items → create tasks), and only then build fully autonomous agents.

## Rules

**Always:** Maintain a prompt library with version history. Document what works and what doesn't.

**Always:** Audit AI tool costs monthly. Usage-based pricing can scale unexpectedly.

**Never:** Publish AI-generated content without human review and editing.

**Never:** Use AI to impersonate real people or generate fake testimonials.

**Avoid:** Over-reliance on a single AI provider. Maintain the ability to switch models.

**Prefer:** Open-source or self-hosted models for sensitive business operations.

## Decision Frameworks

### Tool Selection Matrix

If the task is creative (writing, design, ideation):
- Then: Use AI as a brainstorming partner, not the final creator. Generate options, then curate.
- Otherwise: If the task is analytical (data processing, summarisation, classification) → AI can handle end-to-end with spot-check quality gates.

### Build vs Buy Decision

If the AI capability is core to your competitive advantage:
- Then: Build custom (fine-tuned model, custom agent). Own the IP.
- Otherwise: If it's a commodity capability (transcription, translation, scheduling) → buy/subscribe to an existing tool.
