# Team_Culture.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building and maintaining team culture.

## Core Philosophy
**Culture is the operating system of your company.**

- Culture determines outcomes
- Culture must be intentional
- Culture requires maintenance
- Culture is a competitive advantage

## Principles

**1. Build Strong Values**
- Transparency
- Excellence
- Hustle
- Ownership

**2. Maintain High Standards**
- A players only
- No mediocrity
- Continuous improvement
- Excellence as habit

**3. Overcommunicate**
- Weekly updates
- All-hands meetings
- Clear priorities
- Constant alignment

**4. Polar Opposites Can Work**
- Different strengths
- Different perspectives
- Same values
- Common goals

**5. Alignment on Values Matters Most**
- Not about agreeing on everything
- Same ethics
- Same vision
- Same goals

**6. Disagreement Is Healthy**
- See all sides
- Better decisions
- Balanced outcomes
- Stronger company

**7. Don't Lose the Startup Feeling**
- Keep the hunger
- Stay humble
- Never think you've arrived
- Ego is the enemy

**8. Celebrate Briefly, Then Move On**
- 1,000 locations = "Another day"
- Don't rest on laurels
- The game is never won
- Keep moving forward

**9. Humility Is a Competitive Advantage**
- Ego kills businesses
- Be confident but not arrogant
- Listen to feedback
- Always improve

## Core Philosophy Addendum
**The best partnerships balance each other.**

## Core Philosophy Addendum 2
**You're still a startup even at 1,000 locations.**

## ADDITION: AI Adoption Culture

**Core Philosophy Addendum 3:**
**If you're not AI-native, you're obsolete.**

### Additional Principles:

**10. Everyone Must Be AI-Native**
- No exceptions
- 2-3 hours daily for AI
- If you're not using AI, you're getting cooked
- One day a week dedicated to AI deep dive

**11. Teach AI, Don't Fear It**
- AI is a tool, not a threat
- Partner with AI = More valuable
- Those who partner win
- Those who fear lose

**12. The 3Ds of Automation**
- Dangerous jobs → Robots
- Dull jobs → Robots/AI
- Dirty jobs → Robots/AI
- Focus on what's hard for computers

**13. The Two-Percent Problem**
- 2% of people act on what they learn
- Don't be the 98%
- Knowledge is cheap, execution is everything
- Action is the differentiator

### 14. Sandbox Culture
- Give people room to experiment
- Safe space to try new things
- Fail fast, learn faster
- Innovation requires freedom

### 15. Obligation to Impact
- Everyone has a responsibility to contribute
- Not just do their job
- Look for ways to make impact
- Culture is everyone's job

### 16. Daily Standup
- 15-minute daily sync
- What did you do yesterday?
- What are you doing today?
- Any blockers?
- Keeps everyone aligned
- No exceptions

### 17. Two Types of People
- Builders (make things happen)
- Optimizers (make things better)
- Both are essential
- Don't mix them up
