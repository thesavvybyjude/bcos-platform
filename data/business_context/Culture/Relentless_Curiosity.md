# Relentless Curiosity

## Purpose
To provide a framework for building a culture of curiosity.

## Core Philosophy
**Curiosity is the engine of innovation.**

- Curious companies are learning companies
- Learning companies are growing companies
- The company that stops asking questions stops improving
- Relentless curiosity is a competitive advantage

## Principles

### 1. Curiosity Drives Growth
- The companies that ask the best questions win
- Curiosity is the source of innovation
- A curious culture is a resilient culture
- The world changes; curious companies change with it

### 2. The Death of Curiosity
- Companies stop asking questions when they think they have all the answers
- Arrogance kills curiosity
- Success can be the enemy of learning
- The moment you stop being curious is the moment you start dying

### 3. Curiosity as a Defense
- The curious survive because they adapt
- The arrogant die because they assume
- Curiosity is survival
- Ask good questions now so you do not have to regret later

### 4. The Curious Culture
- Everyone is encouraged to ask questions
- No question is too stupid
- The goal is to learn, not to be right
- Curiosity is celebrated, not punished

## Decision Framework

```
Is My Culture Curious?
  |
  +-- Are questions encouraged?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Problem
  |
  +-- Do people admit when they do not know?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Ego problem
  |
  +-- Is learning rewarded?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Missed opportunity
  |
  +-- Do we ask good questions?
      +-- YES -> Good
      +-- NO -> Need to work on it
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Encourage questions
- Reward learning
- Celebrate curiosity
- Be humble

### Never:
- Kill curiosity
- Reward arrogance
- Think you have all the answers
- Stop asking questions
