# Channel_Economics.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for channel economics and strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**The 2026 playbook is top-of-funnel demand + value realization + volume movement.**

- Channels have different economics
- Some are acquisition, some are retention
- Understand the role of each channel
- Optimize the portfolio, not the individual

## Principles

### 1. Channel Economics
| Channel | Role | Economics |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Meta | New customer acquisition | 80% .com, 20% Amazon |
| Google (search) | Demand capture | Tax you have to pay |
| YouTube | Top of funnel | 50% .com, 50% Amazon |
| TikTok Shop | Attention + volume | Neutral or slightly losing |
| Amazon Ads | Defense | Low incrementality |
| CTV | Brand awareness | Retail/Amazon heavy |

### 2. TikTok Shop Is Different
- Ads to .com = Terrible
- Setup TikTok Shop + affiliate network
- Run at neutral or slightly losing
- Drive effect through entire ecosystem

### 3. Amazon Ads Are a Tax
- They don't create net new demand
- They defend your branded search terms
- Competition drives up the tax
- Low incrementality, high necessity

### 4. The 2026 Pyramid
```
Top: YouTube, TikTok Shop (demand creation)
  │
Middle: Limited edition, high AOV (value realization)
  │
Bottom: Amazon, .com (volume movement)
```

## Process

### Channel Strategy:
```
1. Determine your channel mix
2. Understand the economics of each
3. Set expectations for each channel
4. Measure total business impact
5. Shift budget based on total impact
6. Don't optimize channels in isolation
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Use This Channel?
  │
  ├─ Do I understand the economics?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Test first
  │
  ├─ Can I measure total impact?
  │   ├─ YES → Use
  │   └─ NO → Need measurement first
  │
  ├─ Does it fit my distribution?
  │   ├─ YES → Use
  │   └─ NO → Consider other channels
  │
  └─ What's the role? (Acquisition? Retention?)
      └─ Match strategy to role
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Understand channel economics
- Measure total business impact
- Match channel to role
- Optimize portfolio, not individual

**Never:**
- Use TikTok Shop for .com conversion
- Think Amazon ads drive net new demand
- Ignore the top-of-funnel channels
- Optimize channels in isolation
