# Validity_Marketing.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for validity marketing.

## Core Philosophy
**Validity is the currency of trust.**

- People trust what others trust
- Social proof is marketing
- Restock is the ultimate validation
- Shout your victories

## Principles

**1. Restock Is the Most Powerful Word**
- "Restocked" signals demand
- "Restocked" signals scarcity
- "Restocked" signals success
- Use it everywhere

**2. Number One Is Unbeatable**
- "Number one on Amazon"
- "Best-selling at Target"
- "Fastest-growing energy drink"
- People want what's winning

**3. Screenshot and Share**
- Retailer emails
- Sales reports
- Award notifications
- Share everything

## Process

**Validity Marketing:**
```
1. Win something
2. Screenshot it
3. Post it everywhere
4. Tell your story
5. Let others amplify
6. Repeat
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Share validity statements
- Use "restock"
- Show social proof
- Celebrate wins

**Never:**
- Be quiet about wins
- Underestimate social proof
- Forget to share
- Be humble
