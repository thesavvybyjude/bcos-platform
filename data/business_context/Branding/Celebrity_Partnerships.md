# Celebrity_Partnerships.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for celebrity partnership strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**Celebrity partnerships are about authenticity, not endorsements.**

- 3-year minimum deals
- Equity is key (not just cash)
- Partners must use the product
- Reputation is everything

## Principles

### 1. Minimum 3-Year Deals
- Short-term deals benefit no one
- Long-term commitment required
- Build brand together
- Aligned incentives

### 2. Equity Over Cash
- Equity aligns interests
- Partners become invested in success
- Not transactional
- Long-term vision

### 3. Authenticity Is Everything
- Partners must use the product
- Reputational risk is real
- Product quality is non-negotiable
- They choose you, not just any brand

### 4. Structure for Success
```
Deal Structure:
  │
  ├─ 3-year minimum
  ├─ Equity component
  ├─ Performance-based cash
  └─ Active involvement (taste testing, approvals)
```

## Decision Framework

```
Which Celebrity to Partner With?
  │
  ├─ Do they use the product?
  │   ├─ YES → Good fit
  │   └─ NO → Not right
  │
  ├─ Is it a 3-year view?
  │   ├─ YES → Aligned
  │   └─ NO → Too short
  │
  ├─ Are they willing to take equity?
  │   ├─ YES → Committed
  │   └─ NO → Transactional
  │
  └─ Is their reputation aligned?
      ├─ YES → Proceed
      └─ NO → Risk
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Go for 3-year minimum
- Include equity
- Ensure product usage
- Build long-term together

**Never:**
- Do short-term deals
- Be transactional
- Choose non-users
- Compromise on quality
