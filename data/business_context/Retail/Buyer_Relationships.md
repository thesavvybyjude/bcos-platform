# Buyer_Relationships.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building retail buyer relationships.

## Core Philosophy
**Retailers are run by humans, not faceless corporations.**

- Buyers make emotional decisions
- They want to bet on winners
- They have egos too
- Build personal relationships

## Principles

**1. Go In Person**
- Don't just send a salesperson
- Build first-name relationships
- Be present
- Show you care

**2. Find the Entrepreneur Within**
- Every retailer has entrepreneurial buyers
- They want to take chances
- They want to be the hero
- Be their winning bet

**3. Involve Them in the Process**
- Show them three products (two bad, one good)
- Let them feel involved
- Make them feel like partners
- They will champion you

## Process

**Buyer Relationship Building:**
```
1. Research the buyer
2. Find what they follow on Instagram
3. Sign creators they follow
4. They see your brand everywhere
5. They reach out to you
6. You have leverage
7. Build relationship
8. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
How to Approach This Buyer?
  |
  |- Have they reached out to me?
  |   |- YES → Strong position
  |   |- NO → Need to build awareness
  |
  |- Do I have proof of demand?
  |   |- YES → Show them
  |   |- NO → Build proof first
  |
  |- Can I go in person?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Build relationship remotely
  |
  |- Are they entrepreneurial?
      |- YES → They'll bet on you
      |- NO → Need more evidence
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Go in person
- Build personal relationships
- Make them feel involved
- Show proof of demand

**Never:**
- Send only a salesperson
- Be faceless
- Ignore their ego
- Forget to follow up
