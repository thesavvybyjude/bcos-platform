# Retail_Strategy.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for retail distribution strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**Retail is a barrier to entry.**

- Anyone can start a DTC brand
- Few can get into retail
- Retail creates competitive advantage
- 50,000 doors = Significant moat

## Principles

**1. Shoot Wide, Not Exclusive**
- Give it to anyone who wants it
- Don't play exclusivity games
- Let sales dictate performance
- FOMO is powerful with retailers

**2. One Product, All Channels**
- Same product everywhere
- Don't create channel exclusives
- Make it accessible everywhere
- Simplicity wins

**3. Sell Through > Sell In**
- Being on shelves is not success
- Actually selling is success
- Empty shelf = Bad reputation
- Fill your orders

## Process

**Retail Strategy:**
```
1. Prove demand (DTC/Amazon)
2. Target Tier 1 retailer (Target)
3. Win that account
4. Use as proof to other retailers
5. Expand to 50,000+ doors
6. Maintain relationships
7. Never miss a PO
```

## Decision Framework

```
Which Retailer Should I Target?
  |
  |- Is this a Tier 1 retailer?
  |   |- YES → Prioritize
  |   |- NO → Build proof first
  |
  |- Is my product a fit?
  |   |- YES → Target
  |   |- NO → Need product fit
  |
  |- Do I have proof of demand?
  |   |- YES → Strong position
  |   |- NO → Build proof first
  |
  |- Can I fill POs?
      |- YES → Target
      |- NO → Need supply chain first
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Prove demand first
- Win Tier 1 accounts
- Sell through, not just sell in
- Fill every PO

**Never:**
- Miss POs
- Exclusive with one retailer (unless strategic)
- Ignore sell-through
- Underestimate operations
