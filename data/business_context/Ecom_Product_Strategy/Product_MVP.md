# Product_MVP.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for evaluating product opportunities.

## Core Philosophy
**Every product should solve a specific business problem.**

- Margin problem → Higher margin product
- Volume problem → High velocity product
- Cash problem → Better terms, seasonal smoothing

## Principles

### 1. The Three Product Problems
```
Margin Problem
  │
  ├─ Bad value-to-weight ratio
  ├─ Low gross margin
  └─ Need: Higher margin product

Volume Problem
  │
  ├─ Category growth is slow
  ├─ Competition is high
  └─ Need: High velocity product

Cash Problem
  │
  ├─ Seasonal concentration
  ├─ Long PO lead times
  └─ Need: Product that smoothes cash flow
```

### 2. The MVP Framework
- Most Valuable Product
- What business problem does this solve?
- Score products against the problem
- Not just "what's cool"

### 3. Category Growth vs. Competition
- E-commerce grows 15-20% per year
- Competition grows faster than that
- If category is flat, your growth must come from expansion
- Product expansion is growth marketing

## Process

### Product Evaluation:
```
1. Identify business problem (margin, volume, or cash)
2. Generate product ideas
3. Score each against the problem
4. Choose the highest-scoring product
5. Test with small PO
6. Scale if successful
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Product Should I Launch?
  │
  ├─ What's the biggest business problem?
  │   ├─ Margin → Find higher margin product
  │   ├─ Volume → Find high velocity product
  │   └─ Cash → Find seasonal smoothing product
  │
  ├─ What's the category growth?
  │   ├─ Growing → Opportunity
  │   └─ Flat → Need expansion
  │
  └─ What's the competition?
      ├─ Low → Easier
      └─ High → Need differentiation
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Solve a business problem
- Match product to the problem
- Test before scaling
- Category expansion is growth marketing

**Never:**
- Launch products because they're "cool"
- Ignore business problems
- Launch without testing
- Ignore category dynamics
