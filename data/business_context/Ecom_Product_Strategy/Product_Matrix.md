# Product_Matrix.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for product portfolio management.

## Core Philosophy
**Every SKU is its own little business.**

- Different SKUs have different economics
- Some need investment, some need liquidation
- Treat each SKU accordingly
- Hidden gems are the biggest opportunity

## Principles

### 1. The Product Matrix
```
                    High Media Investment  │  Low Media Investment
────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────
High Volume         │  Champions           │  Hidden Gems
────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────
Low Volume          │  Growth Drivers      │  Underperformers
────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────
```

### 2. Champions
- High volume, good efficiency
- You know what they are (hero SKU)
- Protect and maximize margin
- Don't run out of inventory

### 3. Hidden Gems
- High volume, low media investment
- Massive opportunity
- Invest more to scale
- Usually overlooked

### 4. Growth Drivers
- High media investment, low volume
- New bets
- Could become champions
- Test and learn

### 5. Underperformers
- Low volume, high inventory
- Liquidate
- Make new bet

## Process

### Product Matrix Analysis:
```
1. Map every SKU by volume and media investment
2. Identify Champions (protect)
3. Identify Hidden Gems (invest)
4. Identify Growth Drivers (test)
5. Identify Underperformers (liquidate)
6. Repeat quarterly
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Strategy for This SKU?
  │
  ├─ Champion → Protect margin, ensure inventory
  │
  ├─ Hidden Gem → Invest more, scale
  │
  ├─ Growth Driver → Test, evaluate, scale or cut
  │
  └─ Underperformer → Liquidate, make new bet
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Map every SKU
- Find hidden gems
- Protect champions
- Liquidate underperformers

**Never:**
- Treat all SKUs the same
- Over-invest in underperformers
- Under-invest in hidden gems
- Run out of champion inventory
