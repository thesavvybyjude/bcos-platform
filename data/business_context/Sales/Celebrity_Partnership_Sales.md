# Celebrity_Partnership_Sales.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for selling celebrity partnerships.

## Core Philosophy
**Celebrity partnerships are sold through authenticity and vision.**

- Meet them one-on-one
- Share the journey
- No handing off to juniors
- Win through trust

## Principles

### 1. One-on-One Is Essential
- Don't delegate this
- Personal connection required
- Trust is built in person
- You are the brand

### 2. Share the Vision
- Long-term view (5-20 years)
- Not just another celebrity brand
- Must live beyond them
- Legacy matters

### 3. Product Must Be Great
- Reputational risk is real
- They will be judged harshly
- Product quality is non-negotiable
- Clinical trials matter

### 4. Weekly Engagement
- Weekly calls with their team
- They approve everything (taste, etc.)
- Active partnership
- Not passive endorsement

## Process

### Celebrity Partnership Process:
```
1. Personal introduction
2. One-on-one meeting
3. Share vision (long-term)
4. Show product quality
5. Offer equity (not just cash)
6. Structure 3-year minimum
7. Weekly engagement
8. Build together
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Meet one-on-one
- Offer equity
- Show long-term vision
- Engage weekly

**Never:**
- Delegate to juniors
- Be transactional
- Think short-term
- Ignore reputational risk
