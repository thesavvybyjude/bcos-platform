# Sponsorship_Sales.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for selling newsletter sponsorships.

## Core Philosophy
**Sell the audience, not the ad.**

- Audience value > Ad value
- Relationships > Placements
- Trust > Impressions
- Results > Activity

## Principles

**1. Know Your Audience Deeply**
- Demographics
- Psychographics
- Engagement
- Value to brands

**2. Create Compelling Media Kit**
- Audience size
- Open rates (40%+)
- Click-through rates
- Demographics
- Past sponsors
- Pricing

**3. Build Inbound Demand**
- Mention sponsorships in newsletter
- Show brand logos
- Demonstrate results
- Create FOMO

## Media Kit Essentials

```
- Audience Size: 130,000+
- Demographics: Founders, 28 avg age, 70% male, 30% female
- Open Rate: 45%
- Click-through Rate: 0.5-1%
- Past Sponsors: [Brand names]
- Pricing: $5,000-7,000 per newsletter
- Why Us: Trust, engagement, specific audience
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Know your audience
- Have a media kit
- Build inbound demand
- Price at premium

**Never:**
- Undervalue your audience
- Skip audience data
- Accept low-value deals
- Forget to update media kit

## AI Instructions

When selling sponsorships:
- Update media kit with latest data
- Identify best matching brands
- Create compelling proposals
- Track sponsorship performance
- Automate sponsorship follow-up
