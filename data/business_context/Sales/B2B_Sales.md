# B2B_Sales.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for B2B sales through newsletters.

## Core Philosophy
**Your audience is a sales pipeline.**

- Every subscriber is a prospect
- Trust is pre-built
- Warm leads convert faster
- B2B audiences are valuable

## Principles

**1. Target B2B Decision Makers**
- Founders, CTOs, CMOs
- High LTV customers
- Premium prices
- Long-term relationships

**2. Convert Through Trust**
- Build relationship first
- Educate through content
- Nurture over time
- Convert when ready

**3. Price Discriminate**
- Different prices for different customers
- Enterprise pays more
- Value-based pricing
- Custom quotes

## Example

**Time Magazine Conversion:**
- CTO subscribed to newsletter
- Read for 6 months
- Built trust through content
- Convinced team to switch to Beehiiv
- 100% inbound

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build trust first
- Provide value before selling
- Target decision makers
- Price for value

**Never:**
- Hard sell too early
- Ignore high-value segments
- Underprice premium audiences
- Forget to nurture
