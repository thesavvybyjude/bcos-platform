# B2B Strategy

## Purpose
To provide a framework for B2B sales strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**B2B is about relationships and value, not features.**

- Consumers buy emotion; businesses buy outcomes
- B2B sales is about trust and reputation
- Your network is your net worth
- The best B2B sales are built on relationships

## Principles

### 1. B2B Is About Trust
- Businesses don't buy features; they buy results
- They invest in people who can deliver
- Trust is more important than price
- A trusted advisor gets the deal

### 2. Relationships Are Everything
- The strongest deals are built on relationships
- Long-term relationships lead to more revenue
- People buy from people they know, like, and trust
- Invest in relationships, not just in pipeline

### 3. The Network Effect
- Every deal leads to another
- A satisfied customer is your best source of referrals
- Each relationship is a deposit that earns interest
- The richer your network, the richer your deal flow

### 4. Price Is Not Everything
- If you only compete on price, you are in trouble
- Value-based selling beats price-based selling
- If they need to buy from you, they will pay what you ask
- Be the expert, not the commodity

## Decision Framework

```
Is This a B2B Relationship or a Transaction?
  |
  +-- Am I building a relationship?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Short-term thinking
  |
  +-- Am I delivering value or just features?
  |   +-- Value -> Good
  |   +-- Features -> Need to reframe
  |
  +-- Am I trusted or just convenient?
  |   +-- Trusted -> Good
  |   +-- Convenient -> Replaceable
  |
  +-- Is this the first deal or the first of many?
      +-- First of many -> Good
      +-- One and done -> Need to build relationship
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Build relationships
- Deliver value, not features
- Be a trusted advisor
- Think long-term

### Never:
- Just sell features
- Underinvest in relationships
- Think price is the only thing
- Treat every deal as a transaction
