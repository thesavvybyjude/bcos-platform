# Consultative Selling

## Purpose
To provide a framework for consultative selling.

## Core Philosophy
**The best salespeople are consultants, not salespeople.**

- Ask more than you tell
- Listen more than you talk
- Solve problems before you sell
- They buy because you understand them, not because you pitched

## Principles

### 1. Ask First, Sell Second
- The best salespeople ask the right questions
- They uncover the customer's problem before presenting a solution
- You cannot sell to someone you do not understand
- Ask, listen, diagnose, prescribe

### 2. Create Insight
- Show them something they did not know
- Change their perspective on their problem
- They buy from people who make them smarter
- Insight creates urgency

### 3. Build Trust
- Do not oversell
- Be honest about limitations
- They trust you when you are not just trying to make a sale
- Trust is built over time

### 4. Diagnosis Before Prescription
- You cannot treat what you have not diagnosed
- Spend more time understanding the problem than presenting the solution
- The prescription is easy once the diagnosis is right
- Sell the problem, not the solution

## Process

### Consultative Selling:
```
1. Ask questions to understand the problem
2. Listen more than you talk
3. Diagnose the root cause
4. Prescribe the solution (your product/service)
5. Show them the outcome
6. Build trust throughout
7. They buy because you understood them
```

## Decision Framework

```
Am I Selling or Consulting?
  |
  +-- Am I asking more than I am telling?
  |   +-- YES -> Consulting
  |   +-- NO -> Selling
  |
  +-- Do I understand their problem?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Ask more questions
  |
  +-- Am I creating insight?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need to show them something new
  |
  +-- Have I built trust?
      +-- YES -> Good
      +-- NO -> Need to invest more time
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Ask more than you tell
- Diagnose before prescribing
- Create insight
- Build trust

### Never:
- Pitch before you understand
- Oversell
- Ignore the problem
- Forget trust
