# Relationship_Sales.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for relationship-based sales.

## Core Philosophy
**Relationships are the ultimate currency.**

- People do business with people they trust
- Long-term relationships win
- Trust is more valuable than transactions
- Networks compound over time

## Principles

**1. Be Non-Transactional**
- Don't do things for tit for tat
- Help without expectation
- Build genuine relationships
- Trust is built over time

**2. Build a Network That Compounds**
- 10 years of relationships
- Networks get easier to leverage
- Reputation precedes you
- People want to work with you

**3. The Hockey Stick Effect**
```
Time → Relationships → Opportunities
  |
  |- Years 1-5: Building relationships, little return
  |- Years 5-10: Relationships start paying off
  |- Years 10+: Networks create exponential opportunities
```

**4. Negotiation in Sales**
- Use tactical empathy before disagreeing
- Say "Is now a bad time to talk?" to open
- Use "Have you given up on X?" to re-engage
- Get to "That's right" not "You're right"
- Bargain last, not first (65/85/95/100 framework)

**5. Listening as a Superpower**
- Most negotiations break down over lack of listening
- Hearing ≠ Listening ≠ Comprehension
- Demonstrate comprehension before responding
- Silence triggers neurochemicals (6 seconds)

**6. Genuineness Wins**
- Deception by omission is a time bomb
- Be genuine about your limitations
- Honesty builds trust
- Trust enables collaboration

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Be Transactional or Relational?
  |
  |- Do I want short-term or long-term?
  |   |- Short-term → Transactional
  |   |- Long-term → Relational
  |
  |- Do I want to scale?
  |   |- YES → Build relationships
  |   |- NO → Transactions are fine
  |
  |- Do I want to build a brand?
  |   |- YES → Relationships build reputation
  |   |- NO → Transactions build revenue
  |
  |- What's my timeline?
      |- 1-3 years → Transactional works
      |- 10+ years → Relationships win
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build genuine relationships
- Help without expectation
- Play the long game
- Nurture your network

**Never:**
- Be transactional
- Keep score
- Burn bridges
- Ignore relationships
