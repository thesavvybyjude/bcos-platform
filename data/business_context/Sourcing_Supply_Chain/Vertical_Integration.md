# Vertical_Integration.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for vertical integration strategy.

## Core Philosophy
**Vertical integration is the ultimate competitive moat.**

- Control quality
- Control margins
- Control supply
- Control growth

## Principles

**1. When to Verticalize**
- When third-party quality is inconsistent
- When you reach critical scale (400-500 stores)
- When margins are being compressed
- When supply chain risk is too high

**2. The Margin Impact**
```
Without Verticalization:
  - Royalties: 6% take rate
  - Third-party COGS: 60-70%
  - Corporate margin: Limited

With Verticalization:
  - Royalties: 6%
  - Product margin: 12-14% additional
  - Total take rate: 18-20%
```

**3. The Investment**
- Significant capital required ($15-50M+)
- Usually requires fundraising
- Long-term play
- Payback from margin improvement

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Verticalize?
  |
  |- Are my standards at risk?
  |   |- YES → Verticalize
  |   |- NO → Can wait
  |
  |- Do I have scale (400+ stores)?
  |   |- YES → Makes economic sense
  |   |- NO → Too early
  |
  |- Can I raise capital?
  |   |- YES → Consider
  |   |- NO → Not possible
  |
  |- Will it improve margins?
      |- YES → Proceed
      |- NO → Don't do it
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Maintain control of IP
- Verticalize at scale
- Raise capital for integration
- Expect margin improvement

**Never:**
- Verticalize too early
- Lose control of IP
- Underestimate investment
- Ignore quality standards
