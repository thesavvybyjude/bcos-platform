# Reverse_Prompting.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for reverse prompting.

## Core Philosophy
**Stop overprompting. Start with the outcome.**

- Tell AI what you want, not how to do it
- Let the AI ask you questions
- The AI knows more than you do
- Your job: define the outcome

## Principles

### 1. Old Way: Overprompting
```
Bad: "Go to the store, buy this, then do this, then this..."
  → You don't know the best way
  → You're limiting the AI
  → AI is a yes-man, agreeing with you
```

### 2. New Way: Reverse Prompting
```
Good: "I need a business dashboard for my company."
  → AI asks: What kind of company?
  → AI asks: What data do you have?
  → AI asks: What do you want to see?
  → AI builds exactly what you need
```

### 3. The Cousin Marcus Framework
- Imagine AI is your genius cousin (160 IQ)
- He wants to see you win
- He just needs direction on the outcome
- Let him figure out how to get there

## Process

### Reverse Prompting Workflow:
```
1. Start with the outcome
   "I need to accomplish X"

2. Let AI ask questions
   "Ask me any questions you need"

3. Answer the questions
   Use Whisper Flow for speed

4. Let AI work
   It figures out the best way

5. Review and iterate
```

### Prompt Examples

**Reverse Prompt Examples:**
```
"I have a government contracting company and I need a business dashboard to manage my business."

"Ask me any questions you need to help me create a marketing strategy for my new product."

"I need to automate my finance department. Ask me what you need to know."

"I want to create a master prompt for my role. Interview me to create it."
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Start with the outcome
- Let AI ask questions
- Use dictation for answers
- Trust the AI's process

**Never:**
- Overprompt
- Tell AI exactly how to do it
- Limit the AI's thinking
- Pretend you know the best way
