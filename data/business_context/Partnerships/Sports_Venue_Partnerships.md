# Sports_Venue_Partnerships.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for sports venue partnerships.

## Core Philosophy
**Sports venues are new hospitality frontiers.**

- Stadiums are becoming experiential
- Premium hospitality is growing
- Scarcity applies to sports too
- Retention through experience

## Principles

**1. Create White Glove Sports Experiences**
- VIP lounges
- Exclusive access
- Personal service
- Premium experience

**2. Focus on Retention, Not Acquisition**
- Season ticket holders are VIPs
- Retention drives revenue
- Experience = Retention
- Scarcity = Premium experience

**3. Offer Value Before Getting Value**
- Do it for free to prove yourself
- Show what you can create
- Build trust with ownership
- Then formalize partnership

## Process

**Sports Venue Partnership:**
```
1. Build relationship with ownership
2. Offer to create experiences for free
3. Show what you can do
4. Create buzz and demand
5. Formalize partnership
6. Build dedicated venue
7. Expand to more teams
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build ownership relationships
- Prove yourself first
- Create premium experiences
- Focus on retention

**Never:**
- Ask before proving yourself
- Create average experiences
- Ignore the VIP segment
- Think small
