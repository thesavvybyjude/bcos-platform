# Investor_Relationships.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building investor relationships.

## Core Philosophy
**The best investors are strategic partners, not just capital.**

- Find the right partners
- Alignment is everything
- Strategic over financial
- Long-term vision

## Principles

**1. Find Strategic Partners**
- Shared vision
- Industry expertise
- Global footprint
- Add value beyond money

**2. Align on Vision**
- Same mindset
- Same values
- Same goals
- You should learn from them

**3. Clean Cap Table Through Recapitalization**
- Buy out old investors
- Clean cap table for growth
- Keep strategic investors
- Focus on long-term

## Decision Framework

```
Which Investor Is Right?
  |
  |- Do they share my vision?
  |   |- YES → Consider
  |   |- NO → Skip
  |
  |- Do they add strategic value?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Money alone isn't enough
  |
  |- Are they aligned on values?
  |   |- YES → Strong fit
  |   |- NO → Will be problems
  |
  |- Will they let me operate?
      |- YES → Proceed
      |- NO → Skip (autonomy matters)
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Find strategic partners
- Align on vision
- Value partnerships over capital
- Maintain autonomy

**Never:**
- Take money from misaligned partners
- Compromise vision for capital
- Rush into partnerships
- Ignore values
