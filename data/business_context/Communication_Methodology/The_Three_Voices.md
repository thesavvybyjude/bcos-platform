# The_Three_Voices.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for using the three negotiation voices.

## Core Philosophy
**The tone of voice you use is more important than the words you say.**

- Each voice triggers different responses
- Matching the wrong voice = Boxing match
- The right voice disarms and opens
- Playfulness is a superpower

## Principles

**1. The Three Voices**
```
1. The Assertive Voice
   └─ Direct and honest
   └─ What feels direct to deliverer = Blunt force trauma to receiver
   └─ "I need it and I need it now"
   └─ Donald Trump's public voice (private voice is charming)

2. The Late Night FM DJ (Analyst Voice)
   └─ Slow, downward inflecting
   └─ Drop your chin to bring voice down
   └─ Creates calm and safety

3. The Accommodator's Voice (Playful Voice)
   └─ "I'm happy to see you"
   └─ "This is great"
   └─ "I need you to wave a magic wand"
   └─ Playfulness is disarming
```

**2. Using Assertiveness**
- If someone is assertive, don't match it
- Matching assertiveness = Boxing match
- Assertives get over name-calling fast (Wolverine healing)
- Calming, soothing, differential voice = Assertives love it
- Assertives are vulnerable to difference

**3. Using Playfulness**
- Playfulness is disarming
- You're 31% smarter in a positive frame of mind
- You're healthier
- Financial benefits are secondary
- The bill collector example: 80% off

**4. The Bill Collector Story**
- Called a bill collector with a medical bill
- Said: "I owe you money" (playfully)
- Woman laughed and gave 80% off
- Why? Because nobody calls a bill collector playfully
- It completely disarmed her

## Process

**Using the Right Voice:**
```
1. Assess the situation
2. Choose the right voice
3. For assertives: Use calming, soothing, differential
4. For analysis: Use calm, slow, downward inflecting
5. For accommodations: Use playful voice
6. For most situations: Playful works best
```

## Decision Framework

```
Which Voice Should I Use?
  │
  ├─ Is the other side assertive?
  │   ├─ YES → Use calming, soothing voice (not matching)
  │   └─ NO → Continue
  │
  ├─ Is the other side analytical?
  │   ├─ YES → Use slow, downward inflecting voice
  │   └─ NO → Continue
  │
  ├─ Is the other side accommodator?
  │   ├─ YES → Use playful voice
  │   └─ NO → Try playful anyway
  │
  └─ Is the situation tense?
      ├─ YES → Use playful voice
      └─ NO → Use appropriate voice
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Be aware of which voice you're using
- Don't match assertiveness
- Use playfulness when possible
- Drop your chin for calming voice

**Never:**
- Match assertiveness with assertiveness
- Be cold and distant
- Forget the power of playfulness
- Think tone doesn't matter
