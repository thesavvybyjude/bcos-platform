# Storytelling_Conversion.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for storytelling that converts.

## Core Philosophy
**Storytelling is the connective tissue. Conversion comes from proof.**

- Trust comes from proof or demonstration of ability
- The right topic + right meat = Conversion
- Contrast keeps them watching
- Payoff closes the loop

## Principles

### 1. The Difference: Story vs. Conversion
- Storytelling = Preserves attention (connective tissue)
- Conversion = Hits of what they'd get if they bought
- Topic is the most important thing
- What you cover in the meat of the video matters

### 2. The Shock Score
- Rate each idea from 0-100
- How shocking would it be to reveal this?
- Out of 100 people, how many would have thought it?
- Higher shock = More curiosity

### 3. The Zigzag Path
```
Common belief → New reality → Common belief → New reality
  │
  └─ Keep zigzagging through the story
  └─ Each zig creates curiosity
  └─ Each zag delivers a hit of value
```

### 4. Payoff Is Non-Negotiable
- People hate being bait and switched
- Need to close the last loop
- Payoff confirms the bait
- Without payoff, trust is eroded

## Process

### Storytelling for Conversion:
```
1. Pick the right topic (shock score)
2. Establish context
3. Add contrast
4. Zigzag through the story
5. Include proof/demonstration
6. Deliver the payoff
7. Close the last loop
```

## Decision Framework

```
Will This Story Convert?
  │
  ├─ Is there proof/demonstration?
  │   ├─ YES → Likely to convert
  │   └─ NO → Just entertainment
  │
  ├─ Is the shock score high?
  │   ├─ YES → Good hook
  │   └─ NO → Boring
  │
  ├─ Is there a payoff?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Bait and switch
  │
  └─ Are they getting hits of value?
      ├─ YES → Converting
      └─ NO → Not yet
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Include proof/demonstration
- Use shock score to pick topics
- Zigzag through the story
- Deliver the payoff

**Never:**
- Bait and switch
- Forget proof
- Have no payoff
- Be boring
