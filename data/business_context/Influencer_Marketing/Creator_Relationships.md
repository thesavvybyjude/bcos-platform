# Creator_Relationships.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building authentic creator relationships.

## Core Philosophy
**Creators are partners, not vendors.**

- Know their birthdays
- Send gifts on Mother's Day
- Support them on tough days
- Build real relationships

## Principles

**1. No Attribution = Stronger Relationships**
- No affiliate codes
- No coupon codes
- No pressure to perform sales
- Focus on authentic content

**2. Give Creative Freedom**
- Let creators speak naturally
- Don't over-script
- Trust their expertise
- The algorithm rewards authenticity

**3. Be There for Them**
- Personal outreach
- Gifts and support
- Genuine care
- Long-term relationships

### 4. Deep Authentic Relationships
- Go beyond transactional
- Know their story
- Understand their goals
- Invest in the relationship
- Not just about what they can do for you

### 5. The Who Method
- Know who they are deeply
- What do they care about?
- What are their values?
- What do they need?
- Build around the person, not the audience

## Process

### Creator Relationship Building:
```
1. Initial outreach (personal)
2. Send product (no pressure)
3. Let them create freely
4. Celebrate their wins
5. Show appreciation
6. Build long-term partnerships
7. Go deeper: know their story
8. Invest in the relationship
9. Repeat
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build real relationships
- Give creative freedom
- Support their success
- Be human
- Go deep
- Know who they are
- Invest in the person

**Never:**
- Force affiliate codes
- Over-control creative
- Be transactional
- Forget personal touch
- Treat them as vendors
- Ignore their story
