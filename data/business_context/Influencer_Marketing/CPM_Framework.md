# CPM_Framework.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for measuring and optimizing influencer marketing CPM.

## Core Philosophy
**If you know your Meta CPM, you know your influencer goal.**

- Cut Meta CPM in half = Influencer goal
- CPM below $2 is world-class
- Views are the metric, not sales
- Attribution is overrated

## Principles

**1. Know Your Meta CPM Baseline**
- What does Meta cost you?
- Example: $20 CPM on Meta
- Goal: $10 CPM on influencer
- Push for $2-5 CPM

**2. Calculate CPM Correctly**
```
CPM = Cost / (Views / 1,000)

Example:
$100,000 spend / 50,000,000 views × 1,000
= $2 CPM
```

**3. Adjust for Audience Fit**
- If 50% of following is outside US → Cut CPM by 50%
- If 25% is male (and you want female) → Cut CPM by 25%
- Only pay for your ICP

## Process

**CPM Calculation:**
```
1. Determine Meta CPM baseline
2. Set goal at 50% of Meta CPM
3. Look at creator's last 10 videos
4. Calculate average views
5. Set your budget
6. Pay based on expected views
7. Track actual performance
8. Optimize over time
```

## Benchmark Table

| Performance | CPM | Description |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| World-class | $1-3 | Consistently achievable at scale |
| Strong | $3-5 | Good performance |
| Good | $5-10 | On par with Meta |
| Poor | $10+ | Worse than Meta |

## Decision Framework

```
What Should I Pay This Creator?
  |
  |- What's their avg views (last 10 videos)?
  |   |- 100,000 avg → Calculate budget
  |   |- 10,000 avg → Adjust accordingly
  |
  |- What's the US/International split?
  |   |- 90% US → Full value
  |   |- 50% US → 50% CPM
  |
  |- What's the gender split?
  |   |- 90% female → Full value
  |   |- 50% female → 50% CPM
  |
  |- What's my target CPM?
      |- $5 goal → Budget = 100,000/1,000 × $5 = $500
      |- Adjust for audience quality
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Know your Meta CPM
- Set influencer CPM goal
- Adjust for audience quality
- Track performance

**Never:**
- Pay without calculating CPM
- Ignore audience geography
- Overpay for bad fit
- Forget to optimize

## AI Instructions

When evaluating influencer deals:
- Calculate expected CPM
- Adjust for audience quality
- Compare to Meta baseline
- Track performance over time
- Optimize by creator type
