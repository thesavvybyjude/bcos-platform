# Influencer_Program.md

## Purpose
To provide a complete framework for building an in-house influencer marketing program at scale.

## Core Philosophy
**Your influencer program should be the most efficient marketing channel in your arsenal.**

- CPM should be 50% of Meta
- Build in-house, not with agencies
- Your team should look like your avatar
- Relationships are the competitive advantage

## Principles

**1. Build a Boiler Room Outbound Engine**
- Copy the sales floor model
- Thousands of outbounds daily
- Scale through volume
- Trust the process

**2. Your Team = Your Avatar**
- The "Bloom Girl" concept
- Team should reflect your consumer
- Cultural alignment is everything
- This has been working for 7 years

**3. No Attribution, All Views**
- Don't optimize for Shopify
- Don't use affiliate codes
- Focus on top of funnel
- Omni-channel = Retail makes attribution irrelevant

## Structure

**Influencer Program Team Structure:**
```
Director (Former intern, now leader)
  |
  |- Account Managers (Relationship managers)
  |   |- Scouts (Interns, 24/7 scouting)
  |
  |- 15-25 people total in-house
```

## Process

**Influencer Outreach Workflow:**
```
1. Scout (Intern level)
   → 24/7 on Instagram/TikTok
   → Identify potential creators
   → Based on ICP

2. Account Manager
   → Build relationship
   → Send product
   → Negotiate terms

3. Director
   → Oversee program
   → Set strategy
   → Optimize performance
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Use Influencer Marketing?
  |
  |- Is my product high-quality?
  |   |- YES → Influencer works
  |   |- NO → Fix product first
  |
  |- Can I build in-house?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Consider agency (less ideal)
  |
  |- Can I track CPM?
  |   |- YES → Measure efficiency
  |   |- NO → Need tracking first
  |
  |- Is my team my avatar?
      |- YES → Strong fit
      |- NO → Hire differently
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Build in-house
- Keep team young (under 30 preferred)
- Let creators have creative freedom
- Track CPM religiously

**Never:**
- Use agencies long-term
- Over-control creative
- Optimize for short-term sales only
- Ignore relationship building

## AI Instructions

When building an influencer program:
- Scout aggressively and constantly
- Use VPNs for algorithmic diversity
- Build genuine creator relationships
- Track CPM religiously
- Let creators do what they do best
