# Advertising.md

## Purpose
To provide complete frameworks for monetizing newsletters through advertising.

## Core Philosophy
**Your audience is the asset. Brands pay to access it.**

- Ads are the most common monetization
- Premium audiences command premium rates
- Trust is currency
- Relationships drive premium deals

## Principles

**1. Sell Based on Audience, Not Just Size**
```
Audience Value = Size × Engagement × Demographic Fit
  |
  |- Size: How many subscribers
  |- Engagement: Open rate, CTR
  |- Demographic: Who they are
```

**2. Use Multiple Ad Models**
| Model | Best For | Rate Range |
|-------|----------|------------|
| CPM | Reach/Scale | $3-7 per 1,000 opens |
| CPC | Performance | $2-5 per click |
| Flat Rate | Premium audiences | $1,000-10,000+ |
| Fixed Sponsorship | Brand association | $5,000-50,000+ |

**3. Build a Media Kit That Converts**
- Audience size
- Open rates (40%+ is good)
- Click-through rates (0.5%+ is good)
- Demographic data
- Past sponsors (social proof)
- Testimonials

## Decision Framework

```
Which Ad Model Should I Use?
  |
  |- Am I small (<10k subscribers)?
  |   |- YES → Use Beehiiv Ad Network (hands-off)
  |   |- NO → Continue
  |
  |- Do I have a premium audience?
  |   |- YES → Sell direct, flat rate
  |   |- NO → Use CPM/CPC models
  |
  |- Do I have time to sell?
  |   |- YES → Direct sales (higher rates)
  |   |- NO → Ad network (lower rates, passive)
  |
  |- Is my audience B2B?
      |- YES → Premium rates possible
      |- NO → Standard CPM rates
```

## Benchmark Table

| Metric | Poor | Average | Good | Excellent |
|--------|------|---------|------|-----------|
| Open Rate | <30% | 30-40% | 40-50% | 50%+ |
| CTR (ad) | <0.25% | 0.25-0.5% | 0.5-1% | 1%+ |
| CPC | <$1 | $1-2 | $2-3 | $3+ |
| CPM | <$2 | $2-4 | $4-6 | $6+ |

## Processes

**Setting Up Direct Sponsorships:**
```
1. Create media kit
2. Build in-bound interest through newsletter
3. Set flat rate (e.g., $5,000-7,000)
4. Provide demo/audience data
5. Close deal
6. Execute sponsorship
7. Provide post-campaign data
8. Renew/repeat
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Maintain high open rates (>40%)
- Know your audience deeply
- Share audience data in media kit
- Build trust with sponsors

**Never:**
- Sell to brands you don't believe in
- Overcrowd ads
- Ignore ad performance
- Underprice premium audiences

## AI Instructions

When evaluating ad performance:
- Analyze open rate impact of ads
- Identify high-performing ad language
- Optimize ad placement
- Predict ad performance
- Recommend pricing based on data
