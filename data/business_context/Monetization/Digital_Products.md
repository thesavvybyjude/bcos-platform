# Digital_Products.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for monetizing newsletters through digital products.

## Core Philosophy
**Your knowledge is a product waiting to be packaged.**

- You already have the content
- Repackage existing knowledge
- Create high-margin products
- Leverage your expertise

## Principles

**1. Use Existing Content**
- Presentations → Courses
- Articles → Guides
- Experience → Playbooks
- Knowledge → Templates

**2. Keep It Simple**
- 50-page deck
- 30-minute video
- $10-50 price point
- High perceived value

**3. Leverage Your Audience**
- Promote to your list
- Email to drive sales
- Create scarcity
- Use social proof

## Process

**Creating a Digital Product:**
```
1. Identify what people ask you most
2. Package as a product
3. Record/create in 1-2 days
4. Price based on value
5. Promote to your audience
6. Update/iterate based on feedback
```

## Example

**Big Desk Energy Digital Product:**
- Product: Zero to 100k Full Playbook
- Format: 50-slide deck + 30-min video
- Price: $10
- Promoted: 3 times
- Results: 1,000 sold = $10,000
- Cost to create: 30-40 minutes (from existing presentation)

## Rules

**Always:**
- Use existing content
- Keep production simple
- Price accessibly
- Promote regularly

**Never:**
- Overcomplicate
- Overprice
- Stop promoting
- Ignore feedback
