# IRL_Events.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for monetizing newsletters through IRL events and experiences.

## Core Philosophy
**Online community becomes real relationships in person.**

- Email builds community
- Community wants connection
- IRL experiences are premium products
- High margin, high value

## Principles

**1. Understand Your Audience Deeply**
- Who are they?
- What do they want?
- What problems do they face?
- What experiences would they value?

**2. Create High-Premium Experiences**
- Exclusive access
- Small groups (8-10 people)
- Beautiful location
- Expert content

**3. Price Based on Value**
- $5,000-10,000+
- Founders will pay for network
- Premium experience = Premium price
- Low cost = Low commitment

## Example

**Founder Mastermind Costa Rica:**
- Audience: Founders/Startup employees
- Format: 8 founders, 5 days
- Location: Costa Rica villa
- Activities: Surfing, private boat, private chef
- Content: Strategy sessions, hiring, scaling, AI
- Price: $10,000/person
- Application: 40 applications for 8 spots

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Create IRL Events?
  |
  |- Is my audience B2B/Founders?
  |   |- YES → Strong fit
  |   |- NO → Consider community events
  |
  |- Can I charge premium prices?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Consider lower-cost events
  |
  |- Do I have the network to fill?
  |   |- YES → Go for it
  |   |- NO → Build network first
  |
  |- Can I deliver premium experience?
      |- YES → Launch event
      |- NO → Start with smaller event
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Know your audience deeply
- Create exclusive experiences
- Deliver massive value
- Build a network of alumni

**Never:**
- Undersell the experience
- Overpromise
- Ignore logistics
- Forget post-event engagement

## AI Instructions

When planning IRL events:
- Segment highest-engaged subscribers
- Analyze interest through surveys
- Create compelling event messaging
- Build application process
- Automate follow-up
