# Consulting_Coaching.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for monetizing expertise through consulting and coaching.

## Core Philosophy
**Your experience is valuable. People will pay for access to you.**

- Premium buyers want direct access
- High value, high price
- One-to-one is scalable with price
- Demand is directly from your content

## Principles

**1. Price Based on Value**
- $500-1,000+/hour
- Premium audience = Premium price
- Price filters quality
- High price = High commitment

**2. Use Your Platform to Generate Demand**
- Newsletter readers already know you
- Trust is built through content
- They want to work with you
- Respond to inbound interest

**3. Keep It Simple**
- One-hour calls
- Focused on specific problems
- Clear outcomes
- Easy booking system

## Decision Tree

```
Should I Offer Consulting?
  |
  |- Do people ask me for advice?
  |   |- YES → Consider consulting
  |   |- NO → Build audience first
  |
  |- Is my time valuable?
  |   |- YES → Price high
  |   |- NO → Offer free advice
  |
  |- Does this scale?
  |   |- YES → Offer regularly
  |   |- NO → Only if very high price
  |
  |- Does this distract from business?
      |- YES → Limit or don't do
      |- NO → Go for it
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Price high
- Keep focused
- Deliver massive value
- Automate scheduling

**Never:**
- Undervalue your time
- Overcommit
- Ignore your core business
- Take on wrong clients
