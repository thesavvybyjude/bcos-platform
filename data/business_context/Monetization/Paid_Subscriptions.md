# Paid_Subscriptions.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for monetizing newsletters through subscription models.

## Core Philosophy
**Subscriptions convert attention into recurring revenue.**

- Free builds audience
- Paid converts believers
- Recurring revenue is predictable
- Value justifies payment

## Principles

**1. Free + Premium Model**
- Free newsletter builds trust
- Premium newsletter delivers deeper value
- 90% free, 10% paid is healthy
- Convert top fans to paid

**2. Bundle Value**
```
Free: Weekly newsletter
Paid: Weekly newsletter + Additional weekly content + Community access + Webinars
```

**3. Price Based on Value**
- $10-30/month is standard
- B2B audiences can pay more
- Price signals quality
- Annual discount (e.g., 20% off)

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Add Paid Subscriptions?
  |
  |- Do I have >10k engaged subscribers?
  |   |- YES → Consider paid tier
  |   |- NO → Focus on free growth first
  |
  |- Do I have exclusive content to offer?
  |   |- YES → Proceed with paid
  |   |- NO → Build paid offering first
  |
  |- Is my audience willing to pay?
  |   |- B2B → Higher willingness
  |   |- Consumer → Lower willingness
  |   |- Test with waitlist
  |
  |- Is this the right revenue model?
      |- Advertising works? → Both
      |- Products work? → Both
      |- Events work? → Both
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Free content must be valuable
- Paid content must be 10x more valuable
- Make upgrade frictionless
- Continuously add value

**Never:**
- Paywall everything
- Let free content decline
- Ignore churn
- Price too low
