# TAM_Strategy.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for sizing and pursuing total addressable market (TAM).

## Core Philosophy
**Go after bigger TAMs, not smaller ones.**

- Niche products have low ceilings
- Larger categories = Larger outcomes
- Don't be afraid of saturation
- Saturation means education is already done

## Principles

**1. Start Niche, Scale Up**
```
Booty Bands → $10M TAM
  ↓
Pre-workout → $100M TAM
  ↓
Greens → $1B TAM
  ↓
Energy Drinks → $50B TAM
  ↓
Soda → $100B+ TAM
```

**2. Each Product Has a Ceiling**
- Female pre-workout: $10M max
- Greens: $100M-$1B
- Energy: $50B+
- Soda: $100B+
- Always know your ceiling

**3. If You're Not Growing TAM, You're Stagnating**
- Comfort is the enemy
- Level up constantly
- Go after bigger categories
- Don't settle

## Decision Framework

```
What's the TAM?
  |
  |- What's the total category size?
  |   |- <$100M → Limited upside
  |   |- $100M-$1B → Good opportunity
  |   |- $1B-$50B → Strong opportunity
  |   |- $50B+ → Massive opportunity
  |
  |- Is the category growing?
  |   |- Growing → Enter
  |   |- Declining → Avoid
  |
  |- Is it global?
  |   |- YES → Larger TAM
  |   |- NO → Smaller TAM
  |
  |- Is it saturated?
      |- YES → Education done → Good
      |- NO → Need to educate → Hard
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Know your TAM
- Go after bigger categories
- Don't be afraid of saturation
- Level up constantly

**Never:**
- Stay in niche forever
- Be afraid of competition
- Settle for small TAM
- Ignore category size
