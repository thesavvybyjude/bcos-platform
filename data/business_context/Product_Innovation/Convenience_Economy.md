# Convenience Economy

## Purpose
To provide a framework for winning the convenience economy.

## Core Philosophy
**The future belongs to the frictionless.**

- Consumers are less loyal and more demanding
- Convenience is the new loyalty
- If you're not easier, you're irrelevant
- The winner is the one who removes the most friction

## Principles

### 1. Convenience Is the New Pricing
- Consumers don't just compare price; they compare effort
- The brand that requires the least effort wins
- One-click ordering, free shipping, simple returns
- If it's easier, they'll pay more

### 2. The Loyalty Paradox
- Consumers aren't loyal to brands; they're loyal to convenience
- When a better option appears, they leave
- Loyalty is simply inertia from convenience
- Stop confusing convenience with loyalty

### 3. Friction Removal
- Every step you add is a customer you lose
- Reduce steps to purchase
- Eliminate barriers
- Make it stupidly simple

### 4. The 3-Click Rule
- If it takes more than 3 clicks, you've lost them
- Friction compounds
- Every additional click costs you 20% of potential customers
- The simpler you make it, the more you sell

## Decision Framework

```
How Convenient Is My Business?
  │
  ├─ How many steps to purchase?
  │   ├─ 1-3 → Good
  │   └─ 4+ → Too much friction
  │
  ├─ Is shipping free?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Potential barrier
  │
  ├─ Is there a one-click option?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Add one
  │
  └─ Is returns easy?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → Fix it
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Remove friction
- Make it easy
- Free shipping is table stakes
- One-click is the goal

### Never:
- Add unnecessary steps
- Make returns hard
- Charge surprise fees
- Think loyalty means they'll tolerate friction
