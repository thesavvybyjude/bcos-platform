# Founder_Health.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for founder health and wellness.

## Core Philosophy
**Entrepreneurship is an inner game. Health is the foundation.**

- 72% of founders deal with mental health
- Substances compound the problem
- Testosterone is the chemical that makes effort feel good
- Health = Competitive advantage

## Principles

### 1. Blood Work Twice a Year
- Testosterone levels (target 800+)
- Hormones
- Stress markers
- Everything

### 2. The Sobriety Lesson
- Substances ruin your health
- They impact testosterone (200 vs 800)
- Testosterone makes effort feel good
- Entrepreneurship is hard enough without health issues

### 3. Founders Are Martyrs
- Pay yourself first (eventually)
- Your health is the foundation
- You can't build a business if you're broken
- Health is a business investment

## Decision Framework

```
How to Prioritize Health?
  │
  ├─ When was my last blood work?
  │   ├─ <6 months → Good
  │   └─ >6 months → Schedule it
  │
  ├─ What's my testosterone level?
  │   ├─ 800+ → Healthy
  │   └─ Below → Investigate
  │
  ├─ Am I using substances?
  │   ├─ NO → Good
  │   └─ YES → Consider sobriety
  │
  └─ Am I sleeping 7+ hours?
      ├─ YES → Good
      └─ NO → This is a problem
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Get blood work twice a year
- Prioritize sleep
- Exercise daily
- Consider sobriety

**Never:**
- Ignore your health
- Think substances help
- Be a martyr forever
- Put business before health
