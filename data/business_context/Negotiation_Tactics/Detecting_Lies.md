# Detecting_Lies.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for detecting deception.

## Core Philosophy
**Different personality types lie differently.**

- Accommodators baffle you with bullshit
- Analysts deceive by omission
- Assertives gaslight you

## Principles

**1. The Accommodator Liar**
- Baffles you with bullshit
- Playful, nice interaction
- You're completely confused
- They talk a lot but you feel attacked
- They're lying by overwhelming you

**2. The Analyst Liar**
- Deception by omission
- Tells you literal truths that are very deceptive
- "You're the low bidder" (when you're the only bidder)
- Deception by omission
- Deception by literal truth

**3. The Assertive Liar**
- Gaslighting
- Attacks and accuses you
- Tries to destabilize you
- Knocks you off your game
- They're lying by attacking

**4. How to Tell**
- How quickly do they answer? Too quickly = Plan, not listening
- Are they confused about what they just said? = Lying
- Are they attacking you and claiming to be a victim? = Unreachable
- Look for yellow flags (they become red flags)

## Process

**Detecting Deception:**
```
1. Listen to what they're saying
2. Identify the personality type
3. Look for the corresponding deception pattern
4. If they're baffling you → Accommodator liar
5. If they're being deceptive by omission → Analyst liar
6. If they're attacking you → Assertive liar
7. If you're confused → You're being lied to
```

## Decision Framework

```
Is the Other Side Lying?
  │
  ├─ Are they baffling you?
  │   ├─ YES → Accommodator liar
  │   └─ NO → Continue
  │
  ├─ Are they deceiving by omission?
  │   ├─ YES → Analyst liar
  │   └─ NO → Continue
  │
  ├─ Are they attacking and accusing you?
  │   ├─ YES → Assertive liar
  │   └─ NO → They might be telling the truth
  │
  └─ Are you confused?
      ├─ YES → They're lying
      └─ NO → They might be honest
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Know the personality type
- Look for the corresponding deception pattern
- Trust confusion as a signal
- Look for yellow flags (they become red flags)

**Never:**
- Ignore confusion
- Think all lies look the same
- Forget the personality types
- Trust too quickly
