# Partnership_Flywheel.md

## Purpose
To provide a complete framework for building a partnership flywheel.

## Core Philosophy
**Partnerships are the most efficient way to build trust in a new category.**

- 50%+ of marketing budget on partnerships
- Over 8-figure annual spend
- Partners build trust when brand can't
- Long-term deals create compounding value

## Principles

### 1. Lead with High-Trust Partners
- People trust people more than brands
- Partners bring credibility
- "I tried it, I love it" > Brand ads
- Trust is the currency of new categories

### 2. Work with the Best at What They Do
- Jon Jones = GOAT of UFC
- Dez Lynon = Boston Marathon winner
- Joe Rogan = Best at what he does
- Steven Bartlett = #1 business podcast
- Excellence commands excellence

### 3. Start with Vibes
- Principle-to-principle relationships
- Direct texting basis with partners
- No agents, no middlemen (if possible)
- Creative chemistry matters

### 4. Everything Starts with Organic
- Experts talk about ketones organically
- People Google what they hear
- Find your partners through natural interest
- Then formalize the relationship

### 5. Authentic Before Commercial
- Get to know partners before the deal
- They should never tell you what you want to hear
- Always tell you what you need to hear
- Deep authentic relationships

### 6. High Trust Brand Attracts High Trust Partners
- Tim Ferriss won't partner with a low-trust brand
- Arnold won't take brand risk
- Your brand is your reputation
- Protect it to attract the best

### 7. The Fidosia Decision
- Partners see it: "They made the right decision"
- It anchors trust
- It shows you're long-term
- It attracts other high-trust partners

## Process

### Partnership Flywheel:
```
1. Experts talk about topic organically
   → People Google ketones
   → They find Ketone IQ

2. Formalize partnership with experts
   → Create long-term deals (3+ years)
   → Make content together

3. Chop content into thousands of ads
   → Whitelist through partner accounts
   → Full-funnel distribution

4. Partners get a share of spend
   → They're incentivized to create more
   → Flywheel accelerates
```

### Partnership Building:
```
1. Be at the forefront of high performance
2. Work with the best
3. Do what you say you're going to do
4. Invest in research and certifications
5. Make hard decisions
6. Partners refer other partners
7. Flywheel spins
```

## Decision Tree

```
Which Partner Should I Work With?
  │
  ├─ Are they the best in their field?
  │   ├─ YES → Strong fit
  │   └─ NO → Consider if they have trust
  │
  ├─ Do they command trust?
  │   ├─ YES → Strong fit
  │   └─ NO → Not worth it
  │
  ├─ Do I vibe with them personally?
  │   ├─ YES → Essential
  │   └─ NO → Will be difficult
  │
  ├─ Is this a surface-level relationship?
  │   ├─ NO → Good
  │   └─ YES → Build depth first
  │
  └─ Is there organic interest already?
      ├─ YES → Accelerates success
      └─ NO → Need more education
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Lead with vibes
- Work with the best
- Think long-term (3+ years)
- Build personal relationships
- Build authentic relationships
- Do what you say
- Make hard decisions

**Never:**
- Work with people you don't vibe with
- Use agents as primary contact
- Do one-off deals
- Ignore organic interest signals
- Work with low-trust brands
- Let surface-level relationships be "partners"
- Compromise for short-term gain
