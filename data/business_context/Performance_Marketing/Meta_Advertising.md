# Meta_Advertising.md

## Purpose
To provide a complete framework for scaling Meta advertising.

## Core Philosophy
**Meta is not saturated—it's underutilized.**

- 85% of marketing spend on Meta
- $150,000+ daily spend
- Still room for 24-36 months of growth
- Creative diversity is the key to scaling

## Principles

### 1. Meta Can Scale Massively
- $150K+/day is just the beginning
- Target: $200-300K/day
- Not even close to saturation
- Platform has huge capacity

### 2. Creative Diversity = Scale
- 1,500+ ads running simultaneously
- Started at 50 ads 13 months ago
- Constantly testing new content
- Good content, not just new content

### 3. Statics Are Underrated
- 75% statics, 25% video
- Statics are faster to produce
- Statics are highly effective
- Video is for brand, statics for performance

## Decision Tree

```
How to Scale Meta Spend?
  │
  ├─ Do I have enough creative?
  │   ├─ YES → Scale
  │   └─ NO → Need more creative
  │
  ├─ Is my CAC > LTV?
  │   ├─ YES → Pause
  │   └─ NO → Scale
  │
  ├─ Do I have good statics?
  │   ├─ YES → Scale
  │   └─ NO → Need statics
  │
  └─ Is payback <4 months?
      ├─ YES → Scale aggressively
      └─ NO → Optimize first
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Diversify creative
- Test constantly
- Scale when economics work
- Keep statics in the mix

**Never:**
- Rely on one creative
- Stop testing
- Scale bad economics
- Ignore creative fatigue
