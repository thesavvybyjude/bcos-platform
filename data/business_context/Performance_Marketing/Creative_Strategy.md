# Creative_Strategy.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for creative content strategy at scale.

## Core Philosophy
**The algorithm rewards diversity.**

- 1,500+ ads = Winner
- Statics are the backbone
- Video is for authenticity
- AI generates statics, real people generate video

## Principles

### 1. Volume Matters
- 1,500+ ads running
- From 50 ads to 1,500+ in 13 months
- Constant output is required
- The algorithm needs variety

### 2. Statics Over Videos
```
75% Statics
  │   - AI-generated
  │   - Fast production
  │   - Direct response
  │   - Highly effective
  │
25% Video
  │   - Real people
  │   - Authentic stories
  │   - Brand building
  │   - Premium positioning
```

### 3. Video Will Be the Future Differentiator
- Statics will become a commodity
- AI will make statics easy for everyone
- Video requires real people = Authenticity
- Video will be the competitive advantage

## Process

### Creative Production Workflow:
```
1. Generate statics with AI (volume)
2. Create videos with real people (authenticity)
3. Test everything
4. Kill underperformers
5. Double down on winners
6. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Content Should I Make?
  │
  ├─ For performance → Statics
  │   ├─ AI-generated
  │   └─ Fast production
  │
  ├─ For brand → Video
  │   ├─ Real people
  │   └─ Authentic stories
  │
  └─ For scale → Both
      ├─ Statics for volume
      └─ Video for brand
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Produce high volume
- Use AI for statics
- Use real people for video
- Test everything

**Never:**
- Rely on one creative
- Underinvest in statics
- Forget video authenticity
- Stop testing
