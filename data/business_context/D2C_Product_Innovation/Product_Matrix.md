# Product_Matrix.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for managing product portfolio.

## Core Philosophy
**Not every product is a hit. Kill the duds, scale the winners.**

- Most product launches fail
- You can't make a dud work
- Winners are obvious
- Focus on what works

## Principles

**1. The Album Mentality**
- Album has 12 songs, 1 is the single
- Not all songs are singles
- You can't make a dud work
- Focus on the winner

**2. The Watch Launch Story**
- First product launch after wallets
- Massive flop
- Thought the website was broken
- Nobody bought them
- Two weeks later: Rings launched and crushed

**3. The Discovery Period**
- Try a bunch of stuff
- See what works with your brand
- It's random, not predictable
- Keep trying until you find winners

**4. Price Point Matters**
- High enough for DTOC (over $50)
- Relevant to audience (cross-sell)
- Amazon/wholesale potential
- If Target gives you a PO, do it

## Process

**Product Matrix Management:**
```
1. Launch product (under $500K)
2. Wait for data (email, organic, paid)
3. If it wins → Scale
4. If it doesn't → Move on
5. Don't keep failing products alive
6. Double down on winners
7. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Keep or Kill This Product?
  │
  ├─ Is it winning on email?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Problem
  │
  ├─ Is it winning on organic?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Problem
  │
  ├─ Is it winning on paid?
  │   ├─ YES → Scale
  │   └─ NO → Kill
  │
  └─ Is it profitable?
      ├─ YES → Keep
      └─ NO → Kill
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Kill duds quickly
- Scale winners aggressively
- Keep launch costs low
- Keep trying new things

**Never:**
- Keep failing products alive
- Spend more to make a dud work
- Ignore data
- Bet the farm on one product
