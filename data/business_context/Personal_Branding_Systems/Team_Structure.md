# Team_Structure.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for content team structure.

## Core Philosophy
**Build a media company, not a content department.**

- A players attract A players
- 90% of hires come inbound via your personal brand
- Taste and judgment > Technical skill
- Full-stack creatives are the future

## Principles

### 1. The Video Team
```
Video Producer (Full-Stack)
  │
  ├─ 3 Long-Form Editors
  │   └─ 2 editing main channel
  │   └─ 1 editing second channel
  │
  └─ 2 Short-Form Editors
      └─ Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok
```

### 2. The Written Team
```
Head of Marketing
  │
  ├─ LinkedIn Manager
  │
  ├─ Newsletter Writer (2 newsletters/week)
  │
  ├─ Copywriter (Email sequences, landing pages)
  │
  └─ Social Writer (Tweets, captions, carousels)
```

### 3. The First Hire
- Pen-first: Social Manager
- Video-first: Video Editor
- Look for taste, reliability, alignment
- A players hang with A players

## Process

### Team Building Workflow:
```
1. Define your first hire
2. Use your network (A players know A players)
3. Have them complete a challenge
4. Document all processes
5. Train them on standards
6. Expand team gradually
7. Maintain quality bar
```

## Decision Framework

```
Who Should I Hire First?
  │
  ├─ Am I video-first or pen-first?
  │   ├─ Video-first → Video editor
  │   └─ Pen-first → Social manager
  │
  ├─ What do I hate doing most?
  │   └─ Hire someone to do it
  │
  ├─ What's the highest leverage role?
  │   └─ Hire for that
  │
  └─ What can I afford?
      └─ Start with what you can
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Hire A players
- Test with challenges
- Document standards
- Maintain quality bar

**Never:**
- Hire B players
- Skip testing
- Let standards slip
- Hire without clear role
