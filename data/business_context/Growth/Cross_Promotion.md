# Cross_Promotion.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for growing newsletters through partnerships and cross-promotion.

## Core Philosophy
**Partnerships are growth without spend.**

- Every newsletter has a complementary audience
- Cross-promotion is win-win
- Trust transfers between brands
- Network effects compound

## Principles

**1. Find Complementary (Not Competitive) Partners**
- Same audience, different angle
- Related topics, not identical
- Mutual benefit

**2. Leverage Automated Networks**
- Beehiiv's Recommendation Network
- Standard referral barter system
- Reciprocal recommendations

**3. Use Paid Boost Recommendations**
- Pay for subscriber acquisition
- Control CPA
- Verify quality

## Decision Tree

```
Which Cross-Promotion Strategy?
  |
  |- Am I just starting out?
  |   |- YES → Free recommendation barter
  |   |- NO → Continue
  |
  |- Do I have >5k subscribers?
  |   |- YES → Consider paid boost recommendations
  |   |- NO → Stick with free partnerships
  |
  |- Is my audience highly targeted?
  |   |- YES → Each subscriber is high value → Pay more
  |   |- NO → Keep CPA low
  |
  |- Do I have content budget?
      |- YES → Scale paid acquisition
      |- NO → Focus on organic partnerships
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Only recommend newsletters you genuinely like
- Vet partners thoroughly
- Track conversion rates
- Balance free and paid partnerships

**Never:**
- Compromise quality for quantity
- Recommend competitors
- Ignore data on what converts
- Partner with low-quality newsletters

## AI Instructions

When managing cross-promotion:
- Analyze conversion rates per partner
- Optimize recommendation placement
- Test different copy for each partner
- Monitor quality of acquired subscribers
- Automate partner onboarding
