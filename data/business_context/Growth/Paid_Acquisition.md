# Paid_Acquisition.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for scaling newsletter growth through paid channels.

## Core Philosophy
**Paid acquisition is fuel, not the engine.**

- Organic first, paid second
- Paid scales what organic validates
- Every dollar must return >$1
- LTV > CAC is the only rule

## Principles

**1. Start with Performance Marketing**
- Meta is most scalable
- Google works well
- YouTube has high intent
- Test small, then scale

**2. Focus on Efficient Economics**
```
LTV > 3x CAC
  |
  |- If LTV > 3x CAC → Scale
  |- If LTV > 2x CAC → Maintain
  |- If LTV < 2x CAC → Pause and optimize
```

**3. Don't Overpay**
- CAC should be $2-5 per subscriber
- Quality matters more than quantity
- Engaged subscribers > Cheap subscribers
- Open rate is the key metric

**4. Meta Spend Strategy**
- Meta is still the king
- Spend meta until the next best dollar is better spent elsewhere
- Figure out the perfect level of spend on every channel
- It always changes (messy mix)

**5. Creative Volume**
- $100K+ daily spend = Hundreds of ads per week
- A banger ad taps out at $100K
- Need a banger every day
- 50 ads a day minimum
- Radically different creative needed

**6. Partner Ads**
- Partner ads are working better for everyone
- Single handle helps
- Meta is prioritizing partner ads
- Two handles = Better signal
- AI algorithm prefers it

## Benchmark Table

| Channel | Cost/Subscriber | Open Rate Target | Best For |
|---------|----------------|------------------|----------|
| Meta Ads | $2-5 | 40%+ | Broad audiences |
| Google Ads | $1-3 | 40%+ | High intent search |
| YouTube | $3-7 | 40%+ | Education/content |
| Boost Network | $2-3 | 40%+ | Targeted audiences |
| Partner Ads | $1-4 | 40%+ | Creator audiences |

## Decision Tree

```
Should I Scale Paid Acquisition?
  |
  |- Do I have organic validation?
  |   |- YES → Consider paid
  |   |- NO → Build organic first
  |
  |- Is LTV > 3x CAC?
  |   |- YES → Scale aggressively
  |   |- NO → Optimize or pause
  |
  |- Do I have budget to test?
  |   |- YES → Test with $3-5k/month
  |   |- NO → Focus organic
  |
  |- Is audience targeted enough?
  |   |- YES → Paid works well
  |   |- NO → Broad paid is waste
  |
  |- Are partner ads working?
  |   |- YES → Scale partner ads
  |   |- NO → Build roster
  |
  |- Is creative volume sufficient?
      |- YES → Good
      |- NO → Produce more
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Start small and test
- Track every dollar
- Know your LTV
- Monitor open rates
- Spend meta until next best dollar
- Use radically different creative
- Run partner ads

**Never:**
- Scale unprofitable channels
- Ignore engagement metrics
- Over-rely on paid
- Stop organic growth
- Just tweak existing creative
- Skip partner ads

## AI Instructions

When managing paid acquisition:
- Calculate true LTV
- Monitor CAC trends
- Optimize creative and copy
- Track quality of subscribers
- Automate reporting
