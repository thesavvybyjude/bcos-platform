# Lead_Magnets.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for creating and using lead magnets to grow your newsletter.

## Core Philosophy
**A lead magnet is a promise delivered.**

- Give value before asking for anything
- Make it specific and actionable
- Align with your audience's deepest need
- Build trust through immediate delivery

## Principles

**1. Create High-Perceived-Value, Low-Cost Magnets**
- Repurpose existing content
- Compile your best insights
- Create templates/checklists
- Share what you already know

**2. Target Specific Pain Points**
- "How to raise capital" → Founders
- "How to grow email" → Newsletter operators
- "How to hire" → Team builders
- "How to scale" → Business owners

**3. Multiple Lead Magnets for Different Audiences**
- Series A deck → Early-stage founders
- Series B deck → Later-stage founders
- Remote playbook → Remote teams
- Growth playbook → Newsletter creators

**4. Lead Magnet Strategy**
- Content creates curiosity
- Lead magnet captures
- Email is the only channel not fully taken over by algorithms
- Lead magnets are the rails of the funnel

**5. Audience Matching**
- Match lead magnet to audience stage
- Cold audience → High-value freebie
- Warm audience → Webinar invite
- Hot audience → Direct offer
- Different audiences need different magnets

## Decision Framework

```
What Lead Magnet Should I Create?
  |
  |- What do people ask me most?
  |   |- Collect common questions
  |   |- Turn answers into guides
  |   |- Package as lead magnet
  |
  |- What unique information do I have?
  |   |- Pitch decks
  |   |- Playbooks
  |   |- Templates
  |   |- Frameworks
  |
  |- What would I pay for?
  |   |- Create that
  |   |- Give it away free
  |
  |- What cost me nothing to create?
  |   |- Existing presentations
  |   |- Recorded content
  |   |- Written notes
  |   |- Compiled tweets/threads
  |
  |- What stage is my audience?
      |- Cold → High-value freebie
      |- Warm → Webinar invite
      |- Hot → Direct offer
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Deliver immediate value
- Make it relevant to your audience
- Update magnets regularly
- Test different magnets
- Match magnet to audience stage
- Use content to create curiosity
- Capture email in exchange for value

**Never:**
- Create generic content
- Overpromise and underdeliver
- Gate low-value content
- Forget to track conversion
- Ignore audience stage
- Break the hypnosis with non-native ads

## AI Instructions

When creating lead magnets:
- Extract your best insights
- Package them as actionable guides
- Add your unique perspective
- Make it visually clean
- Include a clear CTA for next step
