# Cash_Flow_Management.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for cash flow management in bootstrapped D2C.

## Core Philosophy
**Cash is king. Know your cash flow.**

- It takes $10M in profit to make $1M yourself
- Growing businesses need cash reinvested
- Debt can be strategic
- Never raise if you can avoid it

## Principles

**1. The Profit Reality**
- Business can do $5M/year in profit
- You might only make $300K/year
- Takes $10M in profit to make $1M yourself
- Growing = Money goes back in

**2. Debt vs. Equity**
```
Equity:
  └─ Give up ownership
  └─ Dilute yourself
  └─ Investors have control

Debt:
  └─ Keep ownership
  └─ Pay back with interest
  └─ Secured by business
```

**3. Types of Debt**
- Revolver: Open line of credit
- Debt Recap: Take chips off the table
- Factoring: Get money vs. POs
- Different uses for each

**4. Debt Recap**
- Bank gives you money
- Secured by business (not personal)
- Take chips off the table
- Derisk your life

## Process

**Cash Flow Management:**
```
1. Track 13-week cash flow
2. Use debt for POs (factoring)
3. Use debt recap to take chips off table
4. Keep a revolver open
5. Never over-leverage
6. Know your burn
7. Be profitable
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Use Debt?
  │
  ├─ Do I have a PO from a blue-chip retailer?
  │   ├─ YES → Factoring (cheap debt)
  │   └─ NO → Harder
  │
  ├─ Do I need cash for inventory?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider debt
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Can I pay it back?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  └─ Is it a 1-3x EBITDA multiple?
      ├─ YES → Reasonable
      └─ NO → Too levered
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Track cash flow
- Use debt strategically
- Keep leverage low
- Be profitable

**Never:**
- Over-leverage
- Ignore cash flow
- Think equity is the only option
- Raise equity if you don't need to
