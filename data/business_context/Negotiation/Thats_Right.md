# Thats_Right.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for achieving the "That's right" breakthrough.

## Core Philosophy
**"That's right" is the most powerful signal in negotiation.**

- "You're right" = They're maintaining defenses
- "That's right" = They feel heard and understood
- "That's right" = They've adopted your perspective
- "You're right" = They still see it as your solution

## Principles

**1. The Difference**
```
"You're right":
  └─ They completely agree
  └─ But it's still YOUR solution
  └─ They haven't adopted it
  └─ There's still a problem
  └─ You won't get full compliance

"That's right":
  └─ They feel heard and understood
  └─ They've adopted the perspective
  └─ They feel respected
  └─ Clears the decks
  └─ Full agreement and compliance
```

**2. The Schilling Case**
- The sociopathic murderer wanted to be a hero
- "War damages" made him sound like a criminal
- "Ransom" made him sound like a criminal
- He wanted to be a freedom fighter
- We summarized everything he said
- Long silence
- "That's right"
- $10 million demand disappeared forever

**3. How to Get It**
- Summarize their position back to them
- Make them feel heard and understood
- Don't explain anything away
- Don't argue against their position
- Let the silence work

**4. The Neurochemical Effect**
- "That's right" triggers oxytocin
- Oxytocin is the truth drug
- It's also the bonding drug
- It takes about 6 seconds to work
- Don't interrupt the process

## Process

**Getting to "That's Right":**
```
1. Summarize the other side's position
2. List all their concerns
3. Don't defend against them
4. Let the silence hang
5. Wait for "That's right"
6. If you get "You're right" → You still have a problem
7. Don't stop until you get "That's right"
```

## Decision Framework

```
Did I Get "That's Right" or "You're Right"?
  │
  ├─ "That's right" → Success
  │   └─ They feel heard and understood
  │   └─ They've adopted the perspective
  │   └─ Full agreement is possible
  │
  └─ "You're right" → Still a problem
      ├─ They're maintaining defenses
      ├─ It's still your solution
      ├─ Full compliance won't happen
      └─ Need to summarize more
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Summarize their position back to them
- Don't defend or argue
- Wait for the response
- Let the silence work

**Never:**
- Accept "You're right" as final
- Explain anything away
- Interrupt the processing
- Think you've won until you get "That's right"
