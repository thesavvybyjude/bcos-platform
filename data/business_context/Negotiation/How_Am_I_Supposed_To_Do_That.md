# How_Am_I_Supposed_To_Do_That.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for using "How am I supposed to do that?" in negotiation.

## Core Philosophy
**"How am I supposed to do that?" forces a 180-degree turn 80% of the time.**

- It feels differential (not confrontational)
- It forces the other side to look at your situation
- It creates forced empathy
- It forces them to think through implementation

## Principles

**1. Why It Works**
```
1. It feels differential
   └─ What and how questions are not confrontational
   └─ The other side doesn't feel trapped

2. It creates forced empathy
   └─ They have to look at your situation
   └─ "My son Brandon always referred to it as forced empathy"

3. It makes them think about implementation
   └─ If it's impossible, they'll reconsider
   └─ If they feel backed into a corner, you get data
```

**2. The 80/20 Rule**
- 80% of the time → 180-degree shift
- They offer something much better
- They were playing "ask high, settle low"
- You just pulled them out of that game

**3. The 20% Exception**
- If they push back hard, they're backed into a corner
- They may be doing the best they can
- There may be a gun held to their head on something else
- Your deal might be their only savior
- This is valuable information

**4. If They Attack**
- Take a break
- Give everyone a few minutes to cool off
- Understand they're backed into a corner
- Look at the deal—it might be the best possible

## Process

**Using "How Am I Supposed To Do That?"**
```
1. Someone throws a price at you
2. Say: "How am I supposed to do that?"
3. Wait for response (silence)
4. 80% of time → They'll offer something better
5. 20% of time → They'll attack
6. If attack → Take a break
7. Use data from response
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Was Their Response?
  │
  ├─ 180-degree shift (80%)
  │   └─ They offered something better
  │   └─ Close or continue negotiation
  │
  └─ Attacked (20%)
      ├─ They're backed into a corner
      ├─ They're doing the best they can
      ├─ Your deal might be their only savior
      └─ Take a break, then reconsider
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Use it when someone throws an unreasonable price
- Wait for their response
- If they shift, take it
- If they attack, take a break

**Never:**
- Be surprised when it doesn't work
- Ignore the data from an attack
- Think they're not backed into a corner
- Forget to take a break
