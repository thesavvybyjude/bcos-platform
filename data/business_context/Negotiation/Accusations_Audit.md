# Accusations_Audit.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for deactivating negativity before it derails negotiation.

## Core Philosophy
**The accusations audit is the most powerful way to disarm the other side.**

- Call out the negatives before they do
- Deactivate their fear systems
- Inoculate against their accusations
- Playfulness makes it safe

## Principles

**1. What Is the Accusations Audit?**
- Listing the bad things they may be harboring about you
- Saying them out loud
- Starting with the biggest things first
- Letting them sink in

**2. Why It Works**
- You can't plant emotions
- You can nurture positives OR deactivate negatives
- Calling out a negative disarms it
- It inoculates from it

**3. How to Do It**
- "Look, this is going to break your heart"
- "When I get done saying this, you're going to want to leap across the table and stab me in the eye with a pencil"
- Go all in—don't pull your punches
- Be fearless

**4. The Steve Jobs Example**
- Jobs laid out all the negatives to Disney's CEO
- Eisner thought the deal was over
- Jobs said: "Sometimes the positives outweigh the negatives"
- Negatives were deactivated
- Positives took over on their own

## Process

**Accusations Audit:**
```
1. List everything they might be thinking about you
2. Start with the biggest things first
3. Say them out loud
4. Let them sink in
5. Be fearless
6. If they defend you, you've gone too far
7. If they say "Hold it, you're being too hard on yourself" → You're winning
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Do an Accusations Audit?
  │
  ├─ Is there negativity in the room?
  │   ├─ YES → Do the audit
  │   └─ NO → Maybe not needed
  │
  ├─ Is the other side defensive?
  │   ├─ YES → Do the audit
  │   └─ NO → Maybe not needed
  │
  ├─ Do I know what they think?
  │   ├─ YES → Audit it
  │   └─ NO → Find out first
  │
  └─ Am I being fearless?
      ├─ YES → Do it
      └─ NO → Get fearless
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Do the audit before they do
- Be fearless
- Start with the biggest accusations
- Let them sink in

**Never:**
- Pull your punches
- Be fearful
- Skip the biggest accusations
- Forget to deactivate negatives
