# What_Makes_You_Ask.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for uncovering the question behind the question.

## Core Philosophy
**"What makes you ask?" is the most powerful way to uncover the real issue.**

- Why creates accusations → "What" creates curiosity
- The question behind the question is the most important issue
- You can't find out if you ask "why"
- You can find out if you ask "what"

## Principles

**1. Why vs. What**
```
"Why did you do that?"
  └─ Implies they did something wrong
  └─ Triggers defense mechanisms
  └─ They feel accused

"What makes you ask?"
  └─ Genuine curiosity
  └─ They feel safe (no accusation)
  └─ They'll tell you the real reason
```

**2. The Security Clearance Story**
- Insurance company asked Chris about his security clearance
- He answered without asking why
- He didn't get the contract
- To this day, he doesn't know why they asked
- The lesson: Always ask what makes them ask

**3. The Deflection Concern**
- People worry it sounds deflective
- They haven't done it before
- It feels uncomfortable at first
- Four times and you'll see it works
- 64 times and it's a habit

**4. The Tone Matters**
- "What makes you ask?" (curious)
- "What makes you ask?" (defensive)
- The tone of voice changes everything
- Genuine curiosity is a superpower

## Process

**Uncovering the Real Question:**
```
1. Someone asks you a question
2. Instead of answering, say: "What makes you ask?"
3. Let them answer
4. Now you know the real issue
5. Answer the real question
6. You've now addressed what they actually care about
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Answer Directly or Ask Why?
  │
  ├─ Is this a sensitive question?
  │   ├─ YES → Ask what makes them ask
  │   └─ NO → Maybe answer directly
  │
  ├─ Do I need context to answer well?
  │   ├─ YES → Ask what makes them ask
  │   └─ NO → Answer directly
  │
  ├─ Am I genuinely curious?
  │   ├─ YES → Ask what makes them ask
  │   └─ NO → Maybe answer directly
  │
  └─ Is the other person tired (>1 PM)?
      ├─ YES → Use a label instead
      └─ NO → Use "What makes you ask?"
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Ask "What makes you ask?" before answering
- Be genuinely curious
- Use the right tone
- Answer the real question

**Never:**
- Ask "Why?"
- Answer without context
- Sound deflective
- Forget to be curious
