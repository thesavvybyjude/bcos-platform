# Have_You_Given_Up.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for the most powerful re-engagement tactic.

## Core Philosophy
**"Have you given up on X?" is the most powerful re-engagement line in negotiation.**

- It triggers safety through "no"
- It triggers loss aversion
- It brings things to top of mind
- It works in 80% of cases

## Principles

**1. Why It Works**
```
1. Saying NO makes people feel safe and protected
   └─ We hate to hear no, we love to say it
   └─ They feel they haven't committed to anything

2. Nobody wants to give up
   └─ Loss aversion is the most dominating factor in decision-making
   └─ The prospect of loss is more powerful than gain

3. Brings it to top of mind
   └─ Everyone's brain is a mess
   └─ Chaos is everywhere
   └─ Top-of-mind triggers action
```

**2. The Three Responses**
```
Response 1: Instant Deal (20-30%)
  └─ They were ready, just needed a push

Response 2: They've Moved On (20%)
  └─ You were being played
  └─ They were using you to drive down price
  └─ Now you know and can stop wasting time

Response 3: Need Nurturing (50-60%)
  └─ Close to deal, but fears are in the way
  └─ Need to feel heard and understood
  └─ Summarize their position back to them
  └─ Ask: "What's stopping us from making this deal?"
```

**3. When It Doesn't Work**
- Dead silence = The answer is yes
- They've given up, but are embarrassed
- They've lost the ability to make the deal
- Don't waste time trying to resurrect it
- Offer to meet as humans, not business people

## Process

**Using "Have You Given Up":**
```
1. Send ONLY that line (no extra text)
2. Use as subject line in email
3. Wait for response
4. If instant deal → Close
5. If they've moved on → Walk away
6. If they need nurturing → Summarize their position
7. Ask: "What's stopping us?"
8. If dead silence → They've given up, move on
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Response Did I Get?
  │
  ├─ Immediate deal → Close it
  │
  ├─ "No, I haven't given up" → Nurture
  │   ├─ Summarize their position
  │   └─ Ask what's stopping the deal
  │
  ├─ "Yes, I've given up" → They've moved on
  │   └─ Walk away
  │
  └─ Dead silence → Answer is yes
      └─ They've lost ability/embarrassed
      └─ Offer non-business meeting
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Send the line word-for-word
- Only the line, nothing else
- Use as subject line in email
- Wait for response

**Never:**
- Water it down
- Add extra text
- Send it without context (early)
- Think you know which bucket they'll fall into
