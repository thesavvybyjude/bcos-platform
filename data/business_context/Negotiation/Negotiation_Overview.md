# Negotiation_Overview.md

## Purpose
To provide a comprehensive framework for negotiation in business and life.

## Core Philosophy
**Negotiation is not about winning—it's about getting what you want without forcing it.**

- Every person is in 7-10 negotiations daily
- Most of them, you will lose
- The framework flips that
- Negotiation is a learned skill

## Principles

**1. The Three Types of Deals**
```
Deals You Should Make
  └─ Collaborative, win-win

Deals You Shouldn't Make
  └─ Take too long, too expensive

Deals You'll Never Make
  └─ About 7% of people are unreachable
  └─ Learn to recognize them
  └─ Move on quickly
```

**2. First Impressions vs. Last Impressions**
- First impression is second most important
- Last impression is more important
- Most people focus on first impressions
- The last thing you say or do is what they remember

**3. Listening > Articulation**
- Most negotiations break down over lack of listening, not lack of articulation
- Great negotiators are listeners
- Hearing ≠ Listening ≠ Comprehension
- Demonstrate your comprehension

**4. Genuineness Wins**
- Deception by omission is a time bomb
- Be genuine about your limitations
- Honesty builds trust
- Trust enables collaboration

## Process

**Negotiation Framework:**
```
1. Enter with tactical empathy
2. Use the right line for the situation
3. Employ silence
4. Listen for tells
5. Create safety through "no"
6. Bargain as a last resort
7. Leave a strong last impression
```

## Decision Framework

```
Is This a Deal Worth Making?
  │
  ├─ Is it collaborative or competitive?
  │   ├─ Collaborative → Pursue
  │   └─ Competitive → Be cautious
  │
  ├─ Will it take too long?
  │   ├─ YES → Walk away
  │   └─ NO → Continue
  │
  ├─ Is the other side reachable?
  │   ├─ YES → Proceed
  │   └─ NO → Move on
  │
  └─ Will it make you money?
      ├─ YES → Do it
      └─ NO → Walk away
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Listen more than you talk
- Demonstrate comprehension
- Be genuine
- Leave a strong last impression

**Never:**
- Deceive by omission
- Think articulation > listening
- Ignore the last impression
- Stay in a deal you should walk away from
