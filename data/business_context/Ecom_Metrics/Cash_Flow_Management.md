# Cash_Flow_Management.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for e-commerce cash flow management.

## Core Philosophy
**Cash flow is the ultimate metric. Everything else is a proxy.**

- Growing your bank account gives ultimate optionality
- E-commerce has terrible cash flow to EBITDA ratio
- The CEO's first tab should be a 13-week cash flow forecast
- Cash flow is managed, not just tracked

## Principles

### 1. The 13-Week Cash Flow Forecast
- First tab on your browser
- Watch your money grow
- If bank account doesn't grow, something is wrong
- Manage invoice timing to optimize cash flow

### 2. Cash Flow vs. EBITDA
- E-commerce: Terrible ratio
- Service businesses: 95% cash flow to EBITDA
- Why agencies have better exits (8-12x EBITDA)
- Why PE loves service businesses

### 3. Vendor as Lender
- Your suppliers can be your lenders
- Net terms = Free cash flow
- The goal: Negative cash conversion cycle
- Pay suppliers with customer money

## Process

### Cash Flow Management:
```
1. Build 13-week cash flow forecast
2. Review daily
3. Optimize payment timing (pay later, not earlier)
4. Negotiate better terms with suppliers
5. Manage inventory turns aggressively
6. Use liquidation to convert aging inventory to cash
```

## Decision Framework

```
How to Improve Cash Flow?
  │
  ├─ Vendor terms → Extend them
  │   ├─ Negotiate with suppliers
  │   ├─ Pay more for COGS in exchange for terms
  │   └─ Build long-term relationships
  │
  ├─ Inventory turns → Increase them
  │   ├─ Liquidate aging inventory
  │   ├─ Use consignment
  │   └─ Smaller POs, more frequent orders
  │
  ├─ Media timing → Extend terms
  │   ├─ Credit cards = 30-day float
  │   └─ Agency terms = 60-120 days
  │
  └─ Customer payments → Speed them up
      ├─ Pre-orders
      └─ Subscription (upfront payment)
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Review 13-week cash flow daily
- Negotiate better terms
- Liquidate aging inventory
- Pay suppliers with customer money

**Never:**
- Let cash flow be a mystery
- Pay suppliers early without reason
- Hold aging inventory
- Extend terms without negotiating
