# Metric_Pyramid.md

## Purpose
To provide the hierarchy of e-commerce metrics.

## Core Philosophy
**One metric is insufficient. The pyramid gives you the full picture.**

- Contribution Margin is at the top (the scoreboard)
- Business metrics are next (revenue, spend, AOV)
- Customer metrics are next (new customer acquisition)
- Channel metrics are at the bottom (least important)

## Principles

### 1. The Pyramid
```
Level 1: Cash Flow (CEO view)
  │   └─ 13-week cash flow forecast
  │
Level 2: Contribution Margin (Team view)
  │   └─ Net sales - product cost - variable expense - ad spend
  │
Level 3: Business Metrics
  │   ├─ Revenue
  │   ├─ Spend
  │   ├─ MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)
  │   └─ AOV
  │
Level 4: Customer Metrics
  │   ├─ New customer acquisition efficiency
  │   ├─ New customer contribution margin
  │   ├─ New customer revenue
  │   └─ Ad spend
  │
Level 5: Channel Metrics
  │   ├─ Facebook ROAS
  │   ├─ Google ROAS
  │   └─ Platform-level metrics
```

### 2. Channel Metrics Are a Proxy
- Not tightly correlated to cash flow
- Governs most organizations' behavior
- Founders wonder why they have no cash flow
- The answer: You're optimizing for the wrong thing

### 3. Transparency Is Power
- Everyone should see contribution margin daily
- If you want people to affect what you care about, show it
- You can't ask people to affect things they aren't looking at

### 4. Active Customer File Is Critical
- New customer acquisition today = Returning customer tomorrow
- Best predictor of future returning revenue
- 80% of customers who will come back do so in first 6-8 months
- Track active customers, not total customer count

## Process

### Metric Pyramid Implementation:
```
1. CEO: 13-week cash flow forecast (first tab, every day)
2. Team: Daily contribution margin view
3. Business: Weekly revenue, spend, MER, AOV
4. Marketing: New customer metrics daily
5. Channel: Platform metrics (but don't optimize solely on them)
6. Review the pyramid weekly
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Metric Should I Optimize For?
  │
  ├─ CEO → Cash flow (13-week forecast)
  ├─ Team → Contribution margin (daily)
  ├─ Marketing → New customer acquisition
  ├─ Channels → Platform metrics (secondary)
  └─ Never → Platform metrics alone
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Track the full pyramid
- Show contribution margin to the team
- Have a 13-week cash flow forecast
- Track active customer file

**Never:**
- Optimize solely on channel metrics
- Hide financials from the team
- Forget new customer acquisition
- Let contribution margin slip
