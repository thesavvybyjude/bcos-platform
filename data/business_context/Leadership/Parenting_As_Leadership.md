# Parenting As Leadership

## Purpose
To provide a framework for applying leadership principles to parenting.

## Core Philosophy
**Your job as a parent is not to tell your kids what to do -- it is to help them think.**

- The same principles that work in business work in parenting
- Help them think for themselves
- Demonstrate collaboration
- Do not order them around -- guide them

## Principles

### 1. Help Them Think
- Do not just tell them what to do
- Help them understand the why
- Ask questions that make them think
- They will make better decisions if they understand the reasoning

### 2. The Collaboration Model
- Show them what collaboration looks like
- Demonstrate listening
- They learn from watching you
- Be the example

### 3. Keep Them Safe
- But also let them make mistakes
- The goal is to protect while allowing growth
- Overprotection hinders development
- Give them room to learn

### 4. Teach Problem-Solving
- Instead of solving problems for them, show them how to solve them
- Ask guiding questions
- Let them figure it out
- They will be better for it

## Decision Framework

```
Am I Helping My Kids Think?
  |
  +-- Am I telling them what to do or helping them think?
  |   +-- Telling -> Need to shift
  |   +-- Helping -> Good
  |
  +-- Am I modeling collaboration?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need to show it
  |
  +-- Am I protecting or overprotecting?
  |   +-- Protecting -> Good
  |   +-- Overprotecting -> Need to let go
  |
  +-- Am I teaching problem-solving?
      +-- YES -> Good
      +-- NO -> Need to guide
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Help them think, not just obey
- Model good behavior
- Teach problem-solving
- Be the example

### Never:
- Order them around
- Solve everything for them
- Overprotect
- Forget you are the model
