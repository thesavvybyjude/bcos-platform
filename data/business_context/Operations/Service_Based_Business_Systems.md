# Service-Based Business Systems

## Purpose
To provide a comprehensive, prioritized framework for systematizing a service-based business—from client delivery and sales to marketing and traffic—so founders can remove themselves as the bottleneck and scale sustainably.

## Core Philosophy

> "Start closest to the cash and work your way outward."

> "I remember being on my first vacation where I didn't bring my laptop and we had a sale come through—someone was able to enroll a new client while I was away."

## Principles

### 1. Build from the Inside Out
- Start with the systems closest to the transaction (delivery, onboarding, sales)
- Work outward toward marketing and traffic
- This ensures you have a solid foundation before you drive more leads

### 2. Closest to the Cash
- The delivery and sales systems are where money changes hands
- Get these right first before investing heavily in marketing and traffic
- This prevents the "leaky bucket" problem

### 3. The Six Systems of a Service Business
- Client Delivery (Onboarding + Delivery/Support)
- Sales
- Marketing / Lead Capture
- Retention, Resell & Upsell
- Traffic (Organic, Paid, Partner)

### 4. Systematize Before You Scale
- It may take 3-4 months to build systems
- It takes another 3-12 months to optimize them
- Don't hire a salesperson until you've tested and documented the process yourself

### 5. Onboarding is a System, Not an Event
- Every client should have the exact same onboarding experience
- Set expectations, show how you deliver, explain timelines
- The first 100 days are critical (reference: "Never Lose a Customer Again")

### 6. The Consultative Close
- A formula for sales conversations
- Walk clients through: biggest pain point → opportunity → how you can help
- Every call follows the same conversational flow

### 7. Delivery & Support Structure
- Clients need to know how to get help
- What frequency? What platforms? What cadence?
- All of this should be structured and standardized

### 8. Traffic Priority Order
- Organic (LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram)
- Paid (retargeting → warm → cold)
- Partner (other vendors, agencies with crossover audiences)

## Decision Frameworks

### The Six Systems Framework (Prioritized)

```
SYSTEM 1: CLIENT DELIVERY (Closest to Cash)
    │
    ├── Onboarding Experience
    │   ├── Repeatable process for every new client
    │   ├── Set expectations
    │   ├── Show how to be successful
    │   ├── Where to get help
    │   └── Implementation pathway (first 100 days)
    │
    └── Delivery & Support
        ├── How clients get help
        ├── Frequency of communication
        ├── Platforms used
        └── Cadence of check-ins

SYSTEM 2: SALES
    │
    ├── Consultative Close formula
    ├── Script and flow documented
    ├── Presentation/Assets
    └── Every call follows the same process

SYSTEM 3: MARKETING / LEAD CAPTURE
    │
    ├── Capture name, email, phone
    ├── Lead magnets (PDFs, webinars, video sales letters)
    ├── Email sequences
    ├── Thank you pages
    └── Invite to sales conversation

SYSTEM 4: RETENTION, RESELL & UPSELL
    │
    ├── Resell: Ongoing support or retainer
    ├── Upsell: Higher ticket items
    ├── Retention: Longer-term commitments
    └── Repeatable process for each

SYSTEM 5: TRAFFIC (Organic First)
    │
    └── Organic: LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram

SYSTEM 6: TRAFFIC (Paid Second)
    │
    └── Retargeting → Warm → Cold (Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn ads)

SYSTEM 7: TRAFFIC (Partner Third)
    │
    └── Other vendors, agencies with crossover audiences
```

### The "Closest to Cash" Prioritization Framework

```
START HERE (Closest to Cash):
    │
    ├── Client Delivery (Onboarding + Delivery/Support)
    │   └── This is where you serve existing clients
    │
    ├── Sales
    │   └── This is where new clients say "yes"
    │
    └── Marketing / Lead Capture
        └── This is where prospects become leads
    │
    ▼
THEN EXPAND OUTWARD:
    │
    ├── Retention, Resell & Upsell
    │   └── Maximize existing client value
    │
    └── Traffic
        └── Organic → Paid → Partner

WHY THIS ORDER:
    │
    └── If you can't deliver and sell well, more traffic won't help
    └── Fix the leaky bucket before you pour more in
```

### The Sales System Framework (Consultative Close)

```
STEP 1: DISCOVERY
    │
    ├── Understand the client's biggest pain point
    ├── Ask questions that reveal the problem
    └── Validate the opportunity

STEP 2: OPPORTUNITY
    │
    ├── Show them what's possible
    ├── Paint the picture of the solution
    └── Demonstrate the value

STEP 3: SOLUTION
    │
    ├── Present your core offer
    ├── Show how you can help
    └── Walk them through the process

STEP 4: CLOSE
    │
    ├── Present pricing
    ├ Handle objections
    └── Enroll the client

KEY: Every sales call follows the exact same conversational flow.
```

### The Onboarding System Framework

```
ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE (Repeatable):
    │
    ├── Welcome call / kickoff
    ├── Set expectations (timelines, deliverables)
    ├── Show how to be successful
    ├── Explain where to get help
    ├── Walk through implementation pathway
    └── First 100 days (critical period)

WHY IT WORKS:
    │
    └── Every client gets the same experience
    └── You can do it in less time with less energy
    └── More clients can be onboarded in any given month

REFERENCE: "Never Lose a Customer Again" by Joey Coleman
```

### Delivery & Support System Framework

```
DELIVERY & SUPPORT:
    │
    ├── Structure: How do they get help?
    ├── Platforms: Where do they go for help?
    ├── Frequency: How often do you communicate?
    ├── Cadence: What's the schedule?
    └── Escalation: What does an emergency look like?

GOAL:
    │
    └── Every single day, you and your team know where to look for client needs
    └── Expectations are set upfront
```

### Traffic Priority Framework

```
TRAFFIC TIER 1: ORGANIC
    │
    ├── LinkedIn posts
    ├── YouTube videos
    ├── Instagram content
    ├── Warm outreach to engagers
    └── Create a system for content creation

TRAFFIC TIER 2: PAID
    │
    ├── Retargeting (people who already engaged)
    ├── Warm targeting (lookalikes, interests)
    ├── Cold targeting (broad)
    └── Platforms: Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn

TRAFFIC TIER 3: PARTNER
    │
    ├── Other vendors
    ├── Agencies with crossover audiences
    └── Non-competing service providers

PRIORITY: Organic → Paid → Partner
```

### The Lead Capture System Framework

```
LEAD CAPTURE MECHANISMS:
    │
    ├── Video Sales Letter (VSL)
    ├── Live Webinars
    ├── Free Facebook Groups
    ├── Downloadable White Papers / PDFs
    └── Lead Magnets

AUTOMATION:
    │
    ├── Capture: Name, email, phone
    ├── Nurturing: Email sequences
    ├── Invitation: To a sales conversation
    └── Thank You Pages: Move to next step

GOAL:
    └── Move leads into a sales conversation
    └── (Consultation or sales call)
```

### The Three R's Framework

```
RETENTION:
    │
    ├── Keep existing clients longer
    ├── Deliver consistent value
    ├── Set expectations upfront
    └── First 100 days are critical

RESELL:
    │
    ├── Sell them again (ongoing support, retainer)
    ├── Same client, same service, longer term
    └── Repeatable process

UPSELL:
    │
    ├── Sell them a higher ticket item
    ├── Move them to a premium service
    ├── Same client, higher value
    └── Repeatable process

GOAL:
    ── Every system should be documented and repeatable
    ── Not custom for each client
```

### The Founder Bottleneck Test

```
ASK YOURSELF:
    │
    ├── Can I take a week off without things falling apart?
    ├── Can a new client be onboarded without me?
    ├── Can a sale happen without me?
    └── Can a client get help without me?

If any answer is "No," you have a system gap.
Start building the closest-to-cash system first.
```

## Rules

### DO:
- Always start closest to the cash—delivery and sales before marketing
- Always systematize before you scale—test and document first
- Always make onboarding repeatable—every client gets the same experience
- Always document your sales process—script, flow, presentation
- Always structure delivery & support—clients need to know how to get help
- Always prioritize organic before paid—content first, ads second
- Always build 3-4 months, then optimize 3-12 months

### DON'T:
- Never hire a salesperson until you've tested the process yourself
- Never build marketing systems before delivery systems
- Never make onboarding a custom project for each client
- Never skip the "closest to cash" prioritization

---

## Heuristics

- If you can't onboard a new client without ad-hoc work, you don't have an onboarding system
- If every sales call is different, you don't have a sales system
- If you can't take a vacation without things falling apart, you don't have systems
- The first 100 days are critical for client retention
- Build 3-4 months, optimize 3-12 months
- If you can't deliver well, you can't keep clients
- If you can't sell well, you can't get clients

## Triggers

### When You Can't Take Time Off
→ You're the bottleneck in your business
→ Ask: "Which system is most broken?"
→ Start closest to the cash (delivery or sales)
→ Build that system first
→ Then work outward
→ Result: Someone else can handle it while you're away

### When Every Client Experience is Different
→ Every new client feels like a custom project
→ Build an onboarding system
→ Create a repeatable process
→ Every client gets the same experience
→ You can onboard more clients in less time
→ Reference: "Never Lose a Customer Again" framework

### When You're Ready to Hire a Salesperson
→ FIRST: Document your sales process
→ Create a script, flow, and presentation
→ Battle-test it yourself
→ THEN: Hand it off to a salesperson
→ Result: Training is infinitely easier

### When You Want to Scale Traffic
→ Ask: "Is my delivery system solid?"
→ Ask: "Is my sales system documented?"
→ Ask: "Is my onboarding repeatable?"
→ If any answer is "No," fix those first
→ Then scale traffic (organic → paid → partner)

## Failure Modes

### Common Mistakes

1. **Building systems in the wrong order**
   - Marketing before delivery
   - Traffic before sales
   - The leaky bucket problem

2. **Making it too complex**
   - Three sales systems, three onboarding processes
   - Start with one, optimize, then add more

3. **Not documenting the process**
   - Everything lives in the founder's head
   - Can't hand off to anyone else

4. **Hiring before systematizing**
   - Salesperson with no sales system
   - Team member with no onboarding process

5. **Not optimizing**
   - Build it and forget it
   - Systems need 3-12 months of iteration

## Alex Hormozi — The First Business Playbook for Service Businesses

### The $100M Offers Framework (Applied to Service Businesses)

```
STEP 1: DREAM OUTCOME
└── What does the client ultimately want?

STEP 2: LIST ALL PROBLEMS
└── What prevents them from getting it?

STEP 3: LIST ALL OBSTACLES
└── What gets in the way?

STEP 4: CREATE SOLUTIONS
└── For each problem and obstacle

STEP 5: BUNDLE INTO MECHANISM
└── A unique system or process

STEP 6: ADD GUARANTEE
└── Reduce risk to zero

STEP 7: ADD BONUSES FOR SPEED
└── Incentivize fast action

STEP 8: ADD SWEETENER
└── Make it irresistible to say no

RESULT: A "Grand Slam Offer" that's 10x value for 10x price
```

### The Skilled vs. Unskilled Path

- If you have a skill: sell the skill directly (consulting, freelancing)
- If you don't have a skill: sell appointments (book appointments for others)
- Both paths work; the skilled path is faster

### The First Hire for Service Businesses

- Hire for fulfillment first (the thing that makes you money)
- Hire someone who's already done it before
- Don't hire for potential—hire for proof
- Pay them 20-30% of what you charge the client
- The margin is the business

### The Pricing Framework for Service Businesses

- People equate price with value
- If you charge $500, they expect $500 of value
- If you charge $5,000, they expect $5,000 of value
- The higher the price, the more they respect you
- "If you're not embarrassed by your first offer, you launched too late"

### The Volume Framework for Service Businesses

- Volume negates luck
- Post more, DM more, call more, meet more
- Reputation is built by doing, not by waiting
- "I was willing to do the thing nobody else wanted to do"
