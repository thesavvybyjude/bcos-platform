# Accountability_Systems.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building accountability in remote organizations.

## Core Philosophy
**Accountability is a system, not a personality trait.**

- Weekly goals drive progress
- Public accountability accelerates action
- Systems > Willpower
- Tracking prevents drift

## Principles

**1. Weekly Success Lists**
- Each team contributes 1-2 items
- 15-20 items total
- Clear ownership
- Friday review

**2. Top Priority Per Person**
- Every employee submits top priority
- Sunday deadline
- Monday review
- Friday accountability

**3. All-Hands Accountability**
- 2 all-hands per week
- Random check-ins
- Public accountability
- Cultural expectation

## Process

**Accountability System Flow:**
```
Sunday:
  1. CEO sends weekly update
  2. Every employee submits top priority
  3. Team leads submit weekly success list

Monday:
  1. All-hands
  2. Review weekly success list
  3. Confirm priorities

Daily:
  1. Work on priorities
  2. Communicate blockers

Friday:
  1. All-hands
  2. Random check-ins
  3. Did you hit your goal?
  4. Celebrate wins
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Set ambitious goals
- Make goals public
- Check progress regularly
- Celebrate wins

**Never:**
- Penalize ambitious goals
- Ignore accountability
- Make goals optional
- Skip reviews
