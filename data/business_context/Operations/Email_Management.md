# Email_Management.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for email management using AI.

## Core Philosophy
**Your inbox is a torture chamber. Get out of it.**

- Email creates emotional highs and lows
- Your presence isn't in your inbox
- Delegate email to AI or humans
- Focus on what makes you different

## Principles

### 1. The Email Emotion Cycle
```
Morning: New deal → Hero
Hour later: Customer quit → Loser
  → All in your inbox
  → 17 emotional shifts daily
```

### 2. Zero Inbox > Inbox Zero
- Don't just clear emails
- Don't read them at all
- Delegate to someone else
- Zero is the goal

### 3. AI Email Processing
```
1. Connect Claude to Gmail
2. "Process my unread emails"
3. AI ranks by priority
4. AI writes draft responses
5. AI creates spreadsheet
6. You approve/ignore
```

## Process

### AI Email Management:
```
1. Connect Claude browser extension to Gmail
2. Ask: "Process my unread emails"
3. AI scans all threads
4. AI creates priority spreadsheet
5. AI writes response drafts
6. Review and send/ignore
7. Your assistant handles the rest
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Delegate Email?
  │
  ├─ Do I spend >1 hour/day on email?
  │   ├─ YES → Delegate or automate
  │   └─ NO → Manage yourself
  │
  ├─ Does email affect my mood?
  │   ├─ YES → Delegate
  │   └─ NO → Manage yourself
  │
  ├─ Do I have someone to delegate to?
  │   ├─ YES → Delegate to human
  │   └─ NO → Use AI
  │
  └─ What's more valuable?
      ├─ My presence → Delegate
      └─ Email response → Manage yourself
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Delegate email to AI or humans
- Don't read your own email
- Focus on what you're uniquely good at
- Use AI for drafts

**Never:**
- Let email control your emotions
- Spend 3+ hours on email
- Think you need to respond personally
- Underestimate AI's email capability
