# First Business Playbook

## Purpose
To provide a complete, practical playbook for launching and scaling a first business—from getting clients, to building reputation, to hiring, to systems—based on Alex Hormozi's $100M Offers framework and real-world experience building multiple service businesses from scratch.

## Core Philosophy

> "I was willing to do the thing that nobody else wanted to do, and that's how I won."

> "Volume negates luck."

> "If you're not embarrassed by your first offer, you launched too late."

## Principles

### 1. The100M Offers Framework
- Give people a result they want in a way they haven't gotten it before
- Make the offer so good people feel stupid saying no
- The goal is to create a "Grand Slam Offer"—10x value for 10x price

### 2. The Value Equation
- Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement / Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
- Increase the top (dream outcome, likelihood), decrease the bottom (time, effort)
- The more desirable the outcome and the more likely they are to get it, the more they'll pay

### 3. The First Business Steps (Service Business)
- Pick one skill you're good at
- Go B2B (sell to businesses, not consumers)
- Sell a result, not a service
- Get clients cold (DMs, calls, door-to-door)
- Charge more than you're comfortable with
- Get a testimonial (even if you have to do it for free)
- Post on social media daily
- Hire someone to do the fulfillment
- Reinvest everything back into the business

### 4. The Skilled vs. Unskilled Path
- If you have a skill: sell the skill directly (consulting, freelancing)
- If you don't have a skill: sell appointments (book appointments for others)
- Both paths work; the skilled path is faster

### 5. Getting Clients Without a Reputation
- Cold DMs: "Hey, I noticed [X]. I can help with [Y]. Want to jump on a call?"
- Cold calls: Call businesses, offer free value first
- Door-to-door: Walk into businesses, offer to help
- Free work: Do it for free for one client, get a testimonial, then charge
- Referrals: Ask every client for 3 referrals

### 6. Pricing: Charge More Than You Think
- People equate price with value
- If you charge $500, they expect $500 of value
- If you charge $5,000, they expect $5,000 of value
- The higher the price, the more they respect you
- "If you're not embarrassed by your first offer, you launched too late"

### 7. The First Hire
- Hire for fulfillment first (the thing that makes you money)
- Hire someone who's already done it before
- Don't hire for potential—hire for proof
- Pay them 20-30% of what you charge the client
- The margin is the business

### 8. Social Media is Free Marketing
- Post 3x a day across all platforms
- Use the same content everywhere (repurpose)
- Talk about problems you solve
- Show proof of results
- "The more you talk about the problem, the more people find you"

### 9. Building Reputation Through Volume
- Volume negates luck
- Post more, DM more, call more, meet more
- Reputation is built by doing, not by waiting
- "I was willing to do the thing nobody else wanted to do"

### 10. The Three Paths to Scale
- More clients (acquire more)
- Higher price (charge more)
- Better fulfillment (deliver more value)
- Do all three simultaneously

### 11. The $100M Offers Framework
- Identify the dream outcome
- List all problems that prevent the outcome
- List all obstacles on the way to the outcome
- Create solutions for each problem and obstacle
- Bundle solutions into a mechanism
- Add a guarantee
- Add bonuses for speed
- Add a sweetener to make it irresistible

### 12. The Lead Magnet → Appointment → Client Pipeline
- Create a lead magnet (free value)
- Drive traffic to it (organic + paid)
- Capture name, email, phone
- Book a call (sales conversation)
- Close the client
- Deliver the result
- Get referrals and testimonials
- Repeat

## Decision Frameworks

### The Grand Slam Offer Framework

```
STEP 1: DREAM OUTCOME
└── What does the client ultimately want?

STEP 2: LIST ALL PROBLEMS
└── What prevents them from getting it?

STEP 3: LIST ALL OBSTACLES
└── What gets in the way?

STEP 4: CREATE SOLUTIONS
└── For each problem and obstacle

STEP 5: BUNDLE INTO MECHANISM
└── A unique system or process

STEP 6: ADD GUARANTEE
└── Reduce risk to zero

STEP 7: ADD BONUSES FOR SPEED
└── Incentivize fast action

STEP 8: ADD SWEETENER
└── Make it irresistible to say no

RESULT: A "Grand Slam Offer" that's 10x value for 10x price
```

### The Value Equation Framework

```
VALUE = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) / (Time × Effort)

INCREASE THE TOP:
├── Bigger dream outcome
├── Higher likelihood of achievement
└── More proof and testimonials

DECREASE THE BOTTOM:
├── Faster time to result
├── Less effort required
└── Fewer obstacles

EXAMPLE:
├── $500 service: "I'll help you lose 5 lbs"
├── $5,000 service: "I'll help you lose 20 lbs in 8 weeks with a done-for-you plan"
└── $50,000 service: "I'll help you lose 50 lbs in 12 weeks with a team of experts, meal plans, and weekly coaching"
```

### The Hiring Framework

```
FIRST HIRE: FULFILLMENT
├── Who does the work that makes you money?
├── Hire someone who's done it before
├── Pay 20-30% of what you charge the client
└── The margin is the business

SECOND HIRE: SALES
├── Who closes more clients?
├── Pay commission only (performance-based)
├── Train them on your process
└── They only succeed if you succeed

THIRD HIRE: OPERATIONS
├── Who runs the day-to-day?
├── Hire someone who can manage people
├── You focus on growth
└── They focus on execution

RULE:
└── Don't hire for potential
└── Hire for proof
```

## Triggers

### When You're Starting from Zero:

```
START HERE:
├── Pick one skill you're good at
├── Go B2B (sell to businesses)
├── Sell a result, not a service
├── Get clients cold (DMs, calls, door-to-door)
├── Charge more than you're comfortable with
├── Get a testimonial (even for free)
├── Post on social media daily
├── Hire someone to do the fulfillment
└── Reinvest everything back into the business

VOLUME NEGATES LUCK:
└── The more you do, the more you learn, the better you get
```

### When You're Not Getting Clients:

```
DIAGNOSIS:
├── Are you talking to enough people? (Volume)
├── Is your offer clear? (Value)
├── Are you charging enough? (Price)
├── Do you have proof? (Testimonials)
└── Are you posting? (Visibility)

FIX:
├── More DMs, more calls, more outreach
├── Clarify the dream outcome
├── Charge 10x more
├── Do it for free for one client
└── Post 3x a day
```

### When You're Ready to Hire:

```
FIRST HIRE CHECKLIST:
├── Is fulfillment taking all your time?
├── Can you afford to pay 20-30% of revenue?
├── Have you done the work yourself first?
├── Do you have a process to hand off?
└── Is the revenue consistent enough?

IF YES: Hire for fulfillment
IF NO: Keep doing it yourself until you can
```
