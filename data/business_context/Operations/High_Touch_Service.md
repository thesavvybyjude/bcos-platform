# High_Touch_Service.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for high-touch customer service.

## Core Philosophy
**Service is the differentiator.**

- Product is table stakes
- Service creates loyalty
- Attention to detail wins
- White glove matters

## Principles

**1. Create an X Team**
- Dedicated VIP service
- Concierge for top guests
- Hand-holding at every level
- Each venue needs its own

**2. Build a Rolodex Program**
- VIP database
- Preferences recorded
- Recognition at every visit
- Personal service at scale

**3. Scale Relationships Through Systems**
- CRM for VIPs
- Notes on preferences
- Shared across venues
- Consistent experience

## The X Team

**Role:** Extend the founder/owner's presence
**Characteristics:**
- Genuinely like people (non-negotiable)
- High emotional intelligence
- Detail-oriented
- Discreet

**Responsibilities:**
- VIP guest management
- Preference tracking
- Concierge services
- Brand representation

## VIP Preference Tracking

**What to Track:**
- Preferred greeting style
- Hover vs. privacy preference
- Favorite tables
- Drink preferences
- Dietary restrictions
- Special occasions
- Guest preferences

**How to Use:**
- Greet appropriately
- Set up table in advance
- Have favorites ready
- Recognize special occasions
- Maintain discretion

## Decision Framework

```
What Level of Service?
  |
  |- Is this a VIP?
  |   |- YES → X Team service
  |   |- NO → Standard service
  |
  |- Have they visited before?
  |   |- YES → Access preferences
  |   |- NO → Learn preferences
  |
  |- Are they celebrating something?
  |   |- YES → Special treatment
  |   |- NO → Standard experience
  |
  |- What's their preference?
      |- Hover → High attention
      |- Privacy → Minimal interaction
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Track VIP preferences
- Provide consistent service
- Scale through systems
- Hire people who like people

**Never:**
- Forget a preference
- Overlook a VIP
- Be inconsistent
- Ignore the details
