# Exit_Frameworks.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for exiting a D2C brand.

## Core Philosophy
**Get to $1-5M in personal net worth first. Everything else is secondary.**

- Life-changing money comes first
- European brands hold forever (LVMH model)
- Private equity is the most common buyer
- Strategic buyers are rare

## Principles

**1. The $1-5M Rule**
- Get to $1-5M in personal net worth first
- Then you can't be taken off the table
- Stress goes way down
- Life gets much easier

**2. European Brands > American Brands**
- Europeans hold brands generationally
- LVMH portfolio model
- Brands can't be hot forever
- Rotate through portfolio

**3. Time Kills All Deals**
- If you start talking on Jan 1, close in October
- They want to see you hit predictions
- They want to make sure numbers aren't inflated
- Due diligence takes forever

**4. Strategic Acquirers Are Rare**
- Hard to go from privately held directly to strategic
- Usually need a private equity stop
- Dr. Squatch: Unilever (strategic) but had Summit Capital first
- The typical path

## Process

**Exit Planning:**
```
1. Get to $1-5M personal net worth
2. Build a sustainable business (15%+ EBITDA)
3. Grow 20%+ year over year
4. Show meat on the bone
5. Sell to private equity (3-5x EBITDA)
6. Or hold forever (LVMH model)
7. Don't get greedy
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Sell?
  │
  ├─ Will it change your life?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider selling
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Is it a good multiple?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Wait
  │
  ├─ Do you love running it?
  │   ├─ YES → Hold
  │   └─ NO → Consider selling
  │
  └─ Is there meat on the bone?
      ├─ YES → Sell with upside
      └─ NO → Hold
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Get life-changing money
- Build a sustainable business
- Show meat on the bone
- Don't be greedy

**Never:**
- Sell for less than life-changing
- Get greedy
- Sell to the wrong buyer
- Think strategic is the only path
