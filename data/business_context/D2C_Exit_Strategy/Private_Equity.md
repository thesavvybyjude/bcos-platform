# Private_Equity.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for understanding private equity in D2C.

## Core Philosophy
**Private equity cares about return on invested capital, not your brand.**

- PE buys with debt (leverage buyouts)
- They want 20% annual returns
- Debt can be 6-7x EBITDA (too high)
- PE can make money even if brand goes bankrupt

## Principles

**1. Leverage Buyouts (LBO)**
```
Company: $100M value, $20M EBITDA (5x multiple)
  │
  ├─ PE puts in $50M
  ├─ Bank loans $50M (secured by company)
  ├─ PE owns company with only $50M invested
  └─ Goal: Double money in 4-5 years
```

**2. The Debt Recaps**
- Year 2: Refinance, take $50M off table
- Year 4: Another recap, take another $50M
- PE has doubled their money
- They don't care if brand goes bankrupt

**3. The Reality**
- PE's goal: Return on invested capital
- Not: Run good brands
- Not: Serve customers
- Not: Make a beautiful happy ending

**4. Too Much Debt**
- 6-7 turns of EBITDA = Very bad
- 2-3 turns = Conservative
- Levered up too high = Bankruptcy risk
- This is why brands blow up

## Process

**PE Exit Process:**
```
1. PE values your company
2. They use debt to buy (LBO)
3. They run the company for 3-5 years
4. They do debt recaps (take chips off table)
5. They sell to strategic or IPO
6. Make 20%+ annual returns
7. Brand may or may not survive
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Sell to Private Equity?
  │
  ├─ Do I understand the terms?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Need to learn
  │
  ├─ Is the leverage reasonable (2-3x EBITDA)?
  │   ├─ YES → Good
  │   └─ NO → Bad
  │
  ├─ Do I get life-changing money?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Skip
  │
  └─ Will the brand survive?
      ├─ YES → Consider
      └─ NO → Hold
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Understand deal terms
- Keep leverage low (2-3x EBITDA)
- Get life-changing money
- Know PE's real goals

**Never:**
- Take too much debt
- Think PE cares about your brand
- Sell without understanding terms
- Ignore the leverage
