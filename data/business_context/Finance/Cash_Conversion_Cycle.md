# Cash_Conversion_Cycle.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for managing the cash conversion cycle.

## Core Philosophy
**The negative cash conversion cycle is the holy grail.**

- Pay suppliers after customers pay you
- Your money works for you
- Free cash flow = Business growth
- The goal: Negative CCC

## Principles

### 1. What Is the Cash Conversion Cycle?
```
CCC = Days Inventory Outstanding + Days Sales Outstanding - Days Payable Outstanding
```

### 2. The Goal: Negative CCC
- Customers pay you before you pay suppliers
- Your money is working for you
- You're using customer cash to fund inventory
- The longer the negative CCC, the better

### 3. How to Achieve Negative CCC
- Pre-orders (customer pays before you produce)
- Subscription (upfront payment)
- Net terms with suppliers (pay later)
- Fast inventory turns (sell quickly)

### 4. Why It Matters
- Negative CCC = Free capital
- You can grow without raising capital
- PE firms love businesses with negative CCC
- It's the ultimate efficiency metric

## Process

### Cash Conversion Cycle Management:
```
1. Measure your current CCC
2. Identify the biggest lever
3. Negotiate better supplier terms
4. Speed up customer payments
5. Reduce inventory days
6. Target negative CCC
7. Monitor monthly
```

## Decision Framework

```
How to Improve CCC?
  │
  ├─ Speed up customer payments
  │   ├─ Pre-orders
  │   ├─ Subscription (upfront)
  │   └─ Net 15 or Net 30 for customers
  │
  ├─ Slow down supplier payments
  │   ├─ Negotiate Net 30, 60, 90, 120
  │   ├─ Use credit cards (30-day float)
  │   └─ Agency terms (60-120 days)
  │
  └─ Reduce inventory days
      ├─ Smaller POs
      ├─ More frequent orders
      └─ Liquidate aging inventory
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Measure CCC monthly
- Negotiate supplier terms
- Speed up customer payments
- Target negative CCC

**Never:**
- Ignore the cash conversion cycle
- Pay suppliers early without reason
- Hold excess inventory
- Let customers pay on Net 90 while you pay Net 30
