# Net_Terms.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for negotiating net terms with suppliers.

## Core Philosophy
**Net terms are the cheapest form of capital.**

- Your cost of capital is likely 10-20%
- Suppliers may subsidize their cost of capital
- Net terms = Free cash flow
- The goal: Extend terms as long as possible

## Principles

### 1. The Value of Net Terms
```
If you have Net 30 terms:
  │
  ├─ You get 30 days of free use of supplier's money
  ├─ Your cost of capital is 10-20%
  ├─ That's 0.8-1.6% savings per month
  └─ Annualized: 10-19% savings
```

### 2. Negotiation Strategy
- Start with Net 30
- Build trust over time
- Extend to Net 60, Net 90, Net 120
- Offer trade-offs (higher margin, longer commitment)

### 3. The Trade-Off
- Pay more for COGS in exchange for better terms
- The margin increase offsets the cost
- Long-term relationships win

## Process

### Net Terms Negotiation:
```
1. Start with standard terms (Net 30)
2. Deliver on time, every time
3. Build trust over 3-6 months
4. Ask for extended terms
5. Offer trade-offs
6. Gradually extend further
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Accept These Terms?
  │
  ├─ Is the cost of capital higher than the discount?
  │   ├─ YES → Take the terms
  │   └─ NO → Pay early for the discount
  │
  ├─ Can I afford the payment timing?
  │   ├─ YES → Take the terms
  │   └─ NO → Negotiate shorter terms
  │
  └─ Is the supplier reliable?
      ├─ YES → Extend terms
      └─ NO → Keep standard terms
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Negotiate net terms
- Build trust first
- Offer trade-offs
- Extend terms gradually

**Never:**
- Accept standard terms without negotiating
- Ignore the cost of capital
- Pay early without a discount
- Burn supplier relationships
