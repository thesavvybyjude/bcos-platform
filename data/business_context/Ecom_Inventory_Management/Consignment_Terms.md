# Consignment_Terms.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for consignment and supplier terms.

## Core Philosophy
**The best way to buy inventory is with your customer's money.**

- Consignment = Pay when it sells
- Net terms = Pay later
- The ultimate is consignment with good unit economics
- Build trust with suppliers

## Principles

### 1. The Payment Spectrum
```
Drop Shipping (Pay after sale)
  │
  │  ↓
  │
Consignment (Pay when sold)
  │
  │  ↓
  │
Net Terms (Pay after delivery)
  │
  │  ↓
  │
Pre-pay (Pay before delivery)
```

### 2. Consignment Is Ideal
- You hold inventory but pay when it sells
- You get economies of scale (unlike drop shipping)
- You don't carry the cash burden
- Requires trust with supplier

### 3. Net Terms Are Negotiable
- Net 7, Net 30, Net 90, Net 120
- The goal: Pay with customer money
- Longer terms = Better cash flow
- Offer trade-offs (better margin, longer commitment)

### 4. Vendor as Lender
- Your suppliers can be your lenders
- They may have cheaper cost of capital (government subsidies)
- Pay more margin for better terms
- Build long-term relationships for better terms

## Process

### Consignment/Terms Negotiation:
```
1. Build relationship with supplier
2. Prove you can sell their product
3. Ask for better terms
4. Offer trade-offs (margin, commitment)
5. If they say no, build more trust
6. Gradually extend terms
7. Eventually, consignment
```

## Decision Framework

```
What Terms Should I Negotiate?
  │
  ├─ What's my cost of capital?
  │   ├─ High → Need better terms
  │   └─ Low → Terms less critical
  │
  ├─ What's supplier's cost of capital?
  │   ├─ Low → They may extend terms
  │   └─ High → They need faster payment
  │
  ├─ Can I build trust?
  │   ├─ YES → Work toward consignment
  │   └─ NO → Work on relationships
  │
  └─ What can I offer in exchange?
      ├─ Higher margin
      ├─ Longer commitment
      └─ More volume
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Negotiate terms
- Build supplier trust
- Work toward consignment
- Pay with customer money

**Never:**
- Accept standard terms without negotiation
- Think you can't get better terms
- Ignore supplier relationships
- Pay before you need to
