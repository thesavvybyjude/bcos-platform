# Business Education & Curriculum Framework

## Purpose
To provide a comprehensive framework for understanding what a business education consists of, how it is structured, and how to approach learning business effectively.

## Core Philosophy

> "Business study is general, not easy. People think it's easy, but it's not."

## The Curriculum Structure

### Foundations (Before Core)

```
1. QUANTITATIVE METHODS
├── Business Math (standard math used in business)
└── Business Statistics (analyzing and interpreting data, finding relationships)

2. ECONOMICS
├── Microeconomics (individual consumers, firms, profit maximisation)
└── Macroeconomics (GDP, employment, wages, interest rates, exchange rates, trade)
```

### Core (Four Pillars)

```
1. ACCOUNTING — The Language of Business
├── Financial Accounting (external reporting: balance sheet, income statement, cash flows)
└── Managerial Accounting (internal decision-making: costs, budgets, pricing)

2. FINANCE — Managing Money
├── Financial Management (managing assets, liabilities, investments)
├── Investments (evaluating ROI)
└── Financing (debt vs equity balance)

3. MARKETING — The Customer
├── Product (features, design)
├── Price (strategy, competition)
├── Promotion (advertising, communication)
└── Place (distribution)

4. MANAGEMENT — Running the Business
├── Planning (goals, strategies)
├── Organising (departments, roles)
├── Leadership (motivating people)
└── Control (monitoring performance)
```

### Capstone

```
STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT
├── How to compete and innovate
├── Builds on all four cores
└── Integration of accounting, finance, marketing, and management
```

## Learning Methodology

### Good vs Bad Students

```
GOOD STUDENTS                    BAD STUDENTS
─────────────                    ────────────
Study consistently               Cram before exams
Read the textbook                Study from PowerPoints
Try to understand                Try to memorise
Ask questions                    Stay quiet
Do exercises                     Skip exercises
Remember 2 weeks after exam      Forget immediately
Get good jobs                    Struggle to find jobs
```

### The "Toilet Flush" Effect

- Students study hard, pass the exam, and forget everything immediately
- This is why weak foundations are the most common problem in business education
- **Rule:** Don't study to pass — study to understand and remember

### Active Learning Principles

- Ask questions — that's what professors are for
- Be proactive and curious
- University is based on reading books — read the textbook
- Do exercises, especially in accounting (practice makes permanent)
- Study from textbooks, not just PowerPoints

### Career Reality

- There are plenty of business graduates, but jobs only go to the skilled ones
- Good students (6-7 out of 50) get good well-paying jobs
- The rest struggle to find good jobs
- **Rule:** You have to be good because if you're not, someone else will be

## Rules

- **Always study to understand** — not just to pass the exam
- **Always read the textbook** — PowerPoints are summaries, not knowledge
- **Always do exercises** — especially in accounting and finance
- **Always ask questions** — that's how you learn
- **Never cram** — the "toilet flush" means you forget everything
- **Never study from PowerPoints alone** — they lack depth and context
- **Never skip foundations** — weak foundations = weak career
- **Never study for grades alone** — knowledge is the real value

## Heuristics

- If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough
- If you're cramming, you're preparing to forget
- If you're not doing exercises, you're not learning accounting
- If you're studying from PowerPoints, you're getting a summary, not knowledge
- If you don't ask questions, you're not curious enough
- If you're studying because your parents want it, you'll struggle
- If you forget everything after the exam, the toilet flush happened

### Business Education Roadmap

```
YEAR 1: FOUNDATIONS
├── Quantitative Methods (Math + Statistics)
├── Economics (Micro + Macro)
└── Introduction to Business

YEAR 2: CORE (Begin)
├── Accounting I (Financial Accounting)
├── Finance I (Financial Management)
├── Marketing I (Marketing Principles)
└── Management I (Management Principles)

YEAR 3: CORE (Complete)
├── Accounting II (Managerial Accounting)
├── Finance II (Investments)
├── Marketing II (Advertising, Sales)
└── Management II (Leadership, Control)

YEAR 4: CAPSTONE + SPECIALISATION
├── Strategic Management (Capstone)
├── Specialisation courses (3-4 courses)
├── International Business (optional)
└── Electives (programming, languages, law)

INTERNSHIP:
├── Apply learnings in real world
├── Build professional network
└── Test career interests
```
