# Franchise_Operations.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for franchise operations and support.

## Core Philosophy
**Culture is the most important thing to maintain.**

- Franchisees must feel part of something bigger
- GMs (General Managers) are the key
- Personal relationships matter
- Support and guidance keep standards high

## Principles

**1. Regional GMs Are the Bridge**
- One GM per region (US, Europe, Asia, Middle East, LatAm)
- They know every franchisee personally
- They visit their homes, meet their families
- They are the culture carriers

**2. Organic Expansion Creates Loyalty**
- Don't market to find franchisees
- Let franchisees come to you
- They love the brand first
- Stronger commitment, better outcomes

**3. Franchisee Selection Is Crucial**
- Must be willing to follow rules
- Must be entrepreneurial but compliant
- Must be aligned with brand values
- Must have financial capability

## Process

**Franchisee Support Structure:**
```
Founder (Culture bearer)
  |
  |- GM - US Region
  |   |- Direct relationship with US franchisees
  |   |- Personal connections
  |   |- Operational support
  |
  |- GM - Europe Region
  |   |- Same for Europe
  |
  |- GM - Asia Region
  |   |- Same for Asia
  |
  |- GM - Middle East Region
      |- Same for Middle East
```

## Decision Framework

```
How to Support Franchisees?
  |
  |- Do I have regional GMs?
  |   |- YES → Strong support
  |   |- NO → Need to build
  |
  |- Do I have personal relationships?
  |   |- YES → Franchisees feel valued
  |   |- NO → Need more touch
  |
  |- Are franchisees following rules?
  |   |- YES → Healthy operation
  |   |- NO → Need intervention
  |
  |- Do they love the brand?
      |- YES → Strong retention
      |- NO → Need cultural alignment
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Maintain personal relationships
- Build regional GM structure
- Be selective in franchisee selection
- Foster brand love

**Never:**
- Be distant from franchisees
- Let standards slip
- Ignore franchisee feedback
- Let culture dilute
