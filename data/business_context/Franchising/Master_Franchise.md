# Master_Franchise.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for master franchise agreements.

## Core Philosophy
**Master franchisees are partners in growth.**

- They develop specific regions/countries
- They sub-franchise to others
- They split fees and royalties
- They must have local expertise

## Principles

**1. Master Franchise Structure**
```
Corporate (Holdco)
  |
  |- 6% Royalty from sub-franchisees
  |   |- 3% goes to Master Franchisee
  |   |- 3% goes to Corporate
  |
  |- Franchise Fee split
      |- 50% to Master Franchisee
      |- 50% to Corporate
```

**2. Development Plan Is Binding**
- Must open X stores in Y years
- Non-negotiable commitments
- Protects brand growth
- Ensures market penetration

**3. Master Franchisee Must Have Local Expertise**
- Know the market deeply
- Have real estate knowledge
- Understand local regulations
- Have operational capability

## Process

**Master Franchise Selection:**
```
1. Identify target region/market
2. Find potential partners with local expertise
3. Evaluate their capability and track record
4. Negotiate development plan
5. Set binding commitments
6. Execute master franchise agreement
7. Support their development
8. Monitor compliance
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Use Master Franchise?
  |
  |- Do I have local knowledge?
  |   |- YES → Can do direct
  |   |- NO → Need master franchisee
  |
  |- Is there a qualified partner?
  |   |- YES → Consider
  |   |- NO → Need to build
  |
  |- Can they meet development plan?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Need different partner
  |
  |- Is the economics fair?
      |- YES → Proceed
      |- NO → Renegotiate
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Require binding development plans
- Split economics fairly
- Choose partners with local expertise
- Monitor compliance

**Never:**
- Sell rights without development commitments
- Work with partners who don't know the market
- Ignore franchisee quality
- Let sub-franchisees fail
