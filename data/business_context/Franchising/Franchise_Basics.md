# Franchise_Basics.md

## Purpose
To provide a comprehensive framework for understanding franchising as a business model.

## Core Philosophy
**Franchising is using other people's money to expand your brand.**

- You license your brand, knowledge, and products
- Franchisees pay for the right to operate
- Standards must be uniform globally
- Franchisees are entrepreneurs, not employees

## Principles

**1. Franchising Is a Form of Licensing**
- Franchisee pays for the right to use your brand
- Franchise fee (one-time, 5-10 year term)
- Royalties (5-12% of revenue monthly)
- Marketing fund (1-3% for global initiatives)

**2. Uniformity Is Everything**
- Same product quality
- Same store design
- Same customer experience
- Same operational standards

**3. Franchisees Are Business Owners**
- They run their own P&L
- They hire their own staff
- They manage their own operations
- They follow your guidelines

## The Franchise Relationship

**What the Franchisee Gets:**
- Brand rights (name, logo, trademarks)
- Operating system (SOPs, manuals, guidelines)
- Product supply chain
- Training and support
- Marketing (global and local)

**What the Franchisor Gets:**
- Franchise fee (one-time)
- Royalties (recurring revenue)
- Marketing fund contributions
- Brand expansion (using franchisee capital)
- Scale economies

## Decision Tree

```
Should I Franchise My Business?
  |
  |- Is my business profitable?
  |   |- YES → Franchising is viable
  |   |- NO → Need to prove model first
  |
  |- Can my operations be standardized?
  |   |- YES → Franchising works
  |   |- NO → May need corporate model
  |
  |- Do I have the infrastructure to support?
  |   |- YES → Proceed
  |   |- NO → Build infrastructure first
  |
  |- Is the market ready?
      |- YES → Franchise
      |- NO → Need education first
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Maintain uniform standards
- Support franchisees
- Protect brand equity
- Be selective

**Never:**
- Let standards slip
- Sell to unqualified franchisees
- Ignore franchisee feedback
- Think you're "done" after selling
