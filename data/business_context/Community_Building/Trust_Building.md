# Trust Building

## Purpose
To provide a framework for building trust as a business asset.

## Core Philosophy
**Trust is the most valuable thing you can build.**

- Trust is the currency of business
- Trust costs nothing to give and everything to take
- Trust compounds over time
- Without trust, you have nothing

## Principles

### 1. Trust Is Built Over Time
- Trust is earned, not given
- Every interaction is a deposit or withdrawal
- Small deposits compound
- It takes years to build and seconds to destroy

### 2. Trust Is a Competitive Advantage
- Trust reduces friction
- Trust enables higher prices
- Trust creates loyalty
- Trust protects you in tough times

### 3. How to Build Trust
- Keep your promises
- Be transparent
- Admit mistakes
- Serve before you sell

### 4. The Cost of Lost Trust
- Trust destroyed is hard to regain
- A broken trust is a broken relationship
- Once trust is lost, everything is harder
- Protect your trust at all costs

## Decision Framework

```
Is This Building Trust?
  |
  +-- Are we keeping our promises?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Problem
  |
  +-- Are we being transparent?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need more
  |
  +-- Are we admitting mistakes?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Need to
  |
  +-- Are we serving or selling?
      +-- Serving -> Trust
      +-- Selling -> No trust
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Keep promises
- Be transparent
- Admit mistakes
- Serve before selling

### Never:
- Break promises
- Hide information
- Refuse to admit mistakes
- Sell before serving
