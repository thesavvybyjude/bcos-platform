# Long Term Relationships

## Purpose
To provide a framework for building long-term relationships.

## Core Philosophy
**Relationships are the ultimate business asset.**

- The longer the relationship, the more valuable it is
- Repeat customers are more profitable
- Long-term partners are better partners
- Relationships compound over time

## Principles

### 1. Relationships Compound
- Each year of relationship is a year of trust
- Long-term relationships are more valuable
- Compound interest on relationships
- The longer you are together, the better you work together

### 2. Short-Term Thinking Destroys Relationships
- The best deals are made with partners you will work with again
- Transactional thinking is short-term
- Short-term gain is long-term loss
- The goal is a lifetime relationship

### 3. The Contract Is the Start, Not the End
- The contract is just the beginning
- The real value is in the next deal
- Relationships get better with time
- The best partnerships are ongoing

## Decision Framework

```
Is This Relationship Built to Last?
  |
  +-- Am I thinking long-term?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Problem
  |
  +-- Is this the start or the end?
  |   +-- Start -> Good
  |   +-- End -> Missed opportunity
  |
  +-- Are we building trust?
  |   +-- YES -> Good
  |   +-- NO -> Problem
  |
  +-- Will we work together again?
      +-- YES -> Good
      +-- NO -> Transactional
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Think long-term
- Build relationships, not transactions
- Invest in the relationship
- The next deal matters more than this one

### Never:
- Sacrifice the relationship for a short-term gain
- Think transactions are better than relationships
- Underinvest in the relationship
- Forget the next deal
