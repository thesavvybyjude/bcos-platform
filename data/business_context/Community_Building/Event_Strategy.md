# Event_Strategy.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for brand events.

## Core Philosophy
**Events are content engines, not just experiences.**

- 500 people at event = Good
- Content from event = Millions of views = Great
- Reverse-engineer events for digital content
- The content is the real product

## Principles

**1. Fewer Big Events > Many Small Events**
- One big event per quarter
- Big activation vs. small gatherings
- Make it memorable
- Go big or go home

**2. Open to Everyone, Not Just VIPs**
- Don't just invite influencers
- Actual consumers should attend
- Line down the street
- Accessible to the public

**3. Reverse-Engineer for Digital Content**
- If event doesn't produce content, it's useless
- Capture everything
- Use content for years
- B-roll is gold

## Process

**Event Strategy:**
```
1. Plan big activation (quarterly)
2. Open to public
3. Make it experiential
4. Capture high-quality content
5. Deploy content for months/years
6. Return to same city
7. Use previous content in marketing
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Host an Event?
  |
  |- Can I capture high-quality content?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Don't bother
  |
  |- Can I open it to the public?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Reconsider
  |
  |- Can I make it experiential?
  |   |- YES → Do it
  |   |- NO → Not worth it
  |
  |- Can I deploy the content long-term?
      |- YES → Do it
      |- NO → Reconsider
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Go big or go home
- Open to public
- Capture content
- Deploy content forever

**Never:**
- Do small, low-impact events
- Only invite VIPs
- Forget to capture content
- Let content go unused
