# Community_Strategy.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building brand community.

## Core Philosophy
**Community is the difference between a product and a brand.**

- People root for your brand like a sports team
- Community = Long-term loyalty
- Listen to your community
- Give value before asking

## Principles

**1. Build Before You Sell**
- Give free value first
- PDF guides before supplements
- Free content before products
- Community comes before commerce

**2. Listen to Your Community**
- They'll tell you what to make
- Comments on social media
- Survey respondents
- In-person events

**3. Watch Out for Loud Minorities**
- 50 comments ≠ 50,000 customers
- Don't build for the vocal minority
- Validate with real data
- In-person events are better

## Process

**Community Building:**
```
1. Give free value (PDFs, guides, content)
2. Build following (Instagram, TikTok)
3. Listen to feedback
4. Create products they ask for
5. Give them what they want
6. Repeat
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should I Build Community?
  |
  |- Am I giving free value?
  |   |- YES → Good foundation
  |   |- NO → Start giving
  |
  |- Am I listening?
  |   |- YES → Good
  |   |- NO → Start listening
  |
  |- Am I responding?
  |   |- YES → Good
  |   |- NO → Need to engage
  |
  |- Am I giving them what they want?
      |- YES → Strong community
      |- NO → Need to pivot
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Give free value first
- Listen to your community
- Respond to feedback
- Give them what they want

**Never:**
- Build for the loud minority
- Ignore community feedback
- Stop giving value
- Forget who built you
