# White Space

## Purpose
To provide a framework for identifying and exploiting white space.

## Core Philosophy
**The biggest opportunities are where no one else is looking.**

- White space is where the competition is not
- Go to market where others are not
- If everyone is doing it, it is already too late
- The most valuable space is often ignored

## Principles

### 1. What Is White Space?
- White space is the gap between existing offerings
- It is where customer needs are not being met
- It is the market no one is serving
- It is the overlooked opportunity

### 2. Why People Miss White Space
- They look where everyone else is looking
- They copy what is already working
- They do not see the gap
- They ignore the niche

### 3. How to Find White Space
- Look where others are not looking
- What needs are underserved?
- What customers are ignored?
- What problems are solved badly?

### 4. The White Space Advantage
- Low competition
- High margins
- Loyal customers
- First mover in an underserved market

## Decision Framework

```
Is This White Space?
  |
  +-- Is anyone else serving this market?
  |   +-- NO -> White space
  |   +-- YES -> Competition
  |
  +-- Is there a need that is not being met?
  |   +-- YES -> Opportunity
  |   +-- NO -> Not white space
  |
  +-- Are customers underserved?
  |   +-- YES -> White space
  |   +-- NO -> Served market
  |
  +-- Can I be first in this space?
      +-- YES -> Opportunity
      +-- NO -> Need differentiation
```

## Rules

### Always:
- Look where no one else is looking
- Serve the underserved
- Fill the gap
- Be first in the white space

### Never:
- Copy what everyone else is doing
- Ignore the underserved
- Think all opportunities are obvious
- Underestimate white space
