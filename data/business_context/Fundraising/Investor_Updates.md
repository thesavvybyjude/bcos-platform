# Investor_Updates.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for building investor relationships through consistent updates.

## Core Philosophy
**The best investor relationship is built on performance, not coffee.**

- Updates > Meetings
- Transparency builds trust
- Results speak loudest
- Nurture over time

## Principles

**1. Monthly Investor Updates**
- Consistent cadence
- Send to all investors + prospects
- Include metrics
- Share wins and losses

**2. Include Everyone**
- Current investors
- Prospective investors
- Investors who passed
- Friends and family

**3. Be Transparent**
- Share revenue numbers
- Share growth metrics
- Share challenges
- Share learnings

## Process

**Investor Update Structure:**
```
1. Executive summary
2. Revenue numbers
3. Growth metrics
4. Key initiatives
5. Challenges
6. Wins
7. Learnings
8. Ask (if needed)
```

## Example

**Results of Consistent Updates:**
- 300+ investors reading updates
- 75-80% open rate
- Warm introductions when raising
- 6-day Series A close
- Inbound investor interest

## Rules

**Always:**
- Send monthly
- Include metrics
- Be transparent
- Include everyone

**Never:**
- Skip updates
- Hide negative news
- Exclude investors who passed
- Be boring
