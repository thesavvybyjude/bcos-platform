# Product_Development.md

## Purpose
To provide a framework for supplement product development.

## Core Philosophy
**Products should be built with science first, not marketing first.**

- The smartest people about health don't live in supplement companies
- Great formulators live in supplement companies
- Formulators aren't the ones saying what needs to happen
- Start with advisory board, then formulate

## Principles

### 1. The Product Development Process
```
1. Advisory Board identifies gaps
2. Look at market trends and opportunity
3. Identify what's needed
4. Research the science
5. Determine ingredient list
6. Evaluate sourcing
7. Understand pricing
8. Launch or don't
```

### 2. Less Is More
- The more ingredients, the more cross-reactivity
- Harder to have efficacious doses
- Pick what matters, not everything
- If you need 17g of an ingredient to be effective, pull it

### 3. Quality Takes Time
- Pre-workout has been 4+ years in development
- Creativity took 18 months
- You could launch in 6 weeks, but it wouldn't meet standards
- Good things take time

### 4. Taste Is a Challenge
- You can make almost anything taste good with artificial flavors
- If you won't use artificial flavors, it's harder
- Sometimes you can't launch a product because you can't make it taste good
- That's okay

## Process

### Product Development Process:
```
1. Advisory board identifies gap
2. Research the science
3. Determine ingredient list
4. Is it practical? (dosing, size, cost)
5. Evaluate sourcing
6. Is it certified?
7. Does it taste good (without artificial flavors)?
8. If yes → Launch
9. If no → Don't launch (or keep working)
```

## Decision Framework

```
Should We Launch This Product?
  │
  ├─ Is there a scientific gap?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Can we get efficacious doses?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Can we source high quality?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Can we certify it (NSF)?
  │   ├─ YES → Consider
  │   └─ NO → Don't
  │
  ├─ Can it taste good (without artificial)?
  │   ├─ YES → Launch
  │   └─ NO → Keep working or don't
  │
  └─ Is it practical (pricing, dosing)?
      ├─ YES → Launch
      └─ NO → Don't
```

## Rules

**Always:**
- Start with advisory board
- Use science to guide decisions
- Source high quality
- Certify everything

**Never:**
- Launch without science
- Use artificial flavors (if your standards forbid them)
- Compromise on sourcing
- Launch before it's ready
