# Innovation.md

## Purpose
To provide frameworks for product innovation and speed to market.

## Core Philosophy
**Speed is a competitive advantage.**

- Ship fast
- Iterate quickly
- Learn from users
- Move before competitors

## Principles

**1. Move Fast**
- Launch early
- Iterate quickly
- Don't overcomplicate
- Speed matters

**2. Listen to Users**
- Build what they need
- Solve real problems
- User feedback is fuel
- Features must deliver value

**3. Innovate Constantly**
- Never stop shipping
- Always improve
- Watch competitors
- Stay ahead

### 4. The Product Development Process
- Start with advisory board, not marketing
- Less is more (ingredients)
- Quality takes time (4+ years for pre-workout)
- Don't use artificial flavors if you can't make it taste good without them

### 5. Science First
- The smartest people about health don't live in supplement companies
- Great formulators live in supplement companies
- Formulators aren't the ones saying what needs to happen
- Start with advisory board, then formulate

### 6. The Product Launch Framework
- Product launches are lottery tickets
- Some products work, some don't
- It's totally random
- Give yourself room to fail
- Never bet the farm
- Test on email, organic, paid
- Launch costs under $500K

## Rules

**Always:**
- Ship early and often
- Listen to users
- Move faster than competition
- Focus on quality
- Start with science
- Use the advisory board
- Treat launches as lottery tickets
- Keep launch costs low

**Never:**
- Wait for perfection
- Ignore user feedback
- Slow down
- Stop innovating
- Launch without science
- Use artificial flavors if your standards forbid them
- Bet the farm on one product
- Keep failing products alive
